Your signature is a closing with your name and e.g. office, phone number or logo, as at the bottom of an email. You set it up once in your account and add it to the end of a message with one tap when writing on the {bereich.board}.

![The Signature card in the account with toolbar](bild:signatur)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom, choose **Account** in the menu at the top and scroll to the **Signature** card. If you are writing a message and don’t have a signature yet, **No signature yet – set one up now** brings you straight here.

## What you see here
- **Set up signature**: if you don’t have one yet.
- **Preview**: what your signature looks like, with **Edit** and **Delete**.
- When setting up and editing: the field with the same toolbar as when writing on the {bereich.board} (font, colours, alignment, lists, links, pictures, tables), plus **Save** and **Cancel**.
- **‹ Back to the message**: if you came from writing on the {bereich.board}.

## Step by step
### Set up a signature
1. Tap **Set up signature**.
2. Write your closing, e.g. “Best wishes” and below it name and office.
3. Add a picture if you like, e.g. your logo: tap **🖼** and choose the picture. You change its size at the corners. Picture and text side by side work with a table (**▦**).
4. Tap **Save**. Below it you see the preview.

![The preview of a signature that has been set up](bild:signatur-vorschau)

### Edit or delete the signature
1. Tap **Edit** below the preview, change it and tap **Save**.
2. To delete it, tap **Delete** and confirm.

## Good to know
- When added, the signature is copied into the message. If you change or delete it here later, messages already written stay as they are.
- Nobody but you sees your signature until you add it to a message.
- You can add as many pictures as you like. The server may reject very large pictures; the app then tells you the maximum size.
- If your account is deleted, your signature is deleted too. Signatures added to messages remain as part of the text.
