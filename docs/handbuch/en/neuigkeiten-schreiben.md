Here you write posts for the {bereich.news} directly in the app, from blocks with formatted text, pictures and ready-made blocks such as a **Countdown**, and change or delete them later. This works if an {rolle.admin} has chosen **Write in the app** as the source of the {bereich.news} (Settings → News). If your app runs as a WordPress plugin with the source **Posts of this WordPress site**, you write the posts in the WordPress editor; the app opens it for you (see “With WordPress: write the website’s posts” below).

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.news}** at the bottom. The **New post** button is at the top. To change a post, open it; below the text are **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (bin).

## What you see here
- **New post** (above the list): opens the form for a new post.
- **Title**: the heading of the post, at most 150 characters.
- **Cover image (optional)**: a picture shown above the post in the list of {bereich.news} and large at the top of the post. **Choose image** uploads it, **Replace image** swaps it, **Remove image** takes it out. The preview shows the crop as in the list (landscape 16:9). Without a cover image, the list shows the first picture in a text for everyone.
- **Content**: the post made of blocks, first a text block with formatting, links and pictures. **+ Insert block** adds more blocks, for example a **Countdown**.
- **Publish** or **Save** and **Cancel**.
- In the opened post: **Last changed** with date and name, below it **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (bin).

## Step by step
### Write a post
1. Under **{bereich.news}**, tap **New post**.
2. Enter a **Title**, choose a **Cover image** if needed and write the text under **Content**. You add pictures with the picture icon in the text. Add more blocks with **+ Insert block** (at the end) or **+ Insert block below**, move them with **↑** and **↓** and take them out with **Remove**.
3. Tap **Publish**. The post is at the top of the list straight away, for everyone allowed to read the {bereich.news}, even without logging in.

### Insert more blocks
- **Button with link**: enter a **Label** and a **Target**: a website with https://, an email address with mailto:, a phone number with tel: or a page of the app such as #/termine. Under **Style** choose **Highlighted** or **Subtle**.
- **Upcoming events**: choose a **Heading** and **How many events** (1 to 20). The block always shows the next events from today from {bereich.events}.
- **Contacts**: under **Offices**, tick the offices whose holders should appear. Email address and mobile number are only seen by people allowed to see personal details.
- **Notice box**: a short **Note**, coloured as **Info**, **Warning** or **Success**.
- **Divider**: a **Line** or **Space only** between two blocks.
- **Schedule**: one **Time** and one **Programme item** per row, e.g. for the schedule of a festival.
- **Facts and figures**: one **Value** and one **Name** per row, large side by side.
- **Poll**: under **Poll**, choose one of the {bereich.polls}. The block shows title and status with **Take part**; those who have already voted see **View answer and result**. Those who may not take part in polls don’t see the block.
- **Album**: under **Album**, choose one from the {bereich.gallery} and **How many photos** (1 to 12). Everyone only sees the pictures they may also see in the {bereich.gallery}.
- **Document**: under **Document**, choose a file from the {bereich.documents}. The block shows it with **Open** (PDF and images) and **Download**, but only for those who may see it in the {bereich.documents}.
- **{bereich.sponsors}**: shows all {bereich.sponsors} with logo and link, optionally with a **Heading**.
- **Map for directions**: enter a **Place or address**. The block shows the place with the **Map** button, which opens it in the device’s maps app, as for an event’s location.

### Insert a countdown
1. Tap **+ Insert block** and choose **Countdown** in the window.
2. Enter a **Heading** if needed and, under **Counts down to**, choose a **Date** (with **Time**) or an **Event** from the {bereich.events}. A moved event is followed automatically.
3. Optional: an end (**Ends on**, **Ends at**) with a **Text while it is running**, and under **Afterwards** a **Text afterwards** or **Hide block**.
4. The **Preview** shows what the countdown looks like right now. It always shows two values: days and hours, on the last day hours and minutes, in the last hour minutes and seconds. It counts by the calendar in your app’s time zone: if a clock change falls in between, it shows the remaining time as on the clock, e.g. from 9:47 pm to midnight always 2 hours 13 minutes.

### Change a post
1. Open the post and tap **Edit**.
2. Change the title or blocks and tap **Save**. The date stays that of publication.

### Write a post in English
If English is switched on in the app, **Write in: Deutsch | English** appears at the top when editing.
1. Publish the post in German as usual.
2. Open it, tap **Edit** and choose **English** at the top.
3. Enter the English title and the texts of the blocks. Empty fields show the German text in grey.
4. Tap **Save**.

You change the cover image, blocks and their settings in German; they apply to both languages. Those using the app in English see the English version. Anything missing appears in German with the note *Only available in German*.

### Delete a post
1. Open the post and tap **Delete**.
2. Confirm. The post and its pictures are then gone for good.

### With WordPress: write the website’s posts
With the source **Posts of this WordPress site**, the {bereich.news} are posts on your website. You then write them in the WordPress editor, with everything it can do (blocks, pictures from the media library, categories, drafts, scheduled publishing).
1. Under **{bereich.news}**, tap **New post**. The WordPress editor opens in a separate window.
2. Write the post and tap **Publish** there. A draft or a scheduled post only appears in the app once it is published.
3. Switch back to the app. It reloads the list, and the post is at the top.
4. To change it, open the post in the app and tap **Edit in WordPress** (pencil) below the text. You can delete it in the WordPress editor (**Move to Trash**).

## Good to know
- Those who subscribed to the {bereich.news} with notifications get a notification for a new post, not for changes. It arrives in the person’s language; it only contains the English title if it has been entered when it is sent.
- Posts coming from WordPress or from a feed only have their one language.
- If someone saved the post while you were editing it, the app tells you when you save. Reload the post and enter your changes again.
- Pictures no longer in any block are deleted by the app when saving, as is a replaced or removed cover image.
- For each block you choose **For everyone who sees the page** or **Members only**. Members-only blocks and their pictures are missing without logging in and for guests.
- If an {rolle.admin} switches the source back to a website or a feed, the posts disappear from the app but stay stored. With **Write in the app** they are back.
- The right for this is called **Write {bereich.news}**. With the source **Write in the app**, and with WordPress also with **Posts of this WordPress site**, the app creates the role “Manage {bereich.news}”; an {rolle.admin} assigns it.
- With WordPress the right gives you the permissions for all posts in the WordPress editor: write, change, publish, delete and upload pictures to the media library. Pages of the website, new categories and the other WordPress settings are not included.
- If you are not logged in in the editor window (this can happen with an app installed on an iPhone), log in there with your account.
