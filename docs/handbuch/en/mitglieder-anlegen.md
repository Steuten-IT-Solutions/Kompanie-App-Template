titel: Create member

Here you create a new account for a person. The app generates a starting password that you hand to the person in person. If the person doesn’t log in (yet), you create them **without access**, e.g. so that they can be chosen in chronicles and under {bereich.offices}.

![“Create member” form](bild:mitglieder-anlegen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Members** and above the list **+ Create member**.

## What you see here
- **‹ All members**: back to the list.
- **Create several at once**: a list for several people (see [Create several members](seite:mitglieder-mehrere-anlegen)).
- **Import from file (Excel or CSV)**: take over an existing member list (see [Import members](seite:mitglieder-import)).
- **First name** and **Surname**.
- **No access (doesn’t log in yet)**: for people without an account (see below). The username field is then not shown.
- **Username (optional)**, **Email (optional)**, **Mobile number (optional)** and **Address (optional)** with country, street, no., address line 2, postcode and town.
- **Role**: the roles you may assign. If you may **assign roles**, these are all roles you could also assign afterwards under **Account and role** (e.g. board); otherwise {rolle.member} and the roles an {rolle.admin} has released for new accounts (e.g. candidate). If there is only {rolle.member}, the choice is not shown. In the standalone version {rolle.admin} accounts can also create {rolle.admin} accounts; the app asks for confirmation.
- **Additional roles (optional)**: only if you may assign roles and the chosen role belongs to the company. Tick the additional roles the person should get straight away.
- **{bereich.points} (starting balance, optional)** with **As of** and **{begriff.rank} (optional)**: only for {rolle.member} and only if you may also manage {bereich.points} or {bereich.ranks}. Meant for taking over existing members.
- **Birthday (optional)** with day, month and year: only if the company uses {bereich.birthdays}.
- **Photo (optional)** with **Choose photo**.
- **Create member**: creates the account.

## Step by step
### Create a member
1. Enter the **First name** and **Surname**.
2. If you like, enter the username, email, mobile number, address, birthday and the other details, and choose a photo with **Choose photo**.
3. Tap **Create member**.
4. The app shows the login details: the link for installing the app, username and password. Tap **Print** (one slip per person), **Copy login details** or **Share …** and hand them over in person.
5. With **Another member** you create the next one straight away; **‹ Back to the member list** takes you back.

![Login details after creating](bild:mitglieder-anlegen-zugangsdaten)

### Create a member without access
This is how you add a person who doesn’t have an account yet, e.g. because they appear in a chronicle, hold an office or don’t use the app.
1. Enter the **First name** and **Surname**.
2. Tick **No access (doesn’t log in yet)**.
3. Enter the other details if you have them and tap **Create member**. There is no starting password.
4. The person is a normal member: they are in the member list and can be chosen everywhere, e.g. under {bereich.offices}. In the admin member list they carry the label **without access**.
5. Later they get access in one of two ways:
   - If they request access themselves, you link the request to them when approving it (see [Requests for access](seite:anfragen-freigeben)). Then no second account is created.
   - Or you tap **Set up access** under **Account and role** on their page and hand them the login details (see [Account and role](seite:rollen-vergeben)).

## Good to know
- Only the first name is required; without a surname it works e.g. for an account like “Treasurer”.
- Without a username the app forms one from the name. A username may only contain letters (a–z), digits and . _ - @. Username and email must not already be in use.
- The app checks the mobile number and address as under **My area → Account**, e.g. whether the postcode matches the country. If something is invalid, the account is not created.
- You enter personal details (email address, mobile number, address, birthday) when creating the account; afterwards only people with the permission **See personal details** see them on the member’s page, and people with **Maintain personal details** may change them. You still see the email address under **Account and role**. If you tap the address there, the maps app shows the place: on Android you choose the app (e.g. Google Maps or Waze), on iPhone and Mac Apple Maps opens, on a PC Google Maps.
- The starting password is shown **only this one time**. The app does not send an email. **Share …** only exists if your device supports it.
- Role and additional roles can be changed afterwards under **Account and role** on the member’s page by everyone who may assign roles.
- If **Request access** is switched on, people can also request their access themselves; you then only approve it (see [Requests for access](seite:anfragen-freigeben)).
- A member without access gets no notifications on their phone. They only get emails to all members if an email address is entered. A person who has already left is better entered as a former member (see **Former members** in the handbook, with the permission **Manage former members**).
- How the new person gets the app onto their phone and logs in is described under [Get the app on your phone](seite:app-installieren) and [My account](seite:anmelden-und-konto).
- Please only upload a photo with the person’s consent. JPG, PNG and WebP are allowed.
