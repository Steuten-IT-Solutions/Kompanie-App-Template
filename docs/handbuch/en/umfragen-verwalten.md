Here you create {bereich.polls} for all {rolle.member} accounts, change them, end them early or delete them. You also see the results of all {bereich.polls}, even while they are running.

![The Manage {bereich.polls} page with the New poll form](bild:umfragen-verwalten)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **{bereich.polls}**. The **Manage {bereich.polls}** button on the {bereich.board} and in the list of all {bereich.polls} also leads here.

## What you see here
- **‹ Admin**: back to the admin start page (only if you have several tasks there).
- **New poll**: the form for creating one.
- **All {bereich.polls}**: all {bereich.polls}, running and ended, newest first. Each shows question, type, end, **Named** or **Anonymous**, who sees the results, and the results. Below them **Edit** (pencil) and **End** (both only while it is running) and **Delete** (bin).

## Step by step
### Create a poll
1. Enter the question under **Question** (at most 150 characters). If needed, write a **Description (optional)** below it.
2. Under **Type**, choose **One answer**, **Multiple answers allowed** or **Free text**.
3. With **One answer** and **Multiple answers allowed**: enter at least two different options under **Answer options**. **+ Option** adds another, **×** removes one.
4. Under **Answers**, choose **Named** or **Anonymous**.
5. Under **Who can see the results?**, choose **Everyone, once they have answered**, **Everyone after the end** or **Only those allowed to see all results**.
6. If needed, enter an **End (optional)** with date and time. Without an end the poll runs until someone ends it.
7. Tap **Publish** and confirm.

![A new poll with three answer options in the form](bild:umfragen-verwalten-neu)

The poll then appears straight away on the {bereich.board} and in the banner of the {rolle.member} accounts.

### Change a poll
1. Tap **Edit** next to the poll.
2. Change the fields. You can always change question, description and end. Once there are answers, type, answer options, anonymity and visibility of the results are fixed.
3. Tap **Save**. **Cancel** leaves everything as it was.

![A running poll with results and the Edit, End and Delete buttons](bild:umfragen-verwalten-umfrage)

### Ask a poll in English too
If the admin has switched on English under **Settings → Languages**, an admin enters the question, description and answer options in English there, in the **Translations** section in the **Running polls** group. The form only shows a note with a link to it. Polls that have ended drop out of the group.

Those using the app in English see the poll in this version, including in notification and reminder. Anything left empty appears in German, with the note *Partly only available in German*. The answers count for all languages together: whoever chooses “Yes” in English has chosen the same option as with “Ja” in German. The English version can also be changed when there are already answers. If you change the German options before the first answer, the English version stays with its option; new options appear in German in English at first.

### End a poll early
1. Tap **End** next to the poll.
2. Confirm. Afterwards nobody can answer or change their answer any more. It can’t be reopened.

### Delete a poll
1. Tap **Delete** next to the poll.
2. Confirm. The poll disappears with all answers, even if it has already ended.

## Good to know
- When published, all {rolle.member} accounts except you get a notification if they have switched on the type **{bereich.polls}: new poll and reminder** on their device.
- 24 hours before the end, the app reminds everyone who hasn’t answered yet, once. If the poll ends within 24 hours of publishing, there is no reminder. If you move the end to later than 24 hours, there is a reminder again.
- Changing sends no new notification. Anyone who had hidden the poll on their device sees it in the banner again afterwards.
- With **Anonymous**, nobody sees who answered what, not even you. With **Named**, the names appear with the results.
- Those who may manage {bereich.polls} or have the permission **{bereich.polls}: see all results** always see the results, even with **Only those allowed to see all results** and while the poll is running.
- Everyone with this permission may change, end and delete, not only whoever created the poll.
- Limits: description at most 1000 characters, 2 to 20 answer options of at most 100 characters each, no duplicates. The end must be in the future.
- Only {rolle.member} accounts take part in {bereich.polls}, not e.g. candidates.
