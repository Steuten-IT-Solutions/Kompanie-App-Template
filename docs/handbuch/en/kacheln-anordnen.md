---
titel: Arrange tiles
---
On pages with buttons (tiles), you decide yourself where each tile sits and how wide it is: in **Admin**, under **Setup**, in every [submenu](seite:untermenue) such as **Internal**, and in **Settings**. The arrangement only applies to you; everyone else keeps seeing their own or the default.

## How to open this page
Open one of these pages and tap the **Arrange tiles** icon (a grid with one wide tile) at the bottom right below the tiles.

## What you see here
- Above the tiles, a note with **Reset** and **Done**.
- Each tile has a dashed border and **−** and **+** for its width at the bottom right.
- Free cells show as grey areas, always with one free row below.

## Step by step
### Move a tile
1. Tap **Arrange tiles**.
2. On a phone: press and hold the tile until it lifts, then drag it to the new place. With a mouse, just drag it.
3. Let go. If there is already a tile there, it moves down one row together with everything below it.
4. Tap **Done**. The **Back** button also ends arranging.

### Change the width
1. Tap **+** (wider) or **−** (narrower) on the tile. A tile is one to all columns wide.
2. If it no longer fits up to the right edge, it moves to the left; tiles in the way move down.

### With the keyboard
Select a tile with the Tab key. The arrow keys move it by one cell, **+** and **−** change its width.

### Reset
Tap **Reset** and confirm. The default then applies to this page again, for all screen widths.

## Good to know
- Every change is saved straight away, in your account. You see the same arrangement on your other devices.
- Depending on the width, the grid has 2 to 5 columns: 2 on a phone, 3 or 4 on a tablet and on a phone held sideways, 5 on a computer (from a window width of about 900 px; pages with tiles are wider there than other pages). Each number of columns has its own arrangement. If you haven’t arranged a width yet, it takes over the order of another one.
- Without a connection the change applies immediately and is noted on the device; the app saves it as soon as there is a network again.
- Without logging in, only this device remembers the arrangement. After logging in, your account’s arrangement applies.
- If a new tile is added (e.g. through a new permission), it goes at the bottom. If a tile disappears, the narrowest tile in the same row gets wider by its width (the left one if they are equally wide); the row stays as wide as before.
- The default for everyone who hasn’t arranged anything is set by an {rolle.admin}: the order or a grid with positions and widths.
- In the **View the app as …** preview, the default always applies.
