titel: Connect an AI assistant
---
You can connect an AI assistant such as Claude to the app if an {rolle.admin} has switched this on. The AI assistant can then, for example, look up events for you or tell you who has accepted. It acts with your permissions: it only sees and changes what you may see and change in the app.

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom and choose **Account** in the menu at the top. The **AI assistants** card only appears if the access is switched on or you have already connected an AI assistant.

## How to proceed
### Connect
1. In the **AI assistants** card, tap **Copy address**.
2. Enter the address in the AI assistant; in Claude as a custom connector.
3. The AI assistant opens the app. Sign in if asked.
4. On the **Connect AI assistant** page you see which program wants access. Choose **Read only** or **Read and change**.
5. Tap **Allow**. You are then taken back to the AI assistant.

Only connect if you just started the connection yourself. Otherwise tap **Decline**.

### Remove an access
1. In the **AI assistants** card, tap the remove icon next to the access.
2. Confirm.

## Good to know
- What the AI assistant changes appears in the change log with your name and the name of the program.
- An access that is not used for 30 days ends. When you change your password, all your accesses end.
