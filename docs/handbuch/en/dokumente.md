Under **{bereich.documents}** you find files of {kompanie.name} such as statutes, minutes or forms. Here you look for a document, open it or save it to your device.

![The list of {bereich.documents}, sorted by category](bild:dokumente)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** at the bottom and then **{bereich.documents}**; **‹ Internal** takes you back. If your role doesn’t have the **Internal** permission, tap **{bereich.documents}** at the bottom directly.

## What you see here
- **Sorting**: sets the order of the {bereich.documents} within a category.
- **Categories**: one row each with the name and, in brackets, the number of {bereich.documents}, e.g. “Minutes (2)”. Anything without a category comes last under **More {bereich.documents}**. If there are no categories at all, all {bereich.documents} are in one list.
- **Document**: title, **Version date** (the date of the document), the type of file (e.g. PDF, image, Word) and its size.
- **Also available offline**: shown for {bereich.documents} already stored on your device.
- **Restricted**: shown for {bereich.documents} only visible to those who maintain {bereich.documents} or have the permission **See restricted {bereich.documents}**. Other {rolle.member} accounts don’t see them at all.
- **Manage {bereich.documents}**: only if you may maintain {bereich.documents}. There you upload and change {bereich.documents}.

## Step by step
### Find a document
1. Under **Sorting**, choose how the {bereich.documents} should be ordered: **Date new → old** (preselected), **Date old → new**, **Name A–Z** or **Name Z–A**.
2. Tap the name of a category. It opens and shows its {bereich.documents}. A second tap closes it again.

![An opened category with the Open and Download buttons](bild:dokumente-kategorie)

### Open or download a document
1. Tap **Open** next to the document. The app shows PDFs and images directly (see [View a document](seite:dokument-ansehen)).
2. Or tap **Download** to save the file on your device. On an iPhone the share menu opens for this; there you choose e.g. “Save to Files”.

Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OpenDocument files only have the **Download** button.

## Good to know
- When the page opens, all categories are closed. When you come back from an open document, its category is open.
- Your device remembers the chosen sorting. It also applies on the **Manage {bereich.documents}** page.
- Anything you have opened or downloaded once can be opened without a connection too. If there is a new version, the app loads it the next time you open it.
- When you log out, the app deletes the stored {bereich.documents} from your device.
- There is no notification for new {bereich.documents}.
