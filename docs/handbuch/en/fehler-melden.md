If something in the app isn’t working, report it here, even without logging in. The report goes to the app’s admins first. They can often help straight away. If it is a bug in the app, they forward the report to the app’s maintainers (Steuten IT Solutions).

![“Report a bug” form with a description filled in](bild:fehler-melden)

## How to open this page

Tap **?** at the top, ideally on the screen where the bug happens. Then tap **Report a bug** at the bottom of the help page. You’ll also find the button at the bottom of the help index.

## What you see here

- **What isn’t working? (required)**: your description, no more than 2000 characters.
- **What did you expect? (optional)**: what should actually have happened.
- **Screenshot (optional)**: a picture of the bug, as PNG, JPEG or WebP, no more than 2 MB.
- **Include my name when forwarding to the maintainers** (only when logged in): without the tick, your name isn’t passed on to the maintainers. The admins see it anyway.
- **Included automatically**: everything that is sent as well.
- **Consent**: that the report goes to the admins with these details and that they may forward it to the maintainers.
- **Send report**: sends the report.
- **Your reports** (only when logged in): your earlier reports with their status.

## Step by step

### Report a bug

1. Under **What isn’t working? (required)**, briefly describe what happened.
2. If you like, write what you expected under **What did you expect? (optional)**.
3. Attach a **Screenshot** if needed. **Remove** takes it away again.
4. If you are logged in, tick **Include my name when forwarding to the maintainers** if the maintainers should also know who the report is from.
5. Check under **Included automatically** what is sent as well, and tick the consent below it.
6. Tap **Send report**.

![Details under “Included automatically”](bild:fehler-melden-senden)

Once the report is saved, “Thank you, your report has arrived” appears. **To help** takes you back.

### See what happens to your report

If you are logged in, your report is listed under **Your reports**:

- **open**: the admins haven’t dealt with it yet.
- **closed**: the admins have dealt with it. Below it is their **Reply from the admins**, e.g. how to get on.
- **forwarded to the maintainers**: the app’s maintainers are looking after it.

If you have switched on notifications, you get one when your report is closed or forwarded.

## What is sent along

Exactly what is listed under **Included automatically**: the page in the app where you tapped **?**, the app version, device and browser, your role (or “not logged in”), the time and the app’s last error message, if there was one. Plus your description and the screenshot. If you are logged in, the admins see your name. It only goes to the maintainers if you choose so.

> Don’t write anything confidential in the report, e.g. no passwords. If the admins forward the report, the maintainers store it in a private GitHub repository.

## Good to know

- Without logging in, you won’t get a reply to the report.
- If no recipient for bug reports has been set up in this app, reports stay with the admins.
- Reports that have been dealt with are deleted after 30 days.
- Anyone who sends a lot of reports in quick succession has to wait a while.
