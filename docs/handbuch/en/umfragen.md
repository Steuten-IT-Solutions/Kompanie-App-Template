With **{bereich.polls}** {kompanie.name} asks all {rolle.member} accounts for their opinion, e.g. “Barbecue on Saturday or Sunday?”. This page lists all {bereich.polls}: the running and the closed ones.

![The list of all {bereich.polls}, running ones at the top, closed ones below](bild:umfragen)

## How to open this page
Open the {bereich.board} (tap **Open board** in the banner at the top) and, under **Running {bereich.polls}**, tap **Closed {bereich.polls}**. From an open poll, **All {bereich.polls}** brings you here.

## What you see here
- **Open**: all {bereich.polls} you can still answer. If there are none, it says **There is no poll running at the moment.**
- **Closed {bereich.polls}**: all {bereich.polls} that have ended.
- For each poll: the question, **Open until …** (or **Open until it is ended**) or **Ended …**, plus **open**, **answered** or **not taken part**. If you may already see the results, the number of participants is shown too.
- **Manage {bereich.polls}**: only if you may manage {bereich.polls}.

## Step by step
### Open a poll
1. Tap the poll.
2. The poll’s page opens. There you answer or see your answer and the results (see [Answer a poll](seite:umfrage-beantworten)).

## Good to know
- With the permission **{bereich.polls}: see all results** you see the results of every poll, even while it is running and with **Only those allowed to see all results**. It does not let you create, change or end polls.
- Every poll has exactly one question: with one answer, with multiple answers or with free text.
- Running {bereich.polls} are also on the {bereich.board}. As long as you haven’t answered, a poll also appears in the banner at the top of every page, with the **Take part** button.
- For a new poll and 24 hours before it ends (only if you haven’t answered yet) you get a notification if you have switched on the type **{bereich.polls}: new poll and reminder** for your device. Tapping it opens the poll.
- Only {rolle.member} accounts take part in {bereich.polls}, not e.g. candidates.
