A member’s page shows everything you maintain about this person as an admin. To change something, just tap the detail; a window then opens.

![A member’s page in the admin area](bild:mitglied-uebersicht)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Members**, and tap the person in the list.

## What you see here
You only see what you have the permission for and what the company uses.
- **‹ All members**: back to the list, at the same place.
- **‹** and **›** at the top right with the position (e.g. “3 / 25”): to the previous or next member.
- **Name** and below it the **Badge** with the {begriff.rank}. Those who maintain {bereich.ranks} tap it to promote or demote; those who may only **See promotions** see the history this way; without a {begriff.rank} there is a circle with “+” and **no {begriff.rank}**.
- **Photo** next to it: a tap shows it large and in full. Without a photo, the initials are shown.
- **Role**: the person’s role (with additional roles “Roles: …”), only if you may **See accounts**. If they have exceptions in their permissions, “with permission exceptions” is added. Without this permission it only says “Former member” or “No access” if that applies.
- **{bereich.points}** and **Open** tiles: the {bereich.points} total and open {bereich.penalties}. A tap opens the person’s entries. You see the tiles with the permission to see {bereich.points} or {bereich.penalties} of all members.
- **{bereich.titles}** tile: their {bereich.titles} (or “None”); a tap opens the selection (with the permission **See {bereich.titles}** only the list).
- **{bereich.companies}** tile (with the permission **See assignment to {bereich.companies}**): their {bereich.companies} (or “None”). With **Maintain {bereich.companies}** a tap opens the selection to tick, otherwise only the list.
- **Birthday**: only with the permission **See personal details**. **Joining date**: for everyone who sees accounts, changed only by those who may create members; **Leaving date** (and for former members also their joining date): for everyone who may manage former members. “Not entered” means: still missing.
- **Personal details**: the person’s email address, mobile number and address, only with the permission **See personal details**. A tap on the email address or mobile number writes or calls.
- **{bereich.attendance}** (with the permission **See {bereich.attendance}**): how often the person was **present**, how often **excused** and how often **unexcused** absent. On the right you choose the year or **Total**; the current year is preset. A tap on the numbers opens the events behind them.
- **{bereich.offices}**: the person’s offices as a timeline, for viewing only.
- **Account and role** at the very bottom: opens when tapped (see [Account and role](seite:rollen-vergeben)).

## Step by step
### Go to the next member
1. Swipe left or right on the page. The page follows your finger, and you already see the next person next to it.
2. Let go: the next person slides over. If you only drag a little, everything stays as it was.
3. On a computer, tap **‹** or **›** at the top right instead.
4. After the last member comes the first again, and from the first you go back to the last. Swiping also works while a page is still loading.

### Change the photo
Everyone who may create members may do this, for former members also everyone who may manage former members.
1. Tap the photo (or the initials).
2. Tap **Replace photo** or **Remove photo**; without a photo, **Upload photo**.
3. Choose a photo. It applies immediately.

### Change personal details
Everyone with the permission **Maintain personal details** may do this.
1. In the **Personal details** card, tap **Edit**.
2. Change **Email**, **Mobile number** or the **Address** (country, street, no., address line 2, postcode, town). An empty field removes the detail; if all address fields are empty, no address is stored.
3. Tap **Save**. If something is wrong, e.g. a postcode that doesn’t match the country, it is shown directly below the field.

### Enter the joining date
1. Tap **Joining date**. The **Joining date** window opens.
2. Under **Date or year**, enter the date as DD.MM.YYYY or just the year.
3. Tap **Save joining date**. **Remove** deletes it after confirmation.

![Joining date window](bild:mitglied-uebersicht-eintritt)

### View a person’s {bereich.attendance}
1. On the right of **{bereich.attendance}**, choose the year or **Total**.
2. Read the three numbers: **present**, **excused** and **unexcused**.
3. Tap the numbers. A window opens with all events of the period, most recent first, each with its entry.

![Attendance on a member’s page](bild:mitglied-uebersicht-anwesenheit)

### Close a window
Tap **✕**, next to the window or the device’s back key.

## Good to know
- You browse in the order in which the list is currently sorted and filtered. Swiping doesn’t browse in an open window. If you don’t open the page via the list (e.g. via a link), you browse the whole list without search and filters.
- The app gradually preloads all people in the list, the direct neighbours first. If you swipe faster, a page not yet loaded already shows the name, and you can keep swiping.
- Photos are allowed as JPG, PNG or WebP. The app shows them round and crops them for this; a portrait with the head centred in the upper part of the picture works best. Please only upload a photo with the person’s consent.
- If the company uses the public **{bereich.directory}** list, the photos of {rolle.member} accounts and candidates are visible there to anyone, even without logging in. The person sees their photo under **My area** but doesn’t change it.
- The email address of admins and of people with more permissions than you can only be changed by those who may manage their account: the password can be reset via the email address. You maintain mobile number and address for everyone with the right.
- Everyone sees and changes their own details under **My area → Account**.
- The joining date can be from the year 1800 and not in the future. Whether it is shown on the person’s page under **{bereich.directory}** is chosen only by them under **My area → Account**; in the window you only see their choice for information.
- For {bereich.attendance}, only what is saved in an attendance list counts. If the person declined an upcoming event, the list only suggests “excused”; it only counts once someone saves the list. Saved excuses for upcoming events count, and the row below says how many there are. Events with nothing entered for the person don’t count.
- How to enter birthday and leaving date is described in the handbook under **A member’s birthday** (with the permission **Maintain personal details**) and **Former members** (for everyone who may manage former members). What the tiles for {bereich.points}, {bereich.penalties}, {begriff.rank} and {bereich.titles} open is explained on the handbook pages for these sections.
