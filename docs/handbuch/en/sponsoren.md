This page shows the **{bereich.sponsors}** of {kompanie.name} with their logos, in the order {kompanie.name} has chosen. The page is open to everyone, even without logging in.

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.sponsors}** at the bottom. If the item isn’t in the bar, you’ll find it in a submenu of the navigation.

## What you see here
- One tile per sponsor with their **logo**. If a sponsor has no logo, their name appears instead.
- The tiles are always light so that every logo is easy to see, even in dark mode.
- Anyone who looks after the {bereich.sponsors} sees the **Manage {bereich.sponsors}** button at the top.

## Step by step
### Open a sponsor’s website
1. Tap the logo.
2. If the sponsor has a link, their website opens in a new tab. Without a link, nothing happens.

## Good to know
- If there are no entries yet, the page says “No {bereich.sponsors} yet.”
