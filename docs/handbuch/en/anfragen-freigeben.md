titel: Requests for access
---
Here you approve or decline requests from people who applied for access to the app themselves. When approved, the account is created with the password the person chose in the request. So you don’t have to pass on an initial password.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Requests**. If requests are waiting, the small number in the red circle at the corner of the button shows how many; if none are waiting, it is missing. The button exists as long as **Request access** is switched on or requests are still waiting.

## What you see here
At the top it shows how many requests are waiting. Below it the list with the names, sorted by surname. **Search requests …** finds a request by name, email address or username. With the {bereich.companies} section you can show only one company’s requests under **Filter by company** (or **No company**, **Passive**, **not specified**); if a filter is set, **active** is shown next to it.

Tap a name to open the request. At the top, **‹ All requests** takes you back to the list; with the arrows next to it or by swiping left and right you go to the next or previous request. The card shows:
- **Name** and the day of the request.
- **Details**: the **Email address** (with email sending with **Address confirmed** or **Address not confirmed**, without email sending **Address not checked**), the **Requested username**, the **Company** and the person’s **Message**, if they wrote one. The company is only shown with the {bereich.companies} section: what the person chose, i.e. their company, **No company** or **Passive**. For requests from before this choice it says **not specified**.
- **Link to a member without access?**: only if there are members without access. The app suggests who might match, with the reason (**same email address**, **same name** or **similar name**). Below it you can choose any other member without access.
- **Assign company**: only with the {bereich.companies} section and the permission **Manage {bereich.companies}**. The company from the request is preselected. The section is closed and only shows the selected companies; if the person chose **No company** or **Passive**, it says **none selected** with their choice. Tap **Assign company** to see all companies and change the selection. Without this permission, **Approval** only says that the person will be assigned to the chosen company.
- **Approval**: the **Username** (you can change it), the **Role** (the roles you may give when creating, as when creating a member; preselected is the role an {rolle.admin} has set for this; {rolle.admin} can never be given here), **Approve** and **Decline …**.

## Step by step
### Approve a request
1. Tap the person’s name in the list.
2. Check whether you know the person. If in doubt, better ask before approving.
3. Change the **Username** if necessary and choose the **Role**. With the {bereich.companies} section, check the **Company**.
4. Tap **Approve**. The account exists immediately and is assigned to the chosen company, and the person can log in with their username (or email address) and password. With email sending they get an email.

### Link a request to a member without access
If the person already exists in the app as a member without access (e.g. created for a chronicle), link their request to this member. This way no second account is created, and everything already entered stays with them.
1. Open the request and, under **Link to a member without access?**, choose the matching member, either a suggestion or another one under **Or another member without access**. **No, create a new account** creates a new account instead.
2. Tap **Approve and link** and confirm.
3. The member gets username, email address and password from the request. Name, role and all entries, e.g. for {bereich.offices}, stay.

### Decline a request
1. Open the request and tap **Decline …**.
2. Write a **Reason** if you like. With email sending it appears in the email, otherwise the person sees it when logging in or under **Status of my request**.
3. Tap **Decline**. **Cancel** keeps the request.

After approving or declining you are back in the list; the processed request no longer appears there.

## Good to know
- Everyone who may create members may approve, so {rolle.admin} accounts always may.
- **View only:** with the permission **See accounts** but without **Create members**, you see the list and the cards with name, email address, message and matching members without access (under **Could match**), but without **Approve** and **Decline …**.
- Members without access with the same email address or the same name are suggested. Upper and lower case, spaces, hyphens and umlauts (ä/ae, ö/oe, ü/ue, ß/ss) don’t matter. Similar means: same surname, and one first name begins with the other (e.g. “Chris” and “Christian”). A suggestion is only preselected with the same email address or the same name. Nothing is linked until you tap **Approve and link**.
- The email address and username of a member without access count as free for a request, so that the person can register themselves.
- If the username has been taken in the meantime, the card says so; then enter another one. If an account now exists for the email address, the request can’t be approved, only declined.
- **Company:** the person must choose as soon as there are active companies; they only see the active ones, plus **No company** and **Passive**. With **No company** and **Passive**, approval assigns no company; **Passive** also doesn’t add the person to the list of passive members. On approval, only those who get a role with the **Member** permission are assigned (as elsewhere). If you link the request to a member without access, the company is added to that member’s companies.
- Without email sending the app doesn’t check whether the email address really belongs to the person.
- The app deletes unprocessed requests after 30 days. With email sending the person then gets an email that their request was not processed.
- Your device can show a notification for new requests. To do so, switch on the type **Access requested: new requests** under **Notifications**.
- After approval you find the person in the member list and can add further details there.
