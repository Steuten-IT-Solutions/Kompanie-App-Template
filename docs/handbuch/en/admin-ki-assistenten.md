Here you decide whether signed-in people can connect an AI assistant such as Claude to the app. The AI assistant can then, for example, look up events or write a message to the board. It always acts with the permissions of the person who connected it. This is off until you switch it on.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and **AI assistants**.

## What you see here
- **Signed-in people can connect an AI assistant (e.g. Claude) to the app**: the switch. Off means: no access is valid, and no new one is issued. The accesses stay stored and are valid again when you switch it on.
- **Address for the AI assistant**: the address to enter in the AI assistant, with **Copy address**.
- **Connected AI assistants**: all accesses with program, person, whether it may only read or also make changes, and when it was last used.

## How to proceed
### Switch the access on
1. Switch on **Signed-in people can connect an AI assistant (e.g. Claude) to the app**.
2. Pass the **Address for the AI assistant** on to everyone who wants to use it. How to connect is described under [Connect an AI assistant](seite:ki-assistent-verbinden).

### Remove an access
1. Tap the remove icon next to the access.
2. Confirm. The AI assistant can then no longer do anything in the app until the person connects it again.

## Good to know
- The AI assistant only sees and changes what the person may see and change in the app. It only sees other people’s personal details with the permission for that.
- Changes appear in the [change log](seite:admin-aenderungen) as “Name via Claude”.
- An AI assistant cannot delete, or manage roles, accounts, emails, settings, updates or backups. It never sends board messages by email.
- An access that is not used for 30 days ends. When a person changes their password, all their accesses end.
- Signing in from the AI assistant needs pretty addresses: permalinks on WordPress, addresses without `index.php?r=` in the standalone version. Otherwise a note appears here instead of the address.
