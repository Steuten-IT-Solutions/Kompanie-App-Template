The **Admissions** overview shows all candidates and which of them meet the requirement to be admitted as {rolle.member}, for example six attendances at meetings. Admission is never automatic: the list is a suggestion, and you admit on the person’s page.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then the **Admissions** button. **‹ Admin** takes you back. The small number in the red circle at the corner of the button shows how many candidates currently meet the requirement. Next to it, a number in a circle in your company’s colour shows how many are close to admission (see **Almost met**). If there are none, the respective circle is missing.

The button exists if {bereich.attendance} is switched on. For anyone to appear there, an {rolle.admin} sets up a candidate role under *Settings → Roles*: the **Candidate role** tick on the role. The role can be the person’s role or an additional role. As long as there is none, the page explains how to do it.

## What you see here
- **Requirement met**: everyone who meets everything, with the day since when. Whoever has waited longest is at the top.
- **Almost met**: who is close to admission. That means: for each condition with attendances at most one is missing, and every minimum period is reached within at most 30 days. With **One is enough** it is sufficient if one option is that close. If **Candidate since** is missing, a minimum period never counts as almost met.
- **Not met yet**: who still needs something, e.g. “2 more attendances at “Meeting”” or “Minimum period reached on …”. For a requirement with **One is enough**, “or” appears between the options.
- **No requirement**: candidates whose role has no requirement.
- For each name: the candidate role, **Candidate since** (if known) and what is still missing. On the person’s page, under **Admission**, each condition is listed with ✓ (met) or ○ (open) and the status, e.g. “4 of 6 attendances at “Meeting””; groups are indented.

## Step by step
### Admit someone as {rolle.member}
1. Tap the name. The person’s page opens with **Account and role**.
2. Under **Admission**, tap **Admit as {rolle.member}** and confirm. If the requirement isn’t met yet, the confirmation says so; admitting is still possible.

If the candidate role is the person’s main role, they become {rolle.member}. If it is an additional role, only that additional role is removed. If the person has no **Joining date** yet, the app enters the day of admission.

### Add or change “Candidate since”
1. Open the person’s page and open **Account and role**.
2. Under **Admission**, enter the date at **Candidate since** and tap **Save date**.

## Good to know
- All attendance lists with the matching event type in which the person is **present** count, including from the time before **Candidate since**. **Excused** doesn’t count. A list’s event types come from the event in the calendar or are chosen on the list. If a list has several types, it counts for each.
- The app sets **Candidate since** itself when someone gets the candidate role in the app (when created, when the role is changed or when a request is approved). For people who were candidates before, the date is missing; the minimum period then only applies once you add it.
- After saving an attendance list, the app shows at the top of the admin area who newly meets the requirement as a result.
- The requirement is set by an {rolle.admin} per candidate role under *Settings → Roles*. Conditions can be combined with **All must be met** (AND) and **One is enough** (OR), also in groups. With **One is enough**, “met since” is the day the first option was met; with **All must be met**, the day of the last.
- Candidates see their own status: under *My area* (with **Internal**) or under *Account*, with the same list but without buttons. They don’t see other people’s status.
- **View only:** with the permission **See accounts** but without **Create members**, you see the overview and the status on the person’s page, but without **Save date** and **Admit as {rolle.member}**.
