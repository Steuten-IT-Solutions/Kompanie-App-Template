Here you switch on the languages in which the app is available for your company. German is always on. If you add English, each person chooses their language themselves.

![The Languages page with German and English](bild:admin-sprachen)

## How to open this page

Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and **Languages**.

## What you see here

- **Deutsch**: always switched on, can’t be switched off.
- **English**: English as an additional language, off at first.
- **Save**: applies the choice.

## Step by step

### Switch on English

1. Switch on **English**.
2. Tap **Save**.

A flag then appears at the top of the header. Tapping it lets people choose **Deutsch** or **English**. Logged-in users have the same choice under **My area → Account** in the **Language** card.

### Enter your own names in English
If you have renamed sections or roles, these names at first stay as they are in English too. You enter all English names in one place: in the **Translations** section below the choice of languages. It appears as soon as English is switched on.

1. Tap a group to expand it, e.g. **Navigation sections**, **Roles**, **{bereich.ranks}**, **{bereich.offices}**, **Office groups**, **Event types** or **Submenus**. Next to each group you see how many names are already translated, e.g. **3 of 5**.
2. The German name is on the left; enter the English one on the right. An empty field shows in grey what appears in English without an entry: usually the German name, or the English default (e.g. **Events**) for sections and roles you haven’t renamed.
3. Tap **Save**. The button shows how many names you have changed.

With **Show missing only** you see only names that don’t have an English one yet; all groups then expand. Use the search field to find a name in German or English. At the top you see how many names are still missing in total.

An empty field means: in English the German name applies (or the English default). If you delete an English name and save, the German one applies again. You keep changing the German names where you create them, e.g. under [Navigation](seite:admin-navigation) or [Roles](seite:admin-rollen-und-mitglieder). Event types and submenus belong to their German name: if you rename one in German, enter the English name again afterwards.

### Content in English
Your own pages and {bereich.news} written in the app can get an English version: whoever maintains them chooses **Write in: English** at the top when editing. Messages on the {bereich.board}, events, {bereich.polls}, {bereich.documents} and albums of the {bereich.gallery} get their English version in the form in the **Write in English (optional)** section, categories of the {bereich.documents} via the **In English** button; notifications (and for messages on the {bereich.board}, the email) are then sent in the recipients’ language. Anyone using the app in English who opens content without an English version sees the German one with the note *Only available in German*. You translate posts on your WordPress site with the Polylang plugin (see [Set up {bereich.news}](seite:admin-neuigkeiten)).

### Switch English off again

1. Switch off **English** and tap **Save**.

The flag disappears, and everyone sees the app in German again.

## Good to know

- The choice applies on the device, also for guests who aren’t logged in. Logged-in users keep their language on other devices too, and emails and notifications to them come in this language.
- Translated are the app’s own texts, i.e. buttons, headings and messages, and the default names of the sections and roles (**Termine** becomes **Events**, **Mitglied** becomes **Member**). Names you have given yourselves, e.g. for sections, roles and ranks, and what you enter, e.g. events or news, stay as you wrote them as long as you don’t enter an English version (see above).
- The help behind the **?** also appears in English. Pages not yet available in English are shown in German with the note *Only available in German*.
- After changing the language, the app reloads once.
- What you enter under **Translations** doesn’t change the German names.
- Some things stay German in the English app: the list under **What’s new**, the column names for imports, and in the change log the type of entry and the field names.
