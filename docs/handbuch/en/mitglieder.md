Under **{bereich.directory}** you see who belongs to the company: all {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates), with photo, {bereich.titles} and {begriff.rank}. Everyone can see the list, even without logging in.

![List under {bereich.directory}](bild:mitglieder)

## How to open this page
Tap **Members’ area** in the bar at the bottom and then **{bereich.directory}**; **‹ Members’ area** takes you back. Without logging in there is no **Members’ area**: then tap **{bereich.directory}** at the bottom straight away.

## What you see here
- **Switch** at the top: **{bereich.directory}**, **{bereich.offices}** (if the company uses them; there is a separate handbook page for them) and **Former members**. The app remembers on this device what you chose last.
- **Counter**: how many people there are in each group, e.g. {rolle.member} and candidates.
- **Search members …**: filters the list by name; part of the name is enough.
- **{bereich.companies}** (if people are assigned to {bereich.companies}): shows only the people in one company.
- **Sorting** and **Order**: **Surname** or **First name** (**A → Z** or **Z → A**) or **Joining date** (**Earliest first** or **Latest first**).
- **The list**: a round photo or the initials at the front, then the name with {bereich.titles}. For candidates (people who are still to be admitted as {rolle.member}), the name of their role appears in front like a title, e.g. “Candidate”. If the company uses {bereich.ranks}, the badge with the {begriff.rank} is on the right.

## Step by step
### Open a person’s page
1. Tap the name. Their page opens: photo, name, {bereich.titles} and {begriff.rank}, birthday and joining date (as far as the person shows them), their {bereich.companies} and below that their {bereich.offices} as a timeline.
2. **‹ Back** takes you back to the list.

### Go to the next person
1. On a person’s page, swipe left or right. The page follows your finger, and you already see the next person beside it.
2. On a computer, tap **‹** or **›** at the top right instead; next to it is the position in the list (e.g. “3 / 25”).
3. You go through the people in the order in which the list is currently sorted and filtered, and with **Former members** in their list. If you opened the page from a link or from the {bereich.offices}, you go through the whole list. After the last person comes the first again and vice versa. **‹ Back** then takes you straight to the list.

![A person’s page](bild:mitglieder-person)

### View a photo full size
1. Tap the photo in the list. It appears large and complete.
2. Close it with **✕** or by tapping next to the photo.

### View the former members
1. Tap **Former members** at the top.
2. Below the name you see, as far as entered, “Founding member” and the joining and leaving dates, e.g. “01.04.1985 – 03.05.2010” or “1985–2010”. Here you can also sort by **Leaving date**.

![Former members under {bereich.directory}](bild:mitglieder-ehemalige)

## Good to know
- Guests are not in the list. The list doesn’t show roles (except the candidates’ role), {bereich.points}, {bereich.penalties}, email addresses or usernames. For former members, no reason for leaving is shown.
- Birthday and joining date only appear if the person has chosen this themselves under **My area → Account**: **Don’t show**, **Show to members** (only logged-in {rolle.member} accounts see it) or **Show publicly** (everyone). The year and age for the birthday only if they have switched on **Show age** and entered a year.
- When sorted by **Joining date**, the joining date the person shows on their page counts; anyone who shows none comes last. The app remembers the sorting on this device.
- The photos are uploaded by the people responsible in Admin. If you would like a different photo or none, talk to them.
- As long as the company uses the list, the names, photos, {bereich.titles} and {begriff.rank} of {rolle.member} accounts, candidates and former members are visible to anyone who knows the app’s address, as is who is a candidate.

> **For advanced users:** With WordPress, the shortcode `[kompanie_mitglieder]` also shows the {rolle.member} accounts (without candidates) as a photo gallery on the website.
