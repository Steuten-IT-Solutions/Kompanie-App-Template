If you have nice photos or videos from a festival, you submit them here for an album of the {bereich.gallery}. They only appear once someone with the permission to review approves them.

## How to open this page

Tap **{bereich.gallery}** at the bottom and **Submit photos and videos** (arrow pointing up) at the top. **‹ All albums** takes you back.

If you are already in an album, tap **Submit photos and videos to this album** (arrow pointing up) at the top there. The album is then already chosen, and **‹ Back to album** takes you back into it.

## What you see here

- **Album**: which album the photos and videos should go into.
- **Photos or videos**: one or more files, photos as JPG, PNG or WebP, videos as MP4 or WebM with at most 1 GB per video.
- **Consent**: you confirm that you hold the rights to the photos and videos and that the recognisable people agree. Without the tick nothing can be submitted.
- **Your submissions**: each photo and video (with ▶) with album and status: _awaiting approval_, _approved_ or _not approved_ with the reason.

## Step by step

### Submit photos and videos

1. Choose the **Album** (if you come from an album, it is already chosen).
2. Under **Photos or videos**, choose the files.
3. Tick the consent box.
4. Tap **Submit**. The app uploads the files one after another and shows how far it has got, for a video also as a percentage. Keep the page open meanwhile.

### Withdraw a submission

As long as a photo or video is awaiting approval, tap **Withdraw** next to it and confirm. The file is deleted.

## Good to know

- If the **Submit photos and videos** button is missing (in the album too), an admin has switched off submitting. Photos and videos already submitted remain and are still reviewed.
- When a photo is submitted, the server removes metadata such as the location and stores the photo at most as large as the other photos of the {bereich.gallery}. If the {bereich.gallery} uses Google Drive, the photo then moves in the background to a private folder in Drive and only into the album once approved.
- The app uploads a video in pieces, so it works with large phone videos too. As a preview it takes a still from the first second.
- Those who review get a notification when you submit photos or videos. You get one as soon as a photo or video is approved or declined (adjustable under **My area → Notifications**).
- The server deletes declined photos and videos immediately; the reason stays here.
- Pending submissions remain until someone deals with them. If your account is deleted, they disappear; photos already approved stay in the {bereich.gallery} without your name.
