Pages of {kompanie.name}’s own, for example its history, the festival programme or directions. Each page has a title and its content below: text, often with pictures, and sometimes ready-made blocks such as a countdown.

## How to open this page
Each page is an item of its own in the navigation, with its title: tap the page’s title at the bottom. If it isn’t in the bar, you’ll find it in a submenu of the navigation, for example under **Members’ area**.

## What you see here
- The page’s title is at the top, its content below.
- A **countdown** counts down to a festival or event, with two figures (e.g. days and hours) and the date below. While the event is on, a text appears there; afterwards a closing text or nothing at all.
- Anyone who looks after the pages sees the note **members only** on pages for members only, and the **Manage {bereich.pages}** button at the top.
- **Only available in German**: the page has no English version yet, so you see the German one.

## Good to know
- Some pages are open to everyone, even without logging in, others only to members. You don’t see pages you aren’t allowed to read at all. Individual parts of a page can also be for members only; without logging in they are missing.
- Anyone who looks after the pages sees the note **members only** on such parts.
- If a page you expect is missing, log in.
