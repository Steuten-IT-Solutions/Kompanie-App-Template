titel: "Manage the {bereich.gallery}"
---
Those who manage the {bereich.gallery} create albums, upload photos and videos, move and delete them and make them members-only or public, directly in the {bereich.gallery}. This works with everything created or uploaded in the app. If Google Drive is where the {bereich.gallery} is stored, it works the same way with albums and photos from Drive: the app then changes them in Drive. Otherwise you change them in Drive. You choose an album’s cover image in the app for all albums, including those from Drive.

## How to open this page

Tap **{bereich.gallery}** at the bottom. The {bereich.gallery}’s buttons are round icons; on a computer the mouse pointer shows their name. At the top you have **Create albums and upload** (folder with plus), in an album of the app **Edit album** (pencil). To select, press and hold an album or photo.

## What you see here

- **Create albums and upload**: all albums with the number of photos from Drive and of uploaded ones, below them **New album in the app**. Tapping an album opens it: there you upload photos and videos and see the uploaded ones.
- **Select albums**: press and hold an album from the app. It is then selected; tap more. Selected ones have a coloured frame. Albums from Google Drive can only be selected if Google Drive is the storage location. At the top you then have **Change year** (calendar) and **Delete** (bin).
- **Select photos and videos**: in an album, press and hold an uploaded photo or video (with Google Drive as storage location, any of them). It is then selected; tap more. Selected ones have a tick ✓. At the top you have **Select all** (double tick), **Move** (folder with arrow), **Members only** (lock), **Public** (globe) and **Delete** (bin).
- **Stop selecting**: tap the selected ones again. When nothing is selected any more, selecting ends by itself. **Back** (the phone’s key or gesture) and the Escape key on a computer also end it, as does every completed action. If a window is open (e.g. **Move**), **Back** first only closes the window; the selection stays.
- In the **large view** of a photo, **Use as album cover** (picture with mountains) is at the bottom. For an uploaded photo it is followed by **Members only** (lock) or **Show publicly** (globe), **Move** and **Delete**.

## Step by step

### Create an album and upload photos

1. Tap **Create albums and upload**.
2. Under **New album in the app**, enter **Title** and **Year** and tap **Create album**. The album opens.
3. Set **Members only (internal)** if needed and tap **Upload photos or videos**. Choose the files.
4. The new album appears in the {bereich.gallery} as soon as it contains a photo or video.

### Move photos or videos to another album

1. Open the album and press and hold a photo or video that should move.
2. Tap the photos and videos that should move, or **Select all**.
3. Tap **Move**, choose the destination under **To album** and tap **Move**.
   You can also move a single photo in the large view with **Move**. If the album is empty afterwards, you return to the overview.

### Delete photos or videos

1. Open the album, press and hold a photo or video and tap more.
2. Tap **Delete** and confirm. The files are then deleted from the server.
   You can also delete a single photo in the large view with **Delete**.

### Make photos or videos members-only or public

Select them as above and tap **Members only** (only {rolle.member} accounts see them) or **Public** (everyone sees them). You switch a single photo in the large view.

### Choose an album’s cover image

1. Open the album and tap the photo that should represent the album in the overview.
2. In the large view, tap **Use as album cover** at the bottom. The button is then highlighted, and **· Cover image** appears next to the number.
   If you tap the button again for the chosen photo (**Choose cover automatically again**), the app chooses the cover itself again: the first public photo by time taken. Videos can’t be chosen as cover.

### Rename an album or move it to another year

1. Open the album and tap **Edit album**.
2. Change **Title** or **Year** and tap **Save**.
   You move several albums to another year in the overview: press and hold an album, tap more, **Change year**, enter the year, **Edit**.

### Enter an album’s title in English too
If the admin has switched on English under **Settings → Languages**, an admin enters the English title of an app album there, in the **Translations** section in the **Gallery albums** group. **Edit album** only shows a note with a link to it. Those using the app in English see this title; if it is empty, the German one appears. An album from Google Drive keeps its German name in Drive.

### Delete an album

In the album, tap **Edit album** and then **Delete album**, or in the overview select one or more albums by pressing and holding and tap **Delete**. The confirmation says how many photos and videos are deleted with it.

## Good to know

- With Google Drive as the storage location, albums are folders in the gallery folder: a new album is a new folder in its year’s folder (if that doesn’t exist, the app creates it), renaming renames the folder, another year moves it. **Members only** moves a photo into the album’s **intern** folder, **Public** moves it out again. **Delete** puts photos, videos and albums into the Google Drive bin; there they can be restored for 30 days and reappear after the next sync. Albums previously created only in the app get a folder in Drive in the background after switching; until then photos from Drive can’t be moved there.
- Otherwise deleted photos, videos and albums can’t be restored. Anyone who viewed or saved a photo beforehand keeps that copy in any case.
- When moved, a public photo gets a new address; an old link to the photo then leads nowhere. Links to the album stay the same.
- If a photo came from a submission, the submission counts towards the new album after moving; after deleting it counts as removed again.
- If the chosen cover image is **members only**, only {rolle.member} accounts see it in the overview. Everyone else still sees a public photo of the album as the cover; an internal photo thus never becomes public.
- If you move or delete the chosen cover image, the app chooses the album’s cover itself again.
- The {bereich.gallery} doesn’t show an empty album. You find it under **Create albums and upload**.
- The source of the {bereich.gallery} and the sync with Google Drive are set up by the admin under **Settings → Gallery**.
