Here you get to know how the app is built: the header at the top, the bar with the areas at the bottom and the notes that appear from time to time. This way you’ll find your way around every screen.

![The app’s start page for a member, with the header at the top and the areas at the bottom](bild:app-ueberblick)

## How to open this page
Open the app’s address in the browser or tap the app’s icon on your home screen. The app starts with the first area of the bottom bar, usually **{bereich.events}**.

## What you see here
- **Logo and title** (top left): the title names the screen you are on. Below it, if you are logged in, is a greeting with your name, otherwise the name of {kompanie.name}.
- **Share icon** (at the top, three connected dots): sends a link to the current screen, see below.
- **?**: opens the help for the current screen.
- **Flag** (only if {kompanie.name} has switched on English as well): choose **Deutsch** or **English**.
- **Moon or sun**: switches between dark and light appearance.
- **Login** (top right, only when not logged in): takes you to the login, see [Log in](seite:anmelden).
- **Bottom bar**: here you switch between the areas. The area you are in is highlighted. If you hold the phone sideways or use the app on a computer or tablet, the bar is on the left.

Which areas are at the bottom depends on which ones {kompanie.name} uses and whether you are logged in:
- **{bereich.events}**, **{bereich.news}** and **{bereich.gallery}**: for everyone, even without logging in.
- **{bereich.directory}**: for everyone, if {kompanie.name} uses the list. If it only uses the {bereich.offices}, the button is called **{bereich.offices}**.
- **{bereich.documents}**: only for logged-in {rolle.member} accounts.
- **Members’ area**: for {rolle.member} accounts (and anyone with the **Members’ area** permission), {bereich.directory} and {bereich.documents} aren’t at the bottom individually but together under **Members’ area**. {rolle.member} accounts also find the {bereich.board}, the {bereich.ranks} and the {bereich.catalog} there, if {kompanie.name} uses them.
- **Admin**: only if you have a task in administration, e.g. awarding {bereich.points}.
- **My area** (far right): your own details and your account, once you are logged in.

## Without logging in
Without logging in, you only see what is public: the public {bereich.events}, the {bereich.news}, the public photos of the {bereich.gallery} and, if there is one, the {bereich.directory} list. **Login** is at the top right. Some companies greet guests on the start page with a short text.

## Step by step
### Open help for the current screen
1. Tap **?** at the top.
2. The app shows the instructions for the screen you were just on. If there are no separate instructions for it, you see the index of all help pages.
3. **‹ All help pages** takes you to the index. There, **Search help …** finds a page by a keyword.

The help only shows you the pages that match your tasks and the areas of {kompanie.name}. At the bottom of every help page you can report a bug, see [Report a bug](seite:fehler-melden).

### Send a screen as a link
1. Open the screen you want to show.
2. Tap the share icon at the top.
3. On a phone the share menu opens, e.g. for WhatsApp or email. On a computer the app copies the link; “Link copied” appears. If copying doesn’t work, the app shows the link in a window for you to select and copy.

Whoever opens the link lands directly on this screen. If it is only for logged-in users, the app first asks them to log in and then opens it. Each person only sees what they are allowed to see; otherwise the app opens their start page. An expanded event or a chosen sorting isn’t part of the link.

### Hold the phone sideways
1. Turn the phone. The app turns with it, as long as the phone’s auto-rotate is switched on.
2. Sideways, the bar with the areas is on the left, and the header is flatter and scrolls away. Lists are in two columns, and in forms two fields sit side by side.
3. Turn the phone back, and everything is as usual again.

If you installed the app a while ago and it stays upright, reopen it once; it can take up to a day for the phone to pick up the change.

### On a computer or tablet
If the window is at least 900 pixels wide, the app uses the full width:
- The bar with the areas is a vertical bar on the left edge.
- Lists are in several columns, and in forms three or four short fields sit side by side. Long text fields span the whole row.
- Reading texts such as news or the handbook stay in an easy-to-read column in the middle, as do pages with tiles.
- Lists that you sort by dragging are in one column in the middle.
- Your own profile under **My area** and a member in Admin are in two columns.

If you make the window narrower, the app looks like it does on a phone again.

### Switch between light and dark
1. Tap the moon at the top to switch on the dark appearance, or the sun for the light one.
2. If you switch to the appearance your device currently has, the app follows the device again.

You can set the appearance permanently under **My area** → **Account**, see [My account](seite:anmelden-und-konto). The choice only applies on this device.

## Notes at the top of the page
Notes sometimes appear below the header:
- **Get the app on your phone**: tap **Show me how** for the illustrated instructions or, if your browser offers it, **Install**. **✕** hides the note. If a message from the {bereich.board} or the maintenance note is already shown at the top, this note waits until it is gone. More under [Get the app on your phone](seite:app-installieren).
- **{bereich.board}**: logged-in {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates) see urgent messages from the company here, and {rolle.member} accounts also see {bereich.polls} they haven’t answered yet. Tapping the title opens the message on the board; **×** hides an entry on this device.
- **Offline – showing the most recently loaded data.**: you have no internet connection at the moment. What you loaded last stays visible, including photos and logos you have seen before.
- **Poor connection – showing the most recently loaded data.**: the network isn’t responding at the moment, or only very slowly. After a few seconds the app shows what you loaded last; if the response does arrive, the new state appears by itself.
- **The app has been updated**: there is a new version of the app, and it has already been loaded on your device. Tap **Restart** to see the new features straight away. If you have entered something and not saved it yet, the app asks first. **✕** hides the note; the next time you open the app, the new version then applies by itself. The app looks for a new version when you bring it to the front again (at most every 15 minutes) and once an hour while it is open.
- **This page is only visible after signing in.**: you have opened a link to a screen for logged-in users. Tap **Log in**.

## Good to know
- If the app asks before doing something (e.g. whether you really want to delete something), a separate app window opens with **Cancel** and **OK**. The rest of the page is dimmed meanwhile. Deleting and removing are on a red button. **Cancel**, **✕** or the Esc key change nothing.
- If an app window is open (e.g. for moving a photo, or a question), **Back** (the phone’s button or gesture, the browser’s back button) first closes the window without changing anything. You stay on the page. Only when nothing is open any more does **Back** take you to the previous page.
- If you have entered something and not saved it yet (e.g. because there is no internet connection right now), the app asks before you leave the page: **Leave** discards the entries, **Cancel** stays on the page. This also applies to **Back** and to reloading the page. If a change doesn’t get through without a connection, the app says “No connection – not saved. Your entries are still here; please save again later.”; save again as soon as you have a network.
- If the app is being maintained, you only see a note about it instead of the content. **Try again** checks whether it is back.
- Each company chooses the names of the areas (e.g. **{bereich.events}**) itself. That’s why they may be called differently for you than in other apps.
