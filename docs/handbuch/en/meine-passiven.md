If you are recorded as the contact person for passive members, you see them here. A passive member’s card shows all details: name, address, phone, email address, date of birth, joining date and remarks, and whether the fee for the current year has been paid.

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom. Then tap the menu at the top and choose **My passive members**. The item only exists if you look after at least one passive member.

## What you see here
- A list with the names of your passive members (surname, first name). Tapping one opens their card.
- On the card, the **Fees** (the current year and every year with a payment: amount, date and payment method, or **open**; before the joining year **not a member yet**) and the **Details**: address, phone, email address and date of birth, plus joining date and remarks if entered. **‹ My passive members** takes you back; the arrows or swiping take you to the next one.
- Tapping the address opens the location in the maps app, tapping the number calls it, tapping the email address opens a new email.

## Good to know
- You can’t change anything here. If a detail is wrong, let the person who manages the passive members know.
- Who you look after is decided by whoever has the **Manage passive members** permission.
