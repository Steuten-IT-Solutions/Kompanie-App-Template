Under **{bereich.offices}** you see who currently holds which office and who held it before, as a timeline that can reach far back. Everyone can see it, even without logging in.

![{bereich.offices} by group](bild:aemter)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** in the bar at the bottom, then **{bereich.directory}** and at the top **{bereich.offices}**. Without logging in there is no **Internal**; then tap **{bereich.directory}** at the bottom straight away. If the company doesn’t use a member list, the button is called **{bereich.offices}** directly.

## What you see here
- **Expand all** (or **Collapse all**): opens or closes all cards at once, including the joint terms of office.
- **Groups**, e.g. the board: their offices are listed below them.
- **Joint terms of office**: if a group has joint terms (e.g. a royal court), there is one card per term with the group photo, the group’s name in the singular and the years (e.g. “Royal court 2026–2028”) and who held which office, with the office in the singular (e.g. “Prince”); the running term is marked **current**. The running term is expanded (if none is running, the newest one), the others are collapsed and only show years and names; tap a term’s heading to expand or collapse it.
- **One card per office**: a seal with the office’s initials, who currently holds it (with photos), otherwise “most recently” and the last term, and how far back the chronicle goes.

## Step by step
### View an office’s chronicle
1. Tap the office’s card. It expands.
2. At the top, under **In office**, are all who currently hold the office, with photo and for how long.
3. Below it, under **Previous**, follows the chronicle: on the left the year in which the term began (below it “until …”), on the right the people, the duration and a short note, if there is one.

![Expanded chronicle of an office](bild:aemter-chronik)

### See all offices by year
1. Tap **Years** at the top.
2. Each office has a column, sorted by group; each row is a year, the newest at the top. A bar shows who held the office in those years. Anyone who held it at the same time as others (e.g. the flag) is shown next to them.
3. Running terms are coloured more strongly, honours have a star. If you tap a bar, the years, people and note appear at the bottom; on a computer it is enough to point at it with the mouse.
4. If there are many offices, you slide the overview sideways; older years are further down. The offices at the top and the years on the left stay in place. You scroll the whole page up and down as usual; at the very top is the **Chronicle | Years** switch again.
5. On a phone the overview is narrower. If you turn the phone sideways, it uses the full width and more offices fit side by side.
6. **Chronicle** takes you back to the cards. The app remembers your choice.

### See a person’s offices
1. Tap their name in the chronicle or under **In office**.
2. Their page opens with all their offices as a timeline.

## Good to know
- The newest term is at the top. In long lists, markers such as **2000s** divide the decades. An honour has a star and the note **Honour**. A single year without an honour, e.g. a king’s year, appears like any other term.
- If “with …” appears separately under a person, that is their companion (e.g. their partner), who doesn’t hold the office themselves.
- Only people who are in the member list can be tapped. People without an account appear with their name and, if the company has entered one, with a photo.
- On a phone the chronicle is in one column, on wide screens alternately left and right of the line.
- As a {rolle.member}, you’ll also find your own offices under **My area**.

> **For advanced users:** With WordPress, the shortcode `[kompanie_aemter]` also shows the offices on the website, `[kompanie_aemter gruppe="…"]` only the offices of one group and `[kompanie_aemter amt="…"]` only one office (both can be combined, e.g. if an office exists in two groups).
