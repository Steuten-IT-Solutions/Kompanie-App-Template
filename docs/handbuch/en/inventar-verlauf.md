The history shows every change in the {bereich.inventory}: when, who, what changed and why. This way you find out where something went.

![The full history in the {bereich.inventory}](bild:inventar-verlauf)

## How to open this page
Open the {bereich.inventory} via **Admin** and tap **Full history** at the top. You open the history of a single entry by opening the item and tapping **History** (clock) at the place.

## What you see here
- **‹ Back to {bereich.inventory}**: back to the list.
- **Heading**: “Full history” or, for a single entry, “History:” with name and location.
- **Type of change**: **Created**, **Changed**, **Moved** or **Deleted**.
- **Time and name**: when the change was made and who made it.
- **Item and location**: only in the full history, so you know which entry is meant.
- **What changed**: e.g. “Quantity: 250 pieces → 249 pieces” or “Moved: 150 pieces from clubhouse to festival-ground garage”.
- **Reason**: shown below if someone gave one.
- **Load older entries**: at the bottom if there are more than 100 entries, and fetches the next ones.

![The history of a single entry](bild:inventar-verlauf-eintrag)

## Good to know
- The history is seen by everyone allowed to see the {bereich.inventory}, even without the permission to change.
- The newest changes are at the top.
- When moving, the old and the new place each get an entry in the history. At the destination it says whether the quantity was added or the entry was newly created.
- Deleted entries also keep their history. You still find them in the full history.
- You can only view the history, not change it.
