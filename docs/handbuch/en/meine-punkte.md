Here you see how many {bereich.points} you have and what you got them for. The page is for viewing only; other members don’t see your {bereich.points}.

![My {bereich.points} with total and history](bild:meine-punkte)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom, then the menu at the top, and choose **{bereich.points}**. Or tap the **{bereich.points}** tile in the overview.

## What you see here
- **My {bereich.points}**: your total.
- **History**: each entry with reason, date, who recorded it (“recorded by …”) and the value on the right. The newest are at the top. Deductions have a minus sign.
- Without entries it says “No entries yet.”

## Good to know
- Entries are made by the people responsible, not by you. If an entry is cancelled, it disappears from the history and no longer counts.
- **{bereich.points} factor**: some roles have a factor, e.g. ×0.5 for young members. An entry then counts correspondingly less (more with a factor above 1). The history already shows the value that counts. The factor is stored with the entry; if your role changes later, older entries stay as they are. The total can therefore have decimal places.
- The app can send you a message with value and reason for every new entry. You switch this on or off under **My area** → **Account** → **Set up notifications** with **{bereich.points}: new entries**.
