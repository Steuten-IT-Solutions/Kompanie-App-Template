Here you set up the app for your company: which sections there are, who may do what, the connections to Google and the status of the app. Only {rolle.admin} accounts see this page.

![The settings overview with tiles per group](bild:admin-einstellungen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Settings**.

## What you see here
Tiles, sorted into groups. Tap a tile to open its page. With **‹ Settings** at the top you get back to the overview from there.

- **App**
  - **Sections**: which sections the app has for everyone. See [Sections](seite:admin-bereiche).
  - **Navigation**: which sections are in the bar at the bottom and which in submenus, and the order of the admin buttons. See [Navigation](seite:admin-navigation).
  - **Legal notice and privacy**: links to the legal notice and privacy policy, visible to everyone. See [Legal notice and privacy](seite:admin-rechtliches).
  - **Languages**: whether the app offers English besides German. See [Languages](seite:admin-sprachen).
- **Members and access**
  - **Roles**: who may do what in the app, below that whether people can request access themselves. See [Roles](seite:admin-rollen-und-mitglieder) and [Set up access requests](seite:admin-zugang).
- **Notifications**
  - **Notifications**: registered devices and time of the reminder. See [Set up notifications](seite:admin-benachrichtigungen).
- **Connections**
  - **Email**: the company address and mail server, e.g. for messages from the **{bereich.board}** section. See [Set up email](seite:admin-email).
  - **Google access**: the connection to Google for the {bereich.gallery} and calendar. See [Google access](seite:admin-google).
  - **{bereich.gallery}**: the folder in Google Drive with the photo albums. See [Set up the {bereich.gallery}](seite:admin-galerie).
  - **News**: where the {bereich.news} come from, including **Write in the app**. If the section has a different name for you than “News”, its name appears here.
- **Events and calendar**
  - **Calendar**: where the members’ calendar comes from, below that the settings for {bereich.birthdays} (only if {bereich.birthdays} is on). See [Set up the calendar](seite:admin-kalender).
- **Operation**
  - **Status and updates**: traffic light per section, installed version, updates and error log. See [Status and updates](seite:admin-zustand).
  - **Backup**: download or restore a backup. See [Backup](seite:admin-updates-und-sicherung).
  - **Maintenance mode**: open the app temporarily only for {rolle.admin} accounts. See [Maintenance mode](seite:admin-wartungsmodus).
  - **Usage**: who uses the app when and which sections. See [Usage](seite:admin-nutzung).
  - **What’s new**: changes from version to version. See [What’s new](seite:admin-neuerungen).

## Good to know
- With WordPress the {bereich.news} come from the website’s posts at first; under **News** you can switch to **Write in the app**. With WordPress you also find some settings in the WordPress back end on the app’s page.
- If something doesn’t work, [Common problems](seite:haeufige-probleme) often helps.
