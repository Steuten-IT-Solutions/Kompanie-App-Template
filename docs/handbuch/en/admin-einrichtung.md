After the installation, the setup assistant takes you step by step through the essentials: choose sections, connect the calendar, {bereich.gallery} and {bereich.news}, enter the legal notice and privacy policy, take over members. Everything you set here can be changed later under **Settings**.

![The assistant’s first step: choosing the sections](bild:admin-einrichtung)

## How to open this page
As long as setup hasn’t been finished, {rolle.admin} accounts see **Welcome!** with the **Start setup** button at the top of every page. Tap it. If the app has no section yet, you land in the assistant straight after logging in.

## What you see here
- **Step … of …**: where you are. How many steps there are depends on the chosen sections.
- **Welcome!**: the first step with one switch per section and a sentence on what it is for.
- **Back** and **Continue**: below the middle steps. With **Continue** you skip a step without saving anything.

## Step by step
### Choose sections
1. Switch on the sections your company uses.
2. Tap **Continue**. The choice is now saved.

You can’t skip this step. Without a section the app only shows the installation guide. More on the individual switches: [Sections](seite:admin-bereiche).

### Connect the calendar, {bereich.gallery} and {bereich.news}
For each chosen section that needs a connection, a separate step follows:
- **Calendar for members** (if {bereich.calendar} is on), see [Set up the calendar](seite:admin-kalender).
- **Google access** and **{bereich.gallery}** (folder in Google Drive, if {bereich.gallery} is on), see [Google access](seite:admin-google) and [Set up the {bereich.gallery}](seite:admin-galerie). If {bereich.gallery} is off, Google access is in the calendar step.
- **News** (if {bereich.news} is on), see [Set up {bereich.news}](seite:admin-neuigkeiten).

1. Fill in the fields and tap **Save** in the section.
2. Tap **Continue**. If you want to do this later, tap **Continue** straight away.

![The step with Google access and {bereich.gallery}](bild:admin-einrichtung-galerie)

### Legal notice and privacy
Here you enter the links to your legal notice and privacy policy, e.g. on your website, and tap **Save**. The app shows them to everyone, even without logging in. More on this: [Legal notice and privacy](seite:admin-rechtliches).

### Import members
If you have a member list in Excel or as CSV, you create all members at once here, including former members if you like. How this works is described under [Import members](seite:mitglieder-import). After importing, tap **Continue to the last step**. Without a list, simply tap **Continue**.

![The Import members step](bild:admin-einrichtung-mitglieder)

### Finish setup
1. In the last step **Almost done**, leave **Send status reports to the app’s maintainers** ticked or untick it. The tick only exists if your app provides for status reports (see [Status and updates](seite:admin-zustand)).
2. Tap **Finish setup**. The app opens the admin area.

![The last step Almost done](bild:admin-einrichtung-fertig)

## Good to know
- Setup counts as finished as soon as an {rolle.admin} has tapped **Finish setup**. After that the **Welcome!** note disappears for everyone.
- You create further members afterwards in the admin area; you give them a username and starting password (see [Create member](seite:mitglieder-anlegen)).
