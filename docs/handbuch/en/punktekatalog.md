In the {bereich.catalog} you create frequent occasions with their number of points as templates, e.g. “Work duty – 4”. When awarding, you then just choose the template instead of typing the points and reason every time.

![The {bereich.catalog} with active and deactivated templates](bild:punktekatalog)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Setup** and the **{bereich.catalog}** button. **‹ Setup** takes you back.

## What you see here
- **New template** and **{bereich.points}**: the form for a new template, with the **Add** button.
- Below it all templates with name, “active” or “deactivated” and the number of points on the right. From two templates on, each has a handle on the left (six dots) for moving it.
- For each template the buttons **Change value** and **Deactivate** (or **Activate**).
- Without templates it says “No templates yet. Create the first one above.”

## Step by step
### Create a template
1. Under **New template**, enter the name, e.g. “King’s shooting”.
2. Next to it, under **{bereich.points}**, enter the number of points (a whole number).
3. Tap **Add**.

![Filled-in form for a new template](bild:punktekatalog-neu)

### Change the order
1. Hold the handle on the left of the template and drag it to the new position. At the edge of the screen the page scrolls along.
2. Let go. The order is saved immediately.
3. With a keyboard: tap the handle and move the template up or down with the arrow keys.

### Change the number of points
1. Tap **Change value** next to the template.
2. Enter the new number of points in the window and confirm.

### Remove a template from the selection
1. Tap **Deactivate** next to the template. It is no longer offered when awarding.
2. If you need it again, tap **Activate**.

## Good to know
- The order here also applies to the **Template from the catalogue** selection when awarding. New templates go to the end. Deactivated ones can be moved too.
- A template can’t be deleted, only deactivated.
- Changes only affect future entries. What has already been awarded stays as it is.
- A template’s name can’t be changed here; if needed, create a new one and deactivate the old one.
