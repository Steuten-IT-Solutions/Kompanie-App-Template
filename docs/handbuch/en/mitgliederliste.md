In the admin area’s member list you find everyone in the company and open a member’s page to change something there. What the rows show depends on your tasks.

![Member list in the admin area](bild:mitgliederliste)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Members**. If the member list is your only task, **Admin** opens it straight away.

## What you see here
- **Counter** at the very top: how many people there are per group, e.g. {rolle.member} accounts and separately counted roles such as candidates. Guests are not counted. Search and filters don’t change the counter.
- **+ Create member**: only if you may create members (see **Create member** in the handbook).
- **Search members …**: filters the list by name; part of the name is enough.
- **Filter and sorting**: closed at first. If a filter is set, **active** is shown next to it.
- **The list**: one row per person. Sorted by surname it shows “Surname, first name”. Depending on your tasks, {bereich.titles} and open {bereich.penalties} are shown below. If the company uses {bereich.ranks}, the badge with the {begriff.rank} is on the right. As soon as there are photos, a small round photo or the initials appear at the front.

## Step by step
### Filter and sort
1. Tap **Filter and sorting**.
2. Under **All roles**, choose a role to see only people with that role (as main or additional role). If there are {bereich.titles}, filter by a title under **All {bereich.titles}**.
3. Under **Sorting**, choose **Surname** or **First name**. Those who see accounts can also sort by **Joining date**, those who award {bereich.points} by **{bereich.points}**.
4. Tap the button under **Order** to reverse the direction (e.g. **A → Z** or **Z → A**, **Earliest first** or **Latest first**, **Most first** or **Fewest first**).

![Opened filter and sorting](bild:mitgliederliste-filter)

### Open a member
1. Tap the person’s row. Their page opens (see [A member’s page](seite:mitglied-uebersicht)).
2. **‹ All members** takes you back to the same place in the list, with the same search term and the same filters.

## Good to know
- Sorted by joining date, the row shows the joining date; sorted by {bereich.points}, the {bereich.points}. Those without a joining date come last. A newly chosen sorting starts with A → Z, with the earliest joining date or with the most {bereich.points}.
- Your device remembers the sorting. If you open the admin area again later, the list starts at the top again.
- The list also includes people with only the internal calendar (e.g. candidates). If their role can be admitted as {rolle.member}, the role’s name appears next to the name like a title, as in the list under **{bereich.directory}**.
- Members who have left or died are not here but under **Former members**.
- Accounts without a role in the app ({rolle.guest}) are not in the list. Those who see accounts find the tick **Also show guest accounts (to give them a role)** under **Filter and sorting**; {rolle.guest} can also be chosen under **All roles** without the tick.
