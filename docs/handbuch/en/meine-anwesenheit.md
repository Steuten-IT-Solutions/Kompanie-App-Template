Here you see at which events you have been recorded as present, excused or absent.

![Your own {bereich.attendance} with totals and events](bild:meine-anwesenheit)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom. Then tap the menu at the top and choose **{bereich.attendance}**.

## What you see here
- **Totals**: at the top, e.g. “2 present · 0 excused · 1 absent”.
- **Events**: for each event the title, the date and who recorded you. The most recent are at the top.
- **present**, **excused**, **absent**: on the right, your entry for this event.

## Good to know
- Entries are made by the people responsible. You can’t change anything here yourself.
- Events for which nothing has been recorded about you yet don’t appear. If nothing at all has been recorded yet, it says “No attendance has been recorded for you yet.”
- You can’t see other people’s {bereich.attendance}.
