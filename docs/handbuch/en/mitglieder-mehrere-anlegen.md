Here you create several people in one step, e.g. all new members after a meeting. Afterwards the login details of all new accounts are on one page.

![Creating several members at once](bild:mitglieder-mehrere-anlegen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Members**, **+ Create member** and at the top **Create several at once**.

## What you see here
- **‹ Create one by one**: back to the form for one person.
- **Role for everyone**: the roles you may assign, as when [creating one member](seite:mitglieder-anlegen). The role applies to each person.
- **Additional roles (optional)**: only if you may assign roles and the chosen role belongs to the company. They apply to each person.
- **Person 1**, **Person 2** …: per person **First name**, **Surname**, **No access (doesn’t log in yet)** (then without a username), **Username (optional)**, **Email (optional)**, **Mobile number (optional)**, **Address (optional)** to expand, **Birthday (optional)** and **Photo (optional)**. For {rolle.member} there is also **{bereich.points} (starting balance, optional)** if you may manage {bereich.points}.
- **+ Another person**: adds a row.
- **Date of the starting points**: the date for all starting balances.
- **Create all**.

## Step by step
### Create several people
1. If offered, choose the **Role for everyone** and the **Additional roles**.
2. Enter at least the first name for each person. For people who don’t log in yet, tick **No access**. For more people, tap **+ Another person**.
3. Tap **Create all**.
4. Print the login details (**Print**, one slip per person) or copy them with **Copy login details**, and hand them over in person.

## Good to know
- At most 50 rows; empty rows are skipped.
- The app checks all entries first. If one is invalid (e.g. a username already in use), nobody is created, and the message names the row.
- The app uploads the photos one by one afterwards. If one fails, this is shown next to the person; you then add the photo on their page.
- You don’t enter the {begriff.rank} here, but afterwards on each member’s page.
- The starting passwords are shown **only this one time**. People **without access** don’t get one; they are listed in a separate row. How they get access later is described under [Create member](seite:mitglieder-anlegen).
