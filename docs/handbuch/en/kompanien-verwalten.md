Here you create the {bereich.companies}, with logo, description and links, set their order and assign members to them.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Setup** and the **{bereich.companies}** button. **‹ Setup** takes you back.

## What you see here
- All {bereich.companies} in the order of the list, with a small preview of the logo, the name, short name, year founded and the number of members. Disbanded ones are marked **former**.
- On the left the **handle** (six dots) for moving.
- For each entry **Edit** (pencil), **Members**, **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo**), **Remove logo** and **Delete** (bin).
- At the bottom the **New company** form.

## Step by step
### Create a company
1. Under **New company**, enter the **Name**. If you like, also **Short name**, **Year founded**, **Description**, **Website** and **Company app**.
2. Tap **Create**. The company then appears at the very bottom.

### Assign members
1. Tap **Members** next to the company.
2. Tick the people; **Search** finds them faster.
3. Tap **Save**.

You can also assign individual people on their page: **Admin** → **Members** → person → **{bereich.companies}** tile. A person can belong to several companies.

### Mark a company as disbanded
1. Tap **Edit** and tick **Disbanded**.
2. Tap **Save**. The company now appears at the bottom as “former”; its members stay assigned.

### Upload a logo
Tap **Upload logo** or **Replace logo** and choose the file. The logo is saved immediately.

### Change the order
Hold an entry by the handle on the left, drag it to the desired position and let go. The order is saved immediately. On a computer it also works with the keyboard: select the handle with the Tab key, then arrow up or down.

### Delete a company
Tap **Delete** and confirm. The logo and all member assignments are then gone. For a disbanded company, **Disbanded** is usually better.

## Good to know
- New assignments are only possible for {rolle.member} accounts.
- **View only:** with the permission **See assignment to {bereich.companies}** but without **Maintain {bereich.companies}**, you see on a member’s page which {bereich.companies} they belong to, even if the **{bereich.directory}** section is switched off. You can’t assign with it, and you don’t have this page.
- Logos: PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, at most 2 MB. Pictures are scaled down to at most 512 pixels on the longer edge. A transparent background is kept; the logo then sits directly on the card, in the light and the dark design. A logo with a transparent background that is easy to see on light and dark backgrounds works best.
- You can copy text from your website into the **Description**. Colours that would be hard to read on the background (e.g. dark text in the dark design) are shown by the app in its normal text colour; very large line spacing is set to normal when displayed. The text stays stored as you entered it.
- For **Website** and **Company app** the address without `https://` is enough, e.g. `example.com/app`; the app adds `https://` when you leave the field. It doesn’t accept insecure addresses with `http://`. Anything wrong with an address is shown directly below the field. The year founded can be as far back as you like, but not in the future.
- Pictures in the description are seen by everyone who sees the {bereich.companies}, even without logging in. Very large pictures are scaled down to at most 2048 pixels on the longer edge.
- Who sees the {bereich.companies} is set by the “{bereich.companies}” permission in the roles; in new installations everyone sees them, even without logging in. Who belongs to which company is only shown on the companies’ pages if the **{bereich.directory}** section is switched on, and then only for roles with the “{bereich.directory}” permission. This way you switch the member lists on or off centrally and decide per role who sees them. You can assign people even if the section is off.
- {bereich.companies} and assignments are part of the backup.
