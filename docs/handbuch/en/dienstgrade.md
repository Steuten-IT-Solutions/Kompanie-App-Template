Here you see all {bereich.ranks} of {kompanie.name} in order, with their badges. If you also have {bereich.points}, each {begriff.rank} shows from how many {bereich.points} it can be reached. The page is for reference only.

![The list of all {bereich.ranks} with badges and thresholds](bild:dienstgrade)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** at the bottom and then the **{bereich.ranks}** button. **‹ Internal** takes you back.

## What you see here
- All {bereich.ranks} from lowest to highest, each with a badge if the people responsible have added one.
- Your own {begriff.rank} is marked (“Your {begriff.rank}”).
- With {bereich.points}: a short note at the top and the threshold on the right of each {begriff.rank}, e.g. “from 25”. {bereich.ranks} without a threshold have nothing on the right.
- If there are no {bereich.ranks} yet, it says “No {bereich.ranks} created yet.”

## Good to know
- The threshold only says from when a {begriff.rank} can be reached. Promotions don’t happen automatically; the people responsible make them.
- How far you are from the next {begriff.rank} and when yours changed is under **My area** → **{begriff.rank}**.
- You don’t see other members’ {bereich.ranks} here.
