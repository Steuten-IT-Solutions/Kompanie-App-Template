Here you add {bereich.penalties} for one or more members at once, e.g. for everyone who came late. At the top you see how much is still open in total.

![The form for recording with the total open amount](bild:strafen-verwalten)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then the **{bereich.penalties} +** button. **‹ Admin** takes you back.

## What you see here
- **Total outstanding: {bereich.penalties}**: how much is still open for everyone together (without guests).
- **Amount in €**: e.g. 2.50.
- **Date**: today is preselected.
- **Reason**: e.g. “Came late”.
- **Members**: all members to tick, with **Search members …**, **All** (or **None**) and below it how many are selected.
- **Record**: saves the entry for everyone ticked.

## Step by step
### Create an entry
1. Enter the **Amount in €**, e.g. 5.00.
2. Change the **Date** if it wasn’t today.
3. Enter the **Reason**.
4. Tick the members. With many names the search helps; **All** ticks everyone found.
5. Tap **Record**. At the bottom it briefly shows for how many members the amount was recorded.

![Filled-in form with amount, reason and one selected member](bild:strafen-verwalten-ausgefuellt)

### Take over a suggestion from an attendance list
Those who also record {bereich.attendance} can record {bereich.penalties} from an attendance list. This form then opens with a suggestion:
1. At the top is the way back “‹ …” with the name of the list. Everyone who was absent is already ticked.
2. The **Reason** is “Absent: name of the list”, the **Date** is that of the list. Enter the **Amount in €**.
3. Check the selection and tap **Record**. Afterwards you are back at the list.

Unlike with {bereich.points}, the list doesn’t remember who already has an entry. If you record twice, there are two entries.

### View all open entries
1. Tap the **Total outstanding** tile at the top (only tappable if something is open).
2. The list shows all open entries, sorted by member and with a total per member.
3. Tap **Paid** next to an entry once it is paid. A tap on the name opens the member’s {bereich.penalties}; there you also find **Still open** and **Cancel entry**.

## Good to know
- An entry can be at most €1,000. Amount and reason are required.
- {bereich.penalties} are only for members, not guests.
- Those who have switched on notifications get a message about the new entry.
- You mark an entry as paid in the list of all open entries or on the member’s page. You cancel wrong entries on the member’s page.
- The admin area’s member list shows for each member how much is still open.
- **Without internet** (or if the server is disrupted, doesn’t respond within 20 seconds or your login has expired): the app saves the entry on the device and sends it by itself as soon as the connection is back. If the server then rejects it, **Not sent** appears at the top of the app until you dismiss it. Until then the form says “1 booking not sent yet”. Nothing is recorded twice, even if only the response got lost on the way.
