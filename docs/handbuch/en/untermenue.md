A submenu groups several areas under one item in the bar. Which submenus exist, what they are called and what they contain is decided by an {rolle.admin} under **Settings → Navigation**.

## How to open this page
Tap the submenu’s item in the bar at the bottom.

## What you see here
One button for each area in the submenu. You only see the areas you are allowed to see.

## Step by step
1. Tap the button of the area you want to open.
2. **‹** with the submenu’s name at the top takes you back to this page.

## Good to know
- Where your buttons sit and how wide they are is up to you: [Arrange tiles](seite:kacheln-anordnen).
