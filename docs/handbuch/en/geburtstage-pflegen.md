titel: A member’s birthday
---
Every member enters their birthday themselves under **My area → Account**. Anyone with the permission **Maintain personal details** can also enter, change or remove the date for others, e.g. if someone doesn’t use the app or to take over an existing list.

![Birthday window on a member’s page](bild:geburtstage-pflegen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Members**, and tap the person. On their page, tap **Birthday** below the tiles; the **Birthday** window opens.

## What you see here
- **The entered date** or “Not entered.”.
- **Show to other members**, **Show age** and (with the **{bereich.directory}** list) whether the birthday is shown on the person’s page: for information only, because only the person can set this.
- **Day**, **Month** and **Year**: the date to change. The year is optional.
- **Save birthday** and **Remove**.

## Step by step
### Enter or change a birthday
1. Choose the **Day** and **Month**.
2. Enter the **Year** if you know it (four digits), otherwise leave the field empty.
3. Tap **Save birthday**.

### Remove a birthday
1. Tap **Remove**.
2. Confirm the question.

## Good to know
- Only real dates are allowed: 29 February only in a leap year (or without a year), years from 1900 and nothing in the future.
- A newly entered birthday is visible to other {rolle.member} accounts, without the age. On the person’s page under **{bereich.directory}**, logged-in {rolle.member} accounts see it until the person chooses otherwise.
- Birthdays are seen by those whose role has the permission, at first the {rolle.member} accounts. For people who don’t belong to the company yet (e.g. candidates) you can already enter one; it appears as soon as the person has been admitted as {rolle.member}.
- When creating new members, anyone allowed to create members can enter the birthday straight away. Afterwards only those allowed to **See personal details** see other people’s full date.
- When an account is deleted, the birthday is gone too.
