Here you read a whole post from the {bereich.news} of {kompanie.name}. If it comes from a website, you can also go there from here.

![Open post with date, title, text and the “Open on the website” button](bild:neuigkeiten-beitrag)

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.news}** at the bottom and then a post.

## What you see here
- **‹ All news**: back to the list of all posts.
- **Image, date and title** of the post, if the source provides them.
- **Text** of the post. You can tap links in it.
- **Open on the website**: shows the post in the browser on the website it comes from. Posts written in the app don’t have this button.
- **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (bin): only for posts from the app, and only with the right to do so (help page “Write {bereich.news}”).
- **Only available in German**: the post has no English version yet, so you see the German one.

## Good to know
- You can send the post as a link with the share icon at the top. Whoever opens it sees exactly this post.
- The app shows the text without the website’s design. If something looks different from the website, open the post with **Open on the website**.
