Under **{bereich.news}** you read the posts of {kompanie.name}, newest first. Anyone can read them, even without logging in.

![List of posts with date, title and a short preview](bild:neuigkeiten)

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.news}** at the bottom.

## What you see here
- **Post**: image (if there is one), date, title and a short preview of the text.
- **Older posts** (at the bottom): loads more, older posts. The button is missing once all posts are shown.
- **Notify me about new posts** (at the bottom, only when not logged in): takes you to the notifications.
- **New post** (at the top): only if you have the right to do so and the {bereich.news} are written in the app or, with WordPress, come from the website’s posts (help page “Write {bereich.news}”).

## Step by step
### Read a post
1. Tap the post. It opens in full, see [Read a post](seite:neuigkeiten-beitrag).
2. **‹ All news** takes you back to the list.

### Get notified about new posts
1. Below the list, tap **Notify me about new posts**.
2. Leave **{bereich.news}: new posts** ticked and tap **Switch on notifications**.

If you are logged in, you set this up under **My area** → **Account** → **Set up notifications**. More under [Notifications](seite:benachrichtigungen).

## Good to know
- The posts come from a source that an {rolle.admin} chooses: the website of {kompanie.name}, a feed or the app itself.
- If you use the app in English, you see the English version of a post where there is one; otherwise the German one with the note “Only available in German”.
- If there are no posts yet, the page says “No posts yet.”
