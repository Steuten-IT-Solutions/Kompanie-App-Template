The app of {kompanie.name} runs in the browser and can be put on your home screen like any other app, without an app store and free of charge. This page shows you step by step how to do that on your phone.

![“Get the app on your phone” instructions for the iPhone](bild:app-installieren)

## How to open this page
- In the **Get the app on your phone** note, tap **Show me how** at the top.
- With an account: tap **My area** at the bottom, choose **Account** in the menu at the top and tap **Install app**.

## What you see here
- **Install app** (at the top): only appears if your browser offers installation directly (e.g. Chrome on Android). One tap is enough.
- **iPhone / iPad** and **Android**: choose the matching instructions. The app preselects your device.
- **Steps with pictures**: the pictures show where to tap; the spot is circled in colour.
- **Share the instructions** (at the bottom): a link that opens exactly these instructions, with the **Share link** button.

## Step by step
### On the iPhone or iPad
1. Open the app’s address in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share icon** (a square with an arrow pointing up). On the iPhone it is usually in the bar at the bottom, on the iPad in the bar at the top. From iOS 26, Safari uses the **Compact** layout by default: then first tap **•••** in the address bar and then **Share**. You can change this under Settings → Apps → Safari → Tabs (**Compact**, **Bottom** or **Top**).
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. The entry is further down the list; swipe up until it appears.
4. Tap **Add** at the top right. If there is an “Open as Web App” switch, leave it on.
5. From now on, always start the app with the new icon with the logo. Your login is kept.

### On an Android phone
1. Open the app’s address in **Chrome**. Why Chrome: on Android, only Chrome creates a real app that is listed among your apps and shows notifications. Other browsers only create a shortcut (see below).
2. If the **Install app** button appears at the top, tap it and confirm. Done. Chrome only shows this button once the page has been open for about 30 seconds in total and you have tapped somewhere once; this is a Chrome rule and the app can’t shorten it. Until then a note is shown there, and you can also use the steps below straight away.
3. Otherwise the instructions under **Android** show the steps with pictures: in Chrome, tap the three dots **⋮** at the top right and choose **Install app** (depending on the phone, the entry may also be called “Add to Home screen”).
4. Confirm with **Install** or **Add**. If Chrome asks “Install” or “Create shortcut”, choose **Install**.

### Other browsers on Android
If you open the instructions in another browser, the app shows a note at the top explaining why, with the buttons **Open in Chrome**, **Download Chrome** and **Copy address**. If Chrome isn’t on the phone yet, first download it free of charge from the Play Store with **Download Chrome**, then tap **Open in Chrome** again. The page itself can’t tell whether Chrome is already installed, which is why both buttons are always there.
- **Samsung browser (Samsung Internet):** please don’t install it there. From Android 14, Google Play Protect blocks the app created by the Samsung browser as unsafe or “built for an older version of Android”. This is a bug in the Samsung browser, reported since April 2026 and not yet fixed. With Chrome it works on Samsung phones too.
- **Edge, Firefox, Opera, Brave and others:** they only create a shortcut, not a real app: it isn’t listed among your apps and opens via the browser. Notifications generally work in Firefox and Opera too; with the others it isn’t certain. If you still want to stay there: in Firefox **⋮ → Install → Add automatically**, in Opera **Menu → Add to … → Home screen**, otherwise **Add to Home screen** (or similar) in the browser’s menu.
- **Built-in browsers of other apps** (e.g. when you tap a link in WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram): they can’t install apps or show notifications. Copy the address and open it in Chrome.

### Not working?
- **No install entry:** if the app is already installed, Chrome doesn’t offer it again. Look in the list of your apps.
- **Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO:** there, a browser may only put icons on the home screen once you allow it. In the phone’s settings, switch on home screen shortcuts in Chrome’s permissions.
- **Huawei without Google services:** there is no Play Store there, so **Download Chrome** leads nowhere. If Chrome is installed anyway, it only creates a shortcut, and notifications may not arrive because they are delivered via Google.
- **Notifications don’t arrive:** some phones (e.g. Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei) close apps in the background to save battery. Exclude Chrome and the app from optimisation in the battery settings.
- **Android 8 or 9:** Chrome no longer gets new versions there (the last one is Chrome 138); the app may work with limitations.

### Send the instructions to others
1. Tap **Share link** at the bottom of the page.
2. Choose e.g. WhatsApp or email. On a computer, the app copies the link.

## Good to know
- From iOS 16.4, other browsers on the iPhone (e.g. Chrome, Edge, Firefox) can also offer **Add to Home Screen** in the share menu; whether the entry is there depends on the browser. The app shows a note in these browsers; if the entry is missing, copy the address and open it in Safari.
- If the address is open in another app’s built-in browser (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or similar), installation won’t work. The app then shows **Copy address**; open the address in Safari or Chrome.
- If the app gets a new logo, Chrome on Android picks up the new icon by itself after a while; for a bigger change, Chrome asks first. On the iPhone, the icon saved when you added it stays.
- If you are already using the installed app, this page says “All done”.
- What you loaded last stays visible even without internet. The top then says “Offline – showing the most recently loaded data.”
- On the iPhone and iPad, notifications only work in the installed app, see [Notifications](seite:benachrichtigungen).
