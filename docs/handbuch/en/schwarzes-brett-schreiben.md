Here you write an urgent message on the {bereich.board}, e.g. “No practice today”: internal, public for everyone, only for certain {bereich.companies} or only for individual people. Its title is shown as a banner at the top of every page of the app for as long as you want (24 hours at first). You format the text as in an email program, with your signature, file attachments and ready-made blocks such as a **Countdown** if you like. You also change or delete messages here and see who changed what.

![The {bereich.board} page with the New message form](bild:schwarzes-brett-schreiben)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **{bereich.board}**. If this is your only task in the admin area, **Admin** opens this page straight away. If a message is already on the board, tapping its title in the banner also brings you here.

## What you see here
- **‹ Admin**: back to the admin start page (only if you have several tasks there).
- **New message**: the **Title** field and below it the **Content** made of blocks: first a text block with the toolbar for formatting, more blocks with **+ Insert block**. Then **Attachments** with **Attach file**, the **Visible to** choice, the **Visible until** field, the **Send notification** and **Send email** ticks and the **Publish** button.
- **Current messages**: all messages currently on the board, public ones with the note **public**, those for {bereich.companies} or people with **only for …** and the names, with **Visible until** and the round **Edit** (pencil), **Delete** (bin) and **History** (clock) icons. Below it is who last changed the message and when, and how far sending the emails has got.
- **History: expired and deleted messages**: messages of the last 30 days that have expired or that someone deleted, with their history.
- **Running {bereich.polls}**: as for all {rolle.member} accounts, if {kompanie.name} uses {bereich.polls}; below it **Closed {bereich.polls}** and, if you may manage {bereich.polls}, **Manage {bereich.polls}**.

## The toolbar
With the bar above the text you format the message as in an email program. First select the text you want to change.

![The text field with the toolbar](bild:schwarzes-brett-werkzeuge)

- **Font** and **Size**: choice at the top left.
- **B**, **I**, **U**, **S**: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough.
- **A** and **ab**: text colour and background colour (for highlighting). A tap opens the colour choice.
- **⌫**: clear the formatting of the selected text.
- **⇤ ↔ ⇥ ≡**: align left, centre, align right, justify.
- **•** and **1.**: bulleted and numbered list.
- **⇠** and **⇢**: decrease and increase indent. In a list this indents or outdents the item.
- **🔗**: link. Addresses with https://, email addresses and phone numbers are allowed.
- **🖼**: insert a picture (JPG, PNG or WebP). You can also drag or paste pictures in. You change the size at the corners of the picture.
- **―**: divider.
- **▦**: insert a table. When the cursor is in a table, buttons for rows and columns appear.
- **↶** and **↷**: undo and redo.
- **Insert signature**: adds your signature at the end. If you don’t have one yet, it says **No signature yet – set one up now**.

## Step by step
### Publish a message
1. Under **New message**, enter a **Title**. It appears in the banner, in the notification and as the subject of the email.
2. Below, under **Content**, write the text and format it with the toolbar. It shows the common tools (bold, italic, underline, text colour, lists, link, picture, undo); behind **⋯** you find font, size, strikethrough, background colour, clear formatting, alignment, indent, divider and table. You add more blocks with **+ Insert block** (at the end) or **+ Insert block below**, e.g. a **Countdown** (see below), move them with **↑** and **↓** and take them out with **Remove**.
3. If you want to send files along, tap **Attach file** under **Attachments** and choose one or more files. Any type of file is allowed; **×** takes one out again.
4. In the **Visibility and delivery** section, choose under **Visible to** who sees the message:
   - **Internal** (default): {rolle.member} accounts and everyone with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates).
   - **Public**: everyone, including visitors who aren’t logged in.
   - **Company**: only those who read the board and are assigned to one of the selected {bereich.companies}. Tick one or more {bereich.companies} below. This choice only exists if {kompanie.name} uses the {bereich.companies} section and {bereich.companies} have been created there; whoever manages {bereich.companies} decides who belongs to which.
   - **Individual**: only the selected people. Tick one or more people below; with many names the **Search …** field helps. Everyone who reads the board can be chosen.
5. Under **Visible until**, choose until when the message should stay. The default is 24 hours from now.
6. Leave **Send notification** ticked if everyone should be told straight away. Who that is depends on **Visible to**; the text below the ticks tells you.
7. Leave **Send email** ticked if everyone should also get the message by email. Next to it is how many people.
8. Tap **Publish**.
9. In the confirmation, check the title, visibility, end, delivery and attachments and confirm. The message is on the board straight away.

![A new message in the form, ready to publish](bild:schwarzes-brett-schreiben-neu)

### Insert more blocks
- **Button with link**: enter a **Label** and a **Target**: a website with https://, an email address with mailto:, a phone number with tel: or a page of the app such as #/termine. Under **Style** choose **Highlighted** or **Subtle**.
- **Upcoming events**: choose a **Heading** and **How many events** (1 to 20). The block always shows the next events from today from {bereich.events}.
- **Contacts**: under **Offices**, tick the offices whose holders should appear. Email address and mobile number are only seen by people allowed to see personal details.
- **Notice box**: a short **Note**, coloured as **Info**, **Warning** or **Success**.
- **Divider**: a **Line** or **Space only** between two blocks.
- **Schedule**: one **Time** and one **Programme item** per row, e.g. for the schedule of a festival.
- **Facts and figures**: one **Value** and one **Name** per row, large side by side.
- **Poll**: under **Poll**, choose one of the {bereich.polls}. The block shows title and status with **Take part**; those who have already voted see **View answer and result**. Those who may not take part in polls don’t see the block.
- **Album**: under **Album**, choose one from the {bereich.gallery} and **How many photos** (1 to 12). Everyone only sees the pictures they may also see in the {bereich.gallery}.
- **Document**: under **Document**, choose a file from the {bereich.documents}. The block shows it with **Open** (PDF and images) and **Download**, but only for those who may see it in the {bereich.documents}.
- **{bereich.sponsors}**: shows all {bereich.sponsors} with logo and link, optionally with a **Heading**.
- **Map for directions**: enter a **Place or address**. The block shows the place with the **Map** button, which opens it in the device’s maps app, as for an event’s location.

### Insert a countdown
1. Tap **+ Insert block** and choose **Countdown** in the window.
2. Enter a **Heading** if needed and, under **Counts down to**, choose a **Date** (with **Time**) or an **Event** from the {bereich.events}. A moved event is followed automatically.
3. Optional: an end (**Ends on**, **Ends at**) with a **Text while it is running**, and under **Afterwards** a **Text afterwards** or **Hide block**.
4. The **Preview** shows what the countdown looks like right now. It always shows two values: days and hours, on the last day hours and minutes, in the last hour minutes and seconds. It counts by the calendar in your app’s time zone: if a clock change falls in between, it shows the remaining time as on the clock, e.g. from 9:47 pm to midnight always 2 hours 13 minutes.

In the email a button appears as “Label: target”, the upcoming events as a list and the countdown as a sentence, e.g. “Countdown to 10.03.2027, 18:00”, as it looks at the time of sending.

### Insert your signature
1. While writing, tap **Insert signature** in a text block. It then appears at the end of this text.
2. You can then change it like the rest of the text; your saved signature is not affected.

If you don’t have a signature yet, tap **No signature yet – set one up now**. The app opens the signature in your account. What you have already written is kept: after saving the signature, **‹ Back to the message** brings you back here. How to set up the signature is described on the [Signature](seite:signatur) page.

### Take over text from Word or Outlook
1. Select the text in Word or Outlook and copy it.
2. Paste it into a text block under **Content**.

Font, colours, bullet points and tables come along. The app takes over pictures where possible. If this doesn’t work for a picture, it tells you; then insert it separately with **🖼**. Colours that would be hard to read on the background (e.g. dark text in the dark design) are shown in the app’s normal text colour; very large line spacing is shown as normal. The text stays stored as you entered it.

### Change a message
1. Tap **Edit** on the message.
2. Change the **Title**, **Content** (texts and blocks), **Attachments**, **Visible to** (including the choice of {bereich.companies} or people) or **Visible until**. You can extend or shorten the end; you can add attachments or remove them with **×**.
3. Tap **Save**. With **Cancel** everything stays as it was.

![A message being edited](bild:schwarzes-brett-schreiben-bearbeiten)

### Also write a message in English
If the admin has switched on English under **Settings → Languages**, the expandable section **Write in English (optional)** appears below the **Content**.

1. Tap **Write in English (optional)**.
2. Under **Title in English**, enter the English title. The German one is shown in grey in the field.
3. Below it, each block with text appears again, with only its texts. Write them in English. For the text block, **German text** expands to show what it says in German.
4. Publish or save the message as usual.

Those using the app in English see the English version and get the notification and email in English. Anything you leave empty appears in German with the note *Partly only available in German*. You can change the English version later when editing, in the same section. The notification and email are only sent when publishing, though, so it’s best to write the English version straight away.

### See who changed what
1. Tap **History** on the message.
2. You see every change with time and name. For title, content, attachments, visibility and end, the old and new versions are shown side by side (**before**, **after**). Blocks such as a countdown appear there as a sentence, members-only blocks with **Members only:** in front.
3. Under **Email delivery per recipient** you see who the email went to and whether it worked.

![The history of a message with before and after](bild:schwarzes-brett-verlauf)

### Delete a message
1. Tap **Delete** on the message.
2. Confirm the question. The message disappears from the board straight away. Emails not yet sent are no longer sent.

It stays under **History: expired and deleted messages** for another 30 days, with the name and time of deletion.

## Good to know
- With **Send notification**, all {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates) get a notification with the title, including you. This way you see how the notification arrives. This applies to everyone who has switched on the type **{bereich.board}: urgent messages** on their device.
- For a **public** message, all other devices on which the type **{bereich.board}: public messages** is switched on also get a notification, e.g. visitors who aren’t logged in and have switched on notifications for events or {bereich.news}. With the first public message, the app switches this type on once on such devices; after that each device decides for itself. Each device gets the message only once.
- With **Company** and **Individual**, only the selected people or the people of the selected {bereich.companies} get the notification and the email, plus you. The number next to **Send email** adjusts to the choice.
- Messages for {bereich.companies} or individual people are also seen by everyone allowed to write on the board, because they may change and delete every message. Anyone reading a message for {bereich.companies} sees which ones it is for; anyone reading a message for individual people only sees **only for you**, not who else gets it.
- For each block you choose **For everyone who sees the message** or **Members only**. Members-only blocks and their pictures are missing from a public message without logging in and for guests; the email contains all blocks, because it only goes to {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar.
- Public means: title, text, pictures, attachments, times and your name are visible to everyone, even without logging in (except members-only blocks). Who changed it last, the email delivery and the history stay with people who have this permission or the permission **See {bereich.board} history**. Don’t write personal details such as phone numbers or addresses in public messages.
- **Send email** exists if an {rolle.admin} has set up sending emails. The email goes to all {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (even for public messages only to them) who have an email address and haven’t switched off emails from the board, including you. This way you see how the email arrives. Each person gets their own email; nobody sees the others’ addresses.
- Everyone who sees the message sees and downloads the attachments. With **Send email** they are also attached to the email. A file may be at most 20 MB (less on some servers; the app then says so).
- If the email with its pictures and attachments is larger than the mail server accepts, the app says so before publishing. Then make pictures or attachments smaller or untick **Send email**.
- Changing sends no new notification and no new email, even if you make an internal message public or change the choice of {bereich.companies} or people. If the title or content changes, those who had hidden the message see it in the banner again. If only the end or the visibility changes, it stays hidden.
- Everyone with this permission may change and delete messages, not just whoever wrote them. Only people with this permission or the permission **See {bereich.board} history** see the history.
- There is no length limit for the title; the texts of all blocks together may be up to about 1 MB long (far more than any message). The banner shortens very long titles; everything is stored. Only the server sets limits, e.g. for very large pictures; the app then tells you which.
- Use the {bereich.board} for urgent matters.

- Layout, pictures, attachments, **Visible to** and **Visible until** apply to all languages. Only the texts exist per language. Even if you use the app in English yourself, you edit the German version; the English one is in the section below.
- In the **History**, every change to the English version appears as **Translation changed**.
