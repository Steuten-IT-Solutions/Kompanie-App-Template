The app can notify you on your phone or computer when there is something new, even when it is closed. On this page you switch this on and choose what you get notified about. With an account, you also get the same notifications by email if {kompanie.name} has set up sending emails (see [By email](#by-email)).

![Notifications with the list of types, all ticked](bild:benachrichtigungen)

## How to open this page
- With an account: tap **My area** at the bottom, choose **Account** in the menu at the top and tap **Set up notifications**. If your device doesn’t have notifications yet, the **Never miss a thing** window also opens after you log in (see below).
- Without an account: under **{bereich.events}**, tap **Notify me about new events**, or under **{bereich.news}**, tap **Notify me about new posts**.

## What you see here
- **Types**: one row with a tick for each type of message, e.g. **{bereich.events}: new and changed**. You only see the types that exist for you (see below).
- **{bereich.penalties}: show amount and reason in the message** (only with an account, if there are {bereich.penalties}): without the tick, the message only contains a note, which is good if others can see your lock screen.
- **Switch on notifications**: switches on notifications on this device.
- **Send test message** and **Switch off notifications**: appear as soon as notifications are on.
- **By email** (only with an account, if sending emails is set up): a second card with a tick for each type for emails to your address.

## Step by step
### Switch on notifications
1. Open the page as described above.
2. All types are ticked. Untick the ones you don’t want.
3. Tap **Switch on notifications**.
4. Your browser asks whether the site may send notifications. Tap **Allow**.
5. “Notifications are on” appears. **Send test message** checks whether everything arrives.

![Notifications without an account: events, reminder and news](bild:benachrichtigungen-gast)

### Switch on via the window after logging in
After you log in, the **Never miss a thing** window opens, whichever page you land on, as long as your browser hasn’t asked about notifications on this device yet.
1. Tap **Switch on notifications**.
2. Your browser asks whether the site may send notifications. Tap **Allow**.
3. “Notifications are on” appears. All types that exist for you are switched on. You can change them as described below.

After some updates, the window also asks everyone who has no notifications on this device once more, even if you chose **Not now** earlier or switched them off. If you have blocked them in the browser, it doesn’t ask.

**Not now** or **✕** hides the window on this device for 30 days. If you chose **Block** in the browser, the app doesn’t ask again; you can then only allow them in the browser’s settings. Notifications can’t be switched on without your consent in the browser; {kompanie.name} can’t do this for you either.

### Change the types
1. Open the page.
2. Tick or untick the boxes. The change applies immediately, without another button.

At least one type stays ticked. To switch off completely, use **Switch off notifications**.

### Switch off notifications
1. Open the page.
2. Tap **Switch off notifications**. “Notifications are off” appears.

## By email
With an account, every notification also arrives as an email at your stored address if {kompanie.name} has set up sending emails. This applies to your account, not to a device: the emails arrive even if you haven’t switched on notifications on any device.
- At first all types are ticked, including types that become available to you later.
- On the **By email** card, untick the types you don’t want by email. The change applies straight away; “By email: off” or “By email: on” appears. Here you may also untick everything.
- The types are the same as on the device (see below). For {rolle.member} and people with the internal calendar there is also **Messages from “{bereich.board}” by email**: this email contains the whole message with pictures and attachments. The {bereich.board} notifications themselves don’t come by email a second time.
- The email contains the title and text of the notification and a link into the app. For {bereich.penalties} it only contains a note, without amount and reason.
- Without a stored email address no email arrives; the card then points this out. You see and change your address under **My area → Account**.

## Which types there are
Which types you see depends on which areas {kompanie.name} uses and whether you are logged in.

For everyone, even without an account:
- **{bereich.events}: new and changed**: when an event is added or its start, end or location changes. For a change, the message only says what has changed, e.g. “Time: 19:00 → 20:00”. If many new events arrive at once, there is one summary message.
- **Reminder the day before**: one message with all of tomorrow’s events, from the time an {rolle.admin} sets (usually 6 pm). Events you have declined aren’t included.
- **{bereich.news}: new posts**: when a new post appears.

You get these messages for the events you see under **{bereich.events}**: without an account for the public events, as a {rolle.member} or with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates) for the calendar of {kompanie.name}, if it uses one.

For everyone who doesn’t otherwise see the {bereich.board} (e.g. without an account):
- **{bereich.board}: public messages**: as soon as someone writes a public message on the {bereich.board} (unless they switch off the notification when doing so).

For {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates):
- **{bereich.board}: urgent messages**: as soon as someone writes a message on the {bereich.board}, internal or public (unless they switch off the notification when doing so). You only get messages meant for particular {bereich.companies} or people if they are meant for you.

Only for {rolle.member} accounts:
- **{bereich.events}: reminder to accept or decline**: if you haven’t yet accepted or declined an event in the internal calendar, at the reminder time a few days before (usually 3), or the day before a registration deadline. Only if you are allowed to accept and decline and an {rolle.admin} has switched on accepting and declining including the reminder.
- **{bereich.polls}: new poll and reminder**: for every new poll and 24 hours before it ends, if you haven’t answered yet.
- **{bereich.birthdays}: on the day itself**: on the birthday of another member who shows their birthday to others, from the time an {rolle.admin} sets (usually 7 am).
- **Passive members: birthdays at the chosen ages**: when a passive member will soon reach one of the ages set under **Admin → Passive members**, the number of days before set there (usually 7), from the same time as the birthdays. Only if you are allowed to manage passive members and see personal details.
- **{bereich.penalties}: new entries**: for a new entry for you.
- **{bereich.points}: new entries**: when you get {bereich.points} or they are deducted, with value and reason.
- **{begriff.rank}: promotion or change**: when your {begriff.rank} changes.
- **{bereich.titles}: newly awarded**: when you are awarded a title.

{bereich.points}, {bereich.penalties}, {begriff.rank} and {bereich.titles} are always only about your own entries.

## On the iPhone and iPad
There, notifications only work in the installed app (from iOS 16.4), not in Safari. If you open the page in the browser, you only see a note and the **Install app** button. If you are logged in, the **Never miss a thing** window also opens after you log in, with the **Install app** button; **Not now** hides it for 30 days. First get the app onto your home screen, see [Get the app on your phone](seite:app-installieren), open it from there and then switch on notifications.

## Good to know
- If you tap a message, the app opens exactly the entry it is about: for an event, the list under **{bereich.events}** scrolls to it, expands it and briefly highlights it. The same goes for messages on the {bereich.board} and for your {bereich.points} and {bereich.penalties}. If the entry no longer exists, you see the list.
- The setting only applies to the device on which you make it. On other devices you switch it on there. The ticks under **By email**, on the other hand, apply to your account.
- With one account, at most 10 devices get notifications. If you switch them on on another device, the one set up longest ago drops out.
- If you log out in the app, it switches off your account’s notifications on this device. If the login ends some other way (e.g. it expired), this happens as soon as the app is opened on the device again.
- If a new type becomes available for you, e.g. because {kompanie.name} switches on an area or you get a new role, it is added automatically on your devices with notifications (within 15 minutes at the latest, immediately when you open this page). Types you have unticked yourself stay off, even if an area is switched off and on again. **{bereich.board}: public messages** is added with the first public message.
- Notifications switched on without an account end by themselves if the app hasn’t been opened on the device for half a year and no message has arrived.
- Messages come in the language you have chosen in the app.

## If nothing arrives
- If it says “Notifications are blocked for this site”, you have declined the browser’s question. Allow notifications in the browser’s settings (or the phone’s, under notifications) and reload the app. In Chrome on Android: tap the icon to the left of the address → **Permissions** → **Notifications** → **Allow** (or **⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Notifications**). For the installed app, also in the phone’s settings under **Apps** → the app → **Notifications**.
- If you open the page in another app’s built-in browser (e.g. after a link in WhatsApp or Facebook), there are no notifications: such browsers can’t show them. Open the address in Chrome or Safari.
- Notifications don’t arrive on Android even though everything is switched on: some phones close apps to save battery. Exclude Chrome and the app from optimisation in the battery settings.
- Power saving mode and “Do not disturb” sometimes hold messages back.
- If it says “This app’s server can’t send notifications yet”, please let an {rolle.admin} know.
- If it says “This browser doesn’t support notifications”, try an up-to-date Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.
