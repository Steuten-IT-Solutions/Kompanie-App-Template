Here you see how much you still have to pay in {bereich.penalties} and which entries have already been paid. The page is for viewing only; other members don’t see your entries.

![My {bereich.penalties} with the outstanding amount and the Open and Paid lists](bild:meine-strafen)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom, then the menu at the top, and choose **{bereich.penalties}**. Or tap the **Open** tile in the overview.

## What you see here
- **Outstanding: {bereich.penalties}**: the amount still outstanding.
- **Open**: entries you still have to pay.
- **Paid**: entries that have already been paid.
- Each entry shows the reason, the date, who recorded it, an “open” or “paid” label and the amount on the right. The newest are at the top.
- Without entries it says “No entries – exemplary!”

## Good to know
- You can’t change anything here yourself. As soon as the money has arrived, the people responsible mark the entry as paid. If an entry is cancelled, it disappears completely.
- The app can send you a message for every new entry. You switch this on or off under **My area** → **Account** → **Set up notifications** with **{bereich.penalties}: new entries**.
- The amount and reason are only in the message if you also switch on **{bereich.penalties}: show amount and reason in the message** there. Otherwise you only get the note “Tap to view.”
