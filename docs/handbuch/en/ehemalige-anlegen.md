Anyone who left before the app existed (e.g. from the chronicle) has no account yet. Here you add such a person one at a time. An account without login is created, already entered as a former member.

![The form for a former member, with the name filled in](bild:ehemalige-anlegen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Former members** and **+ Add former member**. **‹ Former members** takes you back.

## What you see here
- **Import table (e.g. chronicle)**: take over many former members at once (see [Former members](seite:ehemalige-mitglieder)).
- **First name** and **Surname**.
- **Reason for leaving**, **Left on** (for deceased members **Died on**), **Joining date** and **Founding member**.
- **Birthday (optional)**, if your company uses {bereich.birthdays} and you may create members or maintain personal details, and **Photo (optional)**.
- The **Add former member** button.

## Step by step
### Add a former member
1. Enter the **First name** and **Surname**.
2. Choose the **Reason for leaving**.
3. If known, enter **Left on** (for deceased members **Died on**) and the **Joining date**, each as DD.MM.YYYY or just the year.
4. Tick **Founding member** if it applies.
5. If you like, enter the birthday and choose a photo.
6. Tap **Add former member**.
7. Afterwards **Done** takes you to the list of former members; **Another person** clears the form for the next one.

## Good to know
- The person can’t log in; there is no starting password.
- If there is already an account with exactly this first name and surname (upper or lower case doesn’t matter), the app creates nobody and says so. Make people with the same name distinguishable, e.g. with “I” and “II” after the surname.
- If the person has so far appeared in the {bereich.offices} only by name, the entry is linked to their account.
