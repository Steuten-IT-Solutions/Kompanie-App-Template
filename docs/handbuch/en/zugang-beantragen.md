You belong to {kompanie.name} and don’t have access to the app yet? Then you can request it yourself here, if {kompanie.name} has switched this on. Your request waits until someone from {kompanie.name} approves it. Only then is your account created, and you log in with the username and password from your request.

## How to open this page
On the login page, tap **Request access** below **Log in**. When you aren’t logged in, the link is also on the app’s first page (“You’re one of us but don’t have access yet?”). Often {kompanie.name} also sends it to you as a link. If the link is missing, access can’t be requested in this app at the moment.

## What you see here
- **First name** and **Surname**: both are required.
- **Email address**: your address.
- **Username**: the app suggests one based on your name (e.g. “max.mueller”). You can change it; letters (a–z), digits and . _ - @ are allowed.
- **Password (at least 8 characters)** and **Repeat password**: your password for the app.
- **Your company**: only if {kompanie.name} uses the {bereich.companies} area. You must choose here: your company (several are possible), **No company** or **Passive**. Nothing is preselected. That way nobody has to ask you.
- **Message to the admins (optional)**: e.g. who you are or who knows you. This helps with the approval.
- A tick confirming that you have read the **Privacy policy**.
- **Send request**: sends the request.
- **Status of my request**: shows whether your request is still waiting.

## Step by step
### Request access
1. Enter your **First name**, **Surname** and **Email address**.
2. Check the suggested **Username** and change it if you want.
3. Choose a **Password** and repeat it.
4. If the choice is there, choose your company, **No company** or **Passive** under **Your company**.
5. If you like, write a short **Message to the admins**.
6. Tick the privacy policy box and tap **Send request**.

What happens next depends on whether {kompanie.name} has set up sending emails:

- **With emails** it says “Please confirm your email address”. You get an email with a link. Tap it within 24 hours; only then does the request go for approval. You get another email when it is approved or declined.
- **Without emails** it says “Thank you, your request has arrived”. You see whether it has been approved when you log in, or under **Status of my request**.

### Check the status of your request
1. Tap **Status of my request**.
2. Enter the **Username** (or the email address) and the **Password** from your request.
3. Tap **Check status**. The app shows whether the request is still waiting, has been declined (with a reason, if there is one) or whether there is no open request. In the last case it has usually been approved; just log in.

## Good to know
- If you log in before the request has been approved, the app tells you that it is still waiting (or that it has been declined).
- The app deletes a request that nobody deals with for 30 days. You can then make a new one.
- Only your details from the form are stored, your password only in encrypted form. If the request is declined or expires, the app deletes it.
- Whoever approves it sees the company you chose and assigns you to it with the approval. Anyone allowed to manage companies can still change the choice at that point.
- Whoever approves it can still change your username, e.g. if it has been taken in the meantime. With emails, the username is then in the email; you can always log in with your email address as well.
- The app won’t accept too many requests in a short time. Then try again later.
