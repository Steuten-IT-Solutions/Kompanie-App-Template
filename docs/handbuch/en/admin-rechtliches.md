Here you enter the links to your company’s legal notice (Impressum) and privacy policy. The app then shows them to everyone, even without logging in.

![The Legal notice and privacy page with the two links](bild:admin-rechtliches)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and **Legal notice and privacy**. The [setup assistant](seite:admin-einrichtung) has its own step for this.

## What you see here
- **Legal notice**: the address of your legal notice, e.g. `https://www.beispiel.de/impressum`.
- **Privacy policy**: the address of your privacy policy, e.g. `https://www.beispiel.de/datenschutz`.
- **Save**: applies the entries.

## Step by step
### Enter the links
1. In a second window, open the pages with your legal notice and privacy policy, e.g. on your website, and copy each address from the address bar.
2. Paste them into **Legal notice** and **Privacy policy**.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Remove a link
1. Empty the field and tap **Save**.

## Good to know
- The links are on the login page, at the bottom of the account and at the bottom of the help. They open in a new window.
- As long as one of the links is missing, the **Legal notice and privacy** row under [Status and updates](seite:admin-zustand) is yellow.
- The app doesn’t write a legal notice or a privacy policy for you. What they must contain is for your company to clarify, ideally with a lawyer or a data protection adviser. This help is not legal advice.

### What data the app processes
For your privacy policy: depending on which sections you have switched on, the app processes among other things
- the members’ accounts (name, username, role) and, where entered, email address, mobile number, address, birthday and photo,
- points, penalties and attendance per person,
- login cookies so that people stay logged in,
- pictures from Google Drive (copies on your server) and events from your calendar,
- answers in polls (stored separately from the person for anonymous polls, but not anonymised within the meaning of the GDPR),
- per person, when they used which section (see [Usage](seite:admin-nutzung)); this can be switched off,
- status reports to the app’s maintainers if you have agreed (without names and content), bug reports that someone sends themselves via **Report a bug** (first to your admins, to the maintainers only when an admin forwards them),
- your hosting provider’s server logs (e.g. IP addresses); the app has no influence on these.
