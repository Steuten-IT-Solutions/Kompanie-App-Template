The overview shows your own details at a glance: {begriff.rank}, {bereich.points}, open amounts, {bereich.titles}, birthday, joining date and {bereich.offices}. You only look things up here; a tap takes you to the details.

![The overview under My area with badge, tiles and titles](bild:meins-uebersicht)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom. The overview is the first page. If the menu at the top shows something else, tap the menu and choose **Overview**.

## What you see here
- **Menu at the top** (three lines): switches between **Overview**, your {bereich.points}, your {bereich.penalties}, your {begriff.rank}, your {bereich.attendance}, **My passive members** (if you look after passive members) and **Account**. It only lists the sections you use.
- **Your name**, below it the badge with your {begriff.rank} (if you use {bereich.ranks}) and your photo, if one has been added.
- **Role** or **Roles**: which role(s) you have in the app.
- **{bereich.points} tile**: your total of {bereich.points}.
- **Open tile**: how much is still open for {bereich.penalties}.
- **{bereich.titles} tile**: your honorary titles and offices, otherwise “None”.
- **Birthday** and **Joining date**: what you entered, otherwise “Not entered”.
- **{bereich.offices}**: your offices as a timeline, otherwise “You have no {bereich.offices} entered.”

Tiles and rows only appear if you use the section.

![The opened menu under My area](bild:meins-uebersicht-menue)

## Step by step
### View details
1. Tap the **{bereich.points}** or **Open** tile to see all entries.
2. Tap the badge to see your {begriff.rank} with its history.
3. Tap your photo to enlarge it.

### Change birthday or joining date
1. Tap the **Birthday** (or **Joining date**) row. The **Account** page opens.
2. Change the detail there and save.

You can only change the joining date if you use the {bereich.directory}; otherwise the row leads nowhere.

## Good to know
- Only members see this page. Without membership, **My area** only shows your account.
- Photo, name, role and {bereich.titles} are maintained by the people responsible in the admin area, not by you.
- You can have several {bereich.titles}. They don’t depend on your {begriff.rank} or your {bereich.points}. If a title belongs to an office, you hold it as long as the office runs.
- When you receive a new title, the app can send you a message. You switch this on under **Account** → **Set up notifications** with **{bereich.titles}: newly awarded**.
