On a member’s page you open a window with their {bereich.penalties}. There you mark entries as paid, cancel wrong ones and add new ones for this person only.

![The {bereich.penalties} window on a member’s page](bild:mitglied-strafen)

## How to open this page
1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Members**.
2. Tap the member’s name.
3. Tap the **Open** tile. The window opens.

**✕** or the back key closes it again.

## What you see here
- At the top the form as under **{bereich.penalties} +**, but without choosing members and without the total: **Amount in €**, **Date**, **Reason** and **Record**.
- **Entries**: all of the member’s {bereich.penalties} with reason, date, by whom, “open” or “paid” and the amount. The newest are at the top.
- For each entry **Paid** (or **Still open**) and **Cancel entry**.

## Step by step
### Mark an entry as paid
1. Tap **Paid** next to the entry as soon as the money is in.
2. Marked by mistake? Tap **Still open**.

### Cancel an entry
1. Tap **Cancel entry** next to the entry.
2. Confirm “Really cancel the entry …?”.

### Add an entry for this person
1. Enter **Amount in €** and **Reason**, and change the **Date** if needed.
2. Tap **Record**.

## Good to know
- A cancelled entry no longer counts and disappears from the lists, including for the member.
- The **Open** tile shows how much is still open.
- For former members (the admin area’s **Former members** list, only for those who may create members) and for people who are no longer members, the form is missing. You can still mark open amounts as paid or cancel them there.
- **View only:** with the permission **See {bereich.penalties} of all members** but without **Issue {bereich.penalties}**, the window only shows the entries, without the form and without **Paid** or **Cancel entry**.
