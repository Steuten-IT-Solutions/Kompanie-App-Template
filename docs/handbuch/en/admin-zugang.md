---
titel: Set up access requests
---
Here you decide whether people can request access to the app themselves. Then you don’t have to create every account yourselves: the person fills in a form, and someone allowed to create members approves the request (see [Requests for access](seite:anfragen-freigeben)). Only then is the account created. This is off until you switch it on.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and, under **Members and access**, **Roles**. The **Request access** section is below the roles. Older links to this page lead there.

## What you see here
- **People can request access themselves**: switches the form on or off.
- **Suggested role when approving**: only if there is more than one role for new accounts (see [Roles](seite:admin-rollen-und-mitglieder)). It is preselected when approving and can be changed there.
- A note on whether sending emails is set up and what this means for the requests.
- **Link to the form (to share)** with **Copy link**: only if the form is switched on.

## Step by step
### Switch on access requests
1. Tick **People can request access themselves**. The change applies straight away.
2. If the item exists, choose the **Suggested role when approving**.
3. Tap **Copy link** and share the link, e.g. in your group chat. The form can also be found via **Request access** on the login page and on the app’s first page.

## Good to know
- **With email sending** (see [Set up email](seite:admin-email)), the person first confirms their email address via a link and learns of approval or rejection by email. The form doesn’t reveal whether an account already exists for an address.
- **Without email sending**, the email address is not checked. The person learns the result when they log in or check the status of their request. If an account already exists for the address, the form says so.
- If you switch the form off, open requests can still be handled under **Admin → Requests**.
- The form has built-in protection against programs that fill in forms; in addition, the app only accepts a limited number of requests per hour and day.
- When you switch it on for the first time, devices of people allowed to create members who already have notifications get the type **Access requested: new requests** once.
- If the app runs via WordPress, approving creates a user of the website with the password from the request.
