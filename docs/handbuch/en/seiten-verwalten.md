Here you create your own pages, for example history, festival programme or directions. A page is made of blocks: formatted text with pictures and ready-made blocks such as a **Countdown**, a **Button with link** or the **Upcoming events**. You decide who each page is for and in which order the pages appear. Each page is its own item in the navigation.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Setup** and the **{bereich.pages}** button. **‹ Setup** takes you back. On every page, the **Manage {bereich.pages}** button also brings you here.

## What you see here
- All pages in their order with the title, for members-only pages with the note **members only**, and below it when and by whom they were last changed.
- On the left the **handle** (six dots) for moving.
- On each page the round **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (bin) icons.
- At the bottom the **New page** form with **Title**, **For whom?**, the **Content** made of blocks and **Create**.

## Step by step
### Create a page
1. Under **New page**, enter the **Title**.
2. Under **For whom?**, choose **Members only** or **Everyone, even without logging in**.
3. Under **Content**, write the text in the first block. With the buttons above the field you format it (e.g. **Bold**, **Bulleted list**, **Link**, **Insert table**). With **Insert image** you insert a picture.
4. You add more blocks with **+ Insert block** (at the end) or **+ Insert block below** (below a block), see below.
5. Tap **Create**. The page is then at the very bottom of the list and appears as its own item in the navigation.

### Edit a page
1. Tap **Edit** on the page.
2. Change the **Title**, **For whom?** or the blocks under **Content**.
3. Tap **Save** (or **Cancel**).

If someone else saved the page while you were editing it, the app doesn’t save your version and says who changed it. Then copy your changes, tap **Reload** and enter them again. Reloading discards your changes.

### Insert, move and remove blocks
- Tap **+ Insert block**. The window lists each type of block with an icon and a short explanation; tap the one you want: **Text**, **Countdown**, **Button with link**, **Upcoming events**, **Contacts**, **Notice box**, **Divider**, **Schedule**, **Facts and figures**, **Poll**, **Album**, **Document**, **{bereich.sponsors}** or **Map for directions**. With **+ Insert block below** the new block goes directly below an existing one.
- With **↑** and **↓** you move a block up or down, with **Remove** you take it out.
- For each block you choose who it is for: **For everyone who sees the page** or **Members only**. This way a public page can contain individual parts only for members, for example an internal meeting point. Without logging in and for guests, these blocks and their pictures are missing.
- Changes only take effect with **Save** (or **Create**).

### Set up a countdown
A countdown counts down to a point in time and always shows two values: more than 24 hours before, days and hours; on the last day, hours and minutes; in the last hour, minutes and seconds.
1. Insert a **Countdown** block.
2. If needed, enter a **Heading**, for example “Until the festival”.
3. Under **Counts down to**, either choose **Date** and enter the **Date** and **Time**, or **Event** and choose an event from the {bereich.events}. If an event is moved, the countdown follows automatically.
4. Optional: give an end (**Ends on**, **Ends at**; for an event it comes from the event) and a **Text while it is running**, for example “The festival is on!”. It is shown between start and end instead of the numbers.
5. Under **Afterwards**, choose whether the **Text afterwards** appears after the end (**Show text**) or the block disappears (**Hide block**).
6. Under **Preview** you see what the countdown looks like right now. Then tap **Save**.

### Insert a button with link
1. Insert a **Button with link** block.
2. Enter the **Label**, for example “Register now” (at most 60 characters).
3. Under **Target**, enter where the button leads: a website with https://, an email address with mailto: (e.g. mailto:board@example.org), a phone number with tel: or a page of the app such as #/termine.
4. Under **Style**, choose **Highlighted** or **Subtle**.

Websites open in a new window, pages of the app in the same one.

### Show the upcoming events
1. Insert an **Upcoming events** block.
2. If needed, enter a **Heading** and choose under **How many events** how many appear (1 to 20).

The block always shows the next events from today from the {bereich.events}, with date, time and location, and below them **All events**. You never have to adjust it. Those who may not see the {bereich.events} see “Nothing coming up at the moment.” there. If the {bereich.events} are switched off, the block is missing.

### Show contacts
1. Insert a **Contacts** block.
2. If needed, enter a **Heading** and, under **Offices**, tick the offices whose holders should appear (at most 20). They appear in the order in which you tick them.

The block shows who currently holds the office, with a photo as in the offices. Above each person the office appears in the singular, if one has been entered. Email address and mobile number are only seen by people allowed to see personal details, and only for holders whose account the offices show. If an office is vacant, it says “currently vacant”. If the offices are switched off, the block is missing.

### Insert a notice box or a divider
- **Notice box**: optionally enter a **Heading** and the **Note** (at most 1000 characters) and choose **Info**, **Warning** or **Success** under **Type**. The box is highlighted in colour.
- **Divider**: under **Type of divider**, choose **Line** or **Space only**.

### Enter a schedule or figures
- **Schedule**: per row, enter a **Time** (free text, e.g. “14:00” or “from 8 pm”, optional) and the **Programme item**. **+ Programme item** adds a row, **×** takes one out (at most 50).
- **Facts and figures**: per row, enter a **Value** (e.g. “1848”) and a **Name** (e.g. “founded”). **+ Figure** adds one (at most 12). The figures are shown large side by side.

### Show a poll or an album
- **Poll**: under **Poll**, choose one from the {bereich.polls}. The block shows the title, until when it runs, and **Take part**; for those who have already voted or once it has ended, it says **View answer and result**. Voting takes place in the {bereich.polls}. Those who may not take part in polls (e.g. without logging in) don’t see the block.
- **Album**: under **Album**, choose one from the {bereich.gallery} and under **How many photos** 1 to 12 thumbnails. A tap opens the album. Everyone only sees pictures they may also see in the {bereich.gallery}; internal pictures only for members with the permission. If nothing is visible, the block is missing.
- If the {bereich.polls} or the {bereich.gallery} are switched off, the block is missing for everyone except those who manage pages.

### Show a document or the sponsors
- **Document**: under **Document**, choose a file from the {bereich.documents}. The block shows title, type, size and date with **Open** (PDF and images) and **Download**. It only appears for those who may see the document in the {bereich.documents}; so usually not without logging in. Documents “only for maintenance” are only seen by those who manage documents.
- **{bereich.sponsors}**: optionally enter a **Heading**. The block shows all {bereich.sponsors} in their order with logo and link, always up to date. If there are none yet or readers may not see the {bereich.sponsors}, the block is missing.

### Insert a map for directions
Optionally enter a **Heading** and the **Place or address** (e.g. “Am Markt 1, 12345 Musterstadt”). The block shows the place with the **Map** button. As for an event’s location, it opens the place in the device’s maps app: on Android with a choice of maps app, on iPhone and Mac in Apple Maps, otherwise in Google Maps. No map from another provider is embedded, so the page loads nothing from third parties.

### Change the order
Hold an entry by the handle on the left, drag it to the place you want and let go. The order is saved straight away. On a computer it also works with the keyboard: select the handle with the Tab key, then arrow up or down. The order applies in the standard navigation; with your own navigation, that decides the place of each page.

### Write a page in English
If English is switched on in the app, an admin translates the pages under **Settings → Languages** in the **Translations** section, in the **{bereich.pages}** group. This page shows a note with a link to it.
1. Expand the **{bereich.pages}** group. Each page shows how many texts are already translated, e.g. **3 of 4 translated**.
2. Tap **Translate** next to the page.
3. Enter the English title and the texts of the blocks. Empty fields show the German text in grey; for the text block, **German text** expands it.
4. Tap **Save**.

In English you only write texts. Which blocks there are, their order, **For whom?** and settings such as date, link or events you change here in German; they apply to both languages. A block you keep in German also keeps its English version.

### Delete a page
Tap **Delete** and confirm. The page’s pictures are then gone too, and the page disappears from the navigation.

## Good to know
- **Members only** means: the page is seen by those who may read the {bereich.board}, at first {rolle.member} accounts and candidates. Without logging in and for guests the page then doesn’t exist.
- Pictures in the text are seen by those who see the page and the block. If you set a page to **Everyone, even without logging in**, its pictures are public too. Very large pictures are reduced to at most 2048 pixels on the longer edge.
- If you remove a picture from all blocks and save, it is deleted. If you only move it to another block, it is kept. There is no history of earlier versions.
- Your own HTML, CSS or JavaScript is not possible; the app only keeps the formatting of the text field.
- The title has at most 100 characters, a page at most 50 blocks.
- The date and time of a countdown apply in your app’s time zone. The countdown calculates with the device’s clock and keeps counting even without an internet connection.
- If an event is deleted or the {bereich.events} are switched off, a countdown to this event behaves as after the end.
- Those allowed to manage {bereich.pages} see all pages. {rolle.admin} accounts assign the permission under **Settings** → **Roles**; with the section, the role *Manage {bereich.pages}* is created.
- {rolle.admin} accounts decide under **Settings** → **Navigation** where each page goes, at the bottom in the bar or in a submenu. Without your own navigation, the pages come after the sections; those with the permission **Internal** find them under **Internal** if there is no more room at the bottom. With your own navigation, a new page appears at the end of the bar until an {rolle.admin} gives it a place.
- Those using the app in English see the English version. Anything missing there appears in German, with the note *Only available in German* (or *Partly only available in German*) at the top.
- For **Schedule** and **Facts and figures**, the English version only applies as long as it has as many rows as the German one. If you add a row in German, enter the English rows again afterwards.
- The pages including pictures and the English version are part of the backup.
