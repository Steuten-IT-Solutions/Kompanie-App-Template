titel: View the app as a role
---
With the preview you check what a role or a particular person sees in the app: which tabs, sections, buttons and content. You don’t need another account for this.

![The app in the preview as {rolle.member}, with the banner and End at the top](bild:admin-vorschau)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and **Roles**. There you start the preview as a role or without logging in. You start the preview as a person on that person’s page (see below).

## What you see here
- **Preview as …** – view only: the banner at the top of every page while the preview runs. For a person, their roles follow their name, e.g. “Preview as Max Muster (Member, Manage events)”. For the public view it says **Preview without signing in**.
- **End**: in the banner; ends the preview.
- Below it the app as the role sees it.

## Step by step
### View the app as a role
1. On the **Roles** page, tap **View the app as …** (eye) next to the role.
2. The app reloads and starts on this role’s start page. Take a look around.
3. Tap **End** in the banner at the top. You return to the **Roles** page, with all your permissions again.

![The View the app without logging in button on the Roles page](bild:admin-vorschau-starten)

### View the app as a particular person
This way you see what a person sees with all their roles together: base role, additional roles and permissions granted or removed individually.
1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Members**, and tap the person.
2. At the very bottom, expand **Account and role** and tap **View the app as …**.
3. The app reloads and starts on the start page as the person sees it.
4. Tap **End** in the banner at the top. You return to the person’s page, with all your permissions again.

### View the app without logging in
1. At the bottom of the **Roles** page, tap **View the app without logging in**.
2. You see the public view as visitors without an account see it.
3. Tap **End** in the banner.

## Good to know
- **View only:** nothing can be changed in the preview. If you try, the app reports “You can’t change anything in the preview.” The buttons stay visible anyway so that you see what the role is offered. **Log out** still works; it ends the preview and logs you out.
- **As a person: their data.** In the preview as a person, **My area** and other personal places show this person’s data: their {bereich.points}, {bereich.penalties}, {bereich.attendance}, their responses and their account. You don’t see their answers in anonymous polls. Everything else comes with exactly their permissions. You yourself stay logged in.
- **As a role: your own data.** In the preview as a role, **My area** shows your own data, as far as the role has these pages. Everything else comes with exactly the role’s permissions.
- **As a role: no additional roles.** The preview as a role shows exactly this one role. How several roles work together is shown by the preview as a person.
- {rolle.admin} itself can’t be viewed, nor can a person who is {rolle.admin}.
- If [maintenance mode](seite:admin-wartungsmodus) is on, you see the maintenance page as another role, like everyone except {rolle.admin} accounts. The banner with **End** stays.
- The preview only applies in the current browser tab and survives reloading. In another tab you see the app normally.
- It needs an internet connection. If the app reloads without a connection or the server no longer confirms the preview (e.g. because the role was deleted), it ends by itself. What you load in the preview isn’t stored for offline use; your own stored data stays unchanged.
