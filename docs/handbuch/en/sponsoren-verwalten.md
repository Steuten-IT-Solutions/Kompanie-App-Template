Here you create the {bereich.sponsors} that appear in the app, with name, link and logo, and set their order.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Setup** and the **{bereich.sponsors}** button. **‹ Setup** takes you back.

## What you see here
- All {bereich.sponsors} in the order in which they are shown, with a small preview of the logo, the name and the link (or “no link”).
- On the left the **handle** (six dots) for moving.
- For each entry **Edit** (pencil), **Upload logo** (or **Replace logo**), **Remove logo** and **Delete** (bin).
- At the bottom the **New sponsor** form with **Name**, **Link (optional)** and **Create**.

## Step by step
### Create a sponsor
1. Under **New sponsor**, enter the **Name**, and if you like the **Link** to their website.
2. Tap **Create**. The sponsor then appears at the very bottom.

### Upload a logo
1. Tap **Upload logo** or **Replace logo** next to the sponsor.
2. Choose the file. The logo is saved immediately.

### Change the order
1. Hold an entry by the handle on the left.
2. Drag it to the desired position and let go. The order is saved immediately.

On a computer it also works with the keyboard: select the handle with the Tab key, then arrow up or down.

### Change name or link
1. Tap **Edit** next to the sponsor.
2. Change **Name** or **Link** and tap **Save** (or **Cancel**). If you empty the link, the sponsor no longer has one.

### Delete a sponsor
Tap **Delete** and confirm. Their logo is gone too.

## Good to know
- Logos: PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, at most 2 MB. Pictures are scaled down to at most 512 pixels on the longer edge, never enlarged. A logo with a transparent background works best; it is shown on a light tile.
- For the **Link**, the address without `https://` is enough, e.g. `example.com`; the app adds `https://` when you leave the field. It doesn’t accept insecure addresses with `http://`.
- The name has at most 100 characters. It also serves as the logo’s description for screen readers.
- Who sees the {bereich.sponsors} is set by the “{bereich.sponsors}” permission in the roles; in new installations everyone sees them, even without logging in.
- The {bereich.sponsors} including logos are part of the backup.
