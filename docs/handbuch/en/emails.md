Here you write emails from the app, e.g. an invitation to a meeting to all {rolle.member} accounts or a message to the board and an address outside {kompanie.name}. You choose recipients as in an email program in **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc**: mail groups such as **All members**, whole roles, individual members or any email addresses. You format the text just as on the {bereich.board}, with your signature and file attachments if you like. Below it you see which emails were sent and whether they arrived.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then the **{bereich.mail}** button. The button only exists once an {rolle.admin} has set up sending emails.

## What you see here
- **‹ Admin**: back to the admin start page (only if you have several tasks there).
- **Write email**: at the top the sender (**From**), below it the fields **To**, **Cc** and **Bcc** (Cc and Bcc after tapping **Add Cc and Bcc**), **Subject**, **Text** with the toolbar, **Attachments** with **Attach file** and the **Send** button.
- **Manage mail groups**: opens the mail groups (see below).
- **Sent emails**: all emails of the last year with subject, who wrote them when and how far sending has got. A tap opens the email.

## Step by step
### Write and send an email
1. Tap the **To** field and type part of a name. The app suggests matching mail groups, roles and members (for everyone type e.g. “All”); tap a suggestion (or choose it with the arrow keys and Enter).
2. Type an address outside the app in full and confirm it with Enter or a comma. You can also paste several addresses at once.
3. For copies, tap **Add Cc and Bcc** and choose there in the same way.
4. Enter a **Subject**.
5. Write the **Text** and format it with the toolbar above: font, colours, lists, links, pictures and tables, as in an email program. You can paste text from Word or Outlook. **Insert signature** adds your signature at the end.
6. If you want to send files along, tap **Attach file** under **Attachments** and choose one or more files. Any type of file is allowed, at most 20 MB per file (less on some servers); **×** takes one out again.
7. Tap **Send**.
8. In the confirmation, check how many recipients get the email in To, Cc and Bcc and which attachments are included, and confirm. The app sends it straight away in the background.

You remove a recipient with the **×** on their chip. The app remembers what you write on this device until you send.

### Set up mail groups
You choose mail groups like a role when writing. Two groups always exist:
- **All members**: everyone whose role has the permission **Belongs to the company**, so also {rolle.admin} accounts and own roles such as a sergeant major, but no candidates.
- **All accounts**: everyone with an account except guests, including candidates.

The app works out who belongs when sending; you never have to maintain these two groups and can only rename them. You create your own groups, e.g. for the board, like this:
1. Below the form, tap **Manage mail groups**.
2. Tap **New mail group** and enter a **Name**.
3. Under **Group members**, choose roles, individual members and any email addresses, just as when writing.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Edit** changes a group, **Delete** (bin) removes it. Sent emails show a deleted group as “Deleted group”.

### Check whether the email arrived
1. Under **Sent emails**, tap the email.
2. You see the chosen recipients, the text, the attachments (for downloading) and the state of sending.
3. Under **Delivery per recipient** it shows for each address whether the email was sent, is still pending or failed, with the reason.

### Set up your signature
If you don’t have a signature yet, tap **No signature yet – set one up now** while writing. The app opens the signature in your account (**My area → Account**). What you have already written is kept: after saving the signature, **‹ Back to the email** brings you back here.

## Good to know
- **View only:** with the permission **{bereich.mail}: see the history of sent emails with delivery log** but without the permission to write, you only see **Sent emails** and open them with recipients, attachments and **Delivery per recipient**. The form for writing and **Manage mail groups** are then missing.
- The sender is the address of {kompanie.name} that an {rolle.admin} entered under **Settings → Email**. Replies go to this address.
- Those in **To** and **Cc** see all other recipients from To and Cc, as in an email program. Those in **Bcc** are visible to nobody. So when writing to a large group, better use **Bcc**.
- A role includes everyone who has it as role or additional role. The {rolle.member} role therefore doesn’t reach {rolle.admin} accounts or sergeants major who have their own role; for an email to everyone use the **All members** group.
- Members who have left (former members) never get an email, even if they were still entered in a mail group. Each address gets the email only once, even if it appears in several roles or fields; then To comes before Cc before Bcc.
- Members without a stored email address get nothing. Their chips are marked, and the confirmation before sending names them.
- You only get the email yourself if you choose yourself (or one of your roles or a group you belong to).
- The app sends the emails one by one and gradually, as set under **Settings → Email**. If it temporarily fails for an address, it tries again later.
- If the email with its pictures and attachments is larger than the mail server accepts, the app says so before sending. Then make pictures or attachments smaller. Many mail servers only accept 10 to 25 MB per email.
- Sent emails stay in the history for a year; then the app deletes them with log and attachments. The history is seen by everyone who may send emails or see the history.
