Your account holds your name, your role, your email address, your mobile number and your address. Here you set up notifications and the appearance, change your password and log out. As a {rolle.member}, you also enter your birthday and your joining date here.

![My account with name, role and the Birthday card](bild:anmelden-und-konto)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom and choose **Account** in the menu at the top. If there is nothing else for you under **My area** (e.g. as a candidate), the account opens directly, without a menu.

![Expanded menu under My area with the Account entry](bild:anmelden-und-konto-menue)

## What you see here
- **Name and role**: your name and your role, e.g. {rolle.member}, and your photo if someone has uploaded one. Anyone allowed to create members can change the photo.
- **Contact**: your email address, mobile number and address, as {kompanie.name} has them.
- **Birthday** (only for {rolle.member} accounts, if {kompanie.name} uses {bereich.birthdays}): your birthday and who sees it.
- **Joining date** (only for {rolle.member} accounts, if {kompanie.name} uses the {bereich.directory} list): when you joined and who sees this on your page in the list.
- **Set up notifications**: what the app notifies you about on this device. Below it, if {kompanie.name} has set up sending emails, the switch for board messages by email (on at first).
- **Signature**: your sign-off for messages on the board, with text and pictures (for {rolle.member} accounts, candidates and everyone who writes on the board). More on the help page **Signature**.
- **Appearance**: **Same as device**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Language** (only if {kompanie.name} has switched on another language besides German): **Deutsch** or **English**. It applies to the app, to emails and to notifications sent to you. You have the same choice with the flag at the top of the header.
- **Install app**: the instructions for getting the app onto your phone. The card is missing if you have opened the app already installed.
- **Change password**: set a new password.
- **Log out**: log out on this device.

## Step by step
### Change your email address, mobile number and address
1. In the **Contact** card, tap **Edit**.
2. Enter the new **Email address** or **Mobile number**. For the mobile number, choose the country on the left (🇩🇪 +49 by default) and type the number as usual, e.g. 0171 1234567. An empty field removes the entry.
3. For the **Address**, choose the **Country** (🇩🇪 Germany by default) and enter the **Street**, **No.**, an **Address line 2** if needed (e.g. c/o), the **Postcode** and the **Town**. If all fields are empty, no address is stored.
4. If you have changed your email address, enter your **Current password** to confirm. You don’t need a password for the mobile number and address.
5. Tap **Save**.

The app puts everything into the right form itself: spaces in the email address are dropped, and the mobile number is stored in a consistent format, e.g. +49 171 1234567. In the address, the house number moves into its own field if you write it into the street (“Hauptstraße 5a”), and the postcode is written as usual in that country (e.g. “1234 AB” in the Netherlands). If something isn’t right, it says so directly below the field, for example if the postcode doesn’t match the country; for a mistyped provider such as “gmial.com”, the app suggests the correct address for you to tap. Only you and people with the **See personal details** permission can see these details.

### Enter your birthday
1. In the **Birthday** card, choose the **Day** and **Month**; the **Year** is optional.
2. Use **Show to other members** and **Show age** to decide what others see.
3. Tap **Save birthday**.

Everything else about the switches is on the help page **{bereich.birthdays}**.

### Enter your joining date
1. In the **Joining date** card, enter the date under **Date or year** (e.g. 12.03.2005) or just the year (e.g. 2005).
2. Under **On my page under “{bereich.directory}”**, choose who sees it on your page: **Don’t show**, **Show to members** or **Show publicly** (anyone with the app’s address, even without logging in).
3. Tap **Save joining date**.

**Remove** deletes the joining date again. The joining date can’t be in the future.

### Choose the appearance
1. In the **Appearance** card, tap **Light** or **Dark**. The app then stays that way, even when your device switches (e.g. to dark mode in the evening).
2. With **Same as device** (the initial setting), the app changes along with your device.

The choice only applies on this device and is kept after you log out. You can also switch quickly at the top with the moon or the sun.

### Change your password
1. In the **Change password** card, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter the new password twice, under **New password (at least 8 characters)** and **Repeat new password**.
3. Tap **Change password**.

![“Change password” card with the three fields](bild:anmelden-und-konto-passwort)

The new password must be at least 8 characters long, differ from the old one and must not begin or end with a space. You stay logged in on this device; on all other devices you have to log in again.

### Download your data
1. In the **My details** card, tap **Download my data**.
2. The browser saves a file `meine-daten-<date>.json` with everything the app has stored about you: account, roles, birthday, photo and your entries (e.g. {bereich.points}, attendance, answers in polls). This is your right of access under the GDPR (Art. 15).

### Log out
1. Tap **Log out** at the very bottom.
2. The app logs you out and deletes the data it has stored on the device.

## Good to know
- Logging out needs an internet connection. Without a network, everything stays as it is.
- After logging out, your account no longer gets notifications on this device.
- Forgotten your password? An {rolle.admin} of {kompanie.name} (or anyone allowed to create members) can give you a new starting password.
- As an {rolle.admin}, you won’t find the app’s settings here but under **Admin**.
