The **{bereich.board}** holds urgent messages, e.g. “Training cancelled today” or “Meeting point changed”, and for {rolle.member} accounts the running {bereich.polls}. The title of new entries appears as a coloured banner at the top of every page of the app; on this page you read the messages in full.

![The {bereich.board} page with current messages and running {bereich.polls}](bild:schwarzes-brett)

## How to open this page
If you have **Internal** at the bottom, tap the **{bereich.board}** button there. Or tap the title of a message in the banner at the top or **Open board**. If you got a notification or an email about a new message, tapping it or **view in the app** also opens this page.

The banner only appears as long as there is a message or a running poll you haven’t answered yet.

## What you see here
- **Current messages**: each message with the name of the person who wrote it, the time (with “(edited)” for a changed message), the label **public** for messages everyone sees, **only …** with the {bereich.companies} for messages only for certain {bereich.companies}, **only for you** for messages only you and a few others get, the title, the full text with pictures, the attachments (📎, a tap downloads the file) and **Visible until** with day and time. If there are none, it says **There are no messages at the moment.**
- **Running {bereich.polls}**: only for {rolle.member} accounts, if {kompanie.name} uses {bereich.polls}. Each poll with its end, the question and the label **open** (not answered yet) or **answered**.
- **Take part** or **View answer**: opens the poll. There you answer or see your answer and the results.
- **Closed {bereich.polls}**: opens the list of all {bereich.polls}, including the closed ones.

## The banner at the top of every page
As long as there are valid entries, a coloured banner is at the top of every page of the app (except this one). It shows the title of the newest message in one line; tapping the title opens the whole message. Who wrote it and when is shown here on the board page. The banner shortens very long titles with “…”. Running {bereich.polls} you haven’t answered yet are also in it, each with the **Take part** button.

![The banner with a message and the +1 more button](bild:schwarzes-brett-banner)

- **+1 more** (or **+2 more** …): opens the remaining entries. **Show less** closes them again.
- **Open board**: opens this page. The button is next to **+1 more**; with only one entry the title leads here.
- **×**: hides the entry on this device.

![The opened banner with a message and a poll](bild:schwarzes-brett-banner-mehr)

## Step by step
### Hide a message
1. In the banner, tap **×** to the right of the message.
2. The message disappears from the banner. It stays visible on this page.

If you have hidden all entries, a narrow row remains at the top, e.g. **{bereich.board} · 2 hidden**, with **Open board**. It disappears once the entries have expired.

![The narrow row when all entries are hidden](bild:schwarzes-brett-ausgeblendet)

## Good to know
- Each message stays until the time the author chose when writing it (usually **24 hours**) and then disappears by itself, even without a connection if the app had already loaded it. Expired messages are no longer here; the board is not an archive. The app always shows times in your device’s time.
- Hiding only applies to the device on which you do it. If a hidden message or poll is changed afterwards, it reappears in the banner so you notice the change.
- Once you have answered a poll, it is no longer in the banner but still on this page.
- Messages can be formatted and contain pictures. Links open in a new window.
- For a new message you get a notification with its title if you have switched on the type **{bereich.board}: urgent messages** for your device (see [Notifications](seite:benachrichtigungen)). The author can also leave out the notification. If a message is only changed, no new notification is sent.
- If {kompanie.name} has set up sending emails, new messages also arrive by email at your stored address. You can switch this off under **My area → Account** with the switch at **Notifications**. Changes don’t send a new email.
- The {bereich.board} is seen by {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar (e.g. candidates). {bereich.polls} are only for {rolle.member} accounts.
- **View only:** with the permission **See {bereich.board} history** but without the permission to post, you see all messages, including those for other {bereich.companies} or people, who last changed them, how far emailing has got, the **History** (clock) of each message and at the bottom **History: expired and deleted messages**. You can’t post, change or delete with it.
- Messages labelled **public** are seen by everyone, including visitors who are not logged in: in the banner and on this page, with attachments and the author’s name. Internal messages are only seen by {rolle.member} accounts and people with the internal calendar. Messages for certain {bereich.companies} are only seen by those of them assigned to one of these {bereich.companies}, messages for individual people only by them; plus always everyone allowed to see all messages (permission **See {bereich.board} history**; those who post on the board always have it). Visitors who are not logged in don’t find the {bereich.board} in the navigation but via the banner; with the type **{bereich.board}: public messages** switched on they get a notification about new public messages.
