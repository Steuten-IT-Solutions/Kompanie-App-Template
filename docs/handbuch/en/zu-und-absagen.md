titel: "Accept or decline events"
---
For every event in the internal calendar you accept or decline directly in the event list. That way {kompanie.name} knows in advance who is coming. You can change your mind until the start. The buttons only exist if an {rolle.admin} has switched on accepting and declining.

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.events}** at the bottom. Below every upcoming event are the **Accept** and **Decline** buttons.

## What you see here
- **Accept** and **Decline**: after your answer the chosen button says **Accepted** (green) or **Declined** (red).
- **Counter**: e.g. “5 accepted · 2 declined”. It shows how many have already answered.
- **Reply by …**: shown if the event has a deadline.
- **Note on your acceptance** or **decline**: appears in the opened event as soon as you have answered.
- **View replies**: only with the permission **See RSVPs** (those who may maintain calendar events always have it). It shows who accepted or declined and who hasn’t replied yet, with the notes.

## Step by step
### Accept or decline
1. Tap **Accept** or **Decline** next to the event.
2. The button now shows **Accepted** or **Declined**, and the counter includes you.

### Add a note
1. Tap the event to open it.
2. Under **Note on your acceptance** or **decline**, write e.g. “coming later”.
3. Tap **Save note**.

### Change your mind or take it back
- Tap the other button to switch from accepting to declining or vice versa.
- Tap **Accepted** or **Declined** again to take back your answer. You then count as “No reply yet” again.

## Good to know
- If your answer doesn’t reach the server straight away (no internet, no response after 20 seconds, server disrupted or in maintenance mode, login expired), the app remembers it (also a note or taking it back) on the device and shows it at once, with **Not sent yet – the app will send it as soon as the connection is back.** below it. It then sends the answer by itself and tells you. This also works if the event has been moved in the meantime.
- If you tapped before the deadline, the answer counts even if it only arrives later (for example because you had no signal at the event). Those allowed to see RSVPs see **sent late** next to it, as in the attendance list. Your phone’s time is what counts.
- If the server no longer accepts a saved answer (e.g. tapped only after the deadline, event deleted), **Not sent** appears at the top of the app with the event and the reason. The note stays until you dismiss it with ✕. Then enter the answer again or let the admins know.
- When you log out, the app asks if anything hasn’t been sent yet.
- If you haven’t answered yet, the app reminds you a few days before the event (with a deadline, the day before it) if you have switched on notifications and an {rolle.admin} hasn’t switched the reminder off. If you declined, you won’t get the reminder the day before this event either.
- You can accept or decline until the event starts. For events without a time this ends at midnight at the start of the day. If the event has a deadline, it is only possible until the end of that day. After that you only see your answer.
- Your name and your note are seen by those with the permission **See RSVPs** (including everyone who maintains calendar events), and in the attendance list by those who record attendance. Everyone else only sees the number of acceptances and declines.
- Declining doesn’t mark you as excused by itself. In the attendance list it is only preselected; it is entered by those who record attendance.
- Those who record attendance can also accept or decline for you, e.g. if you call.
- If an event is moved, your answer is kept. If it is deleted and created again, you have to answer again.
- About three months after the event the app deletes the replies. Whether you were there is recorded in attendance, if {kompanie.name} keeps it.
