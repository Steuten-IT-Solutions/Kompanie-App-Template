titel: "Set up {bereich.birthdays}"
---
Here you set how long beforehand a birthday appears in the {bereich.events} under **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** and whether the others get a notification on the birthday itself.

![The {bereich.birthdays} page in the settings](bild:admin-geburtstage)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Settings** and, under **Events and calendar**, **Calendar**. The **{bereich.birthdays}** section is below the calendar. Older links to this page lead there.

## What you see here
- **Show upcoming {bereich.birthdays}**: how many days beforehand a birthday appears in the **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** card at the top of the {bereich.events}, from **only on the day itself** to **30 days before** (**7 days before** at first).
- **Notify everyone else on a member’s birthday**: switch for the birthday notification.
- **Birthday notification from**: the time for it (7 am at first). It only appears if the switch above is on.

## Step by step
### Change how far ahead
1. Choose the number of days under **Show upcoming {bereich.birthdays}**.
2. The app saves straight away and reports “Saved”. The others see the change as soon as they open the app again.

### Switch the birthday notification on or off
1. Tap **Notify everyone else on a member’s birthday**.
2. If it is on, choose the time under **Birthday notification from**. This also applies straight away.

## Good to know
- The setting applies to everyone who sees {bereich.birthdays}. Anyone whose birthday is today is always in the card.
- The section only exists if the {bereich.birthdays} section is switched on.
- This is how far ahead a birthday appears at the top of the {bereich.events} under **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}**, for everyone who sees {bereich.birthdays}.
