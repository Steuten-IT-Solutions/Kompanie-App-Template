titel: Report as PDF
---
With the report you put the data of several sections for a period into a PDF file, e.g. for the annual general meeting or when a new board takes over.

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Report**.

## What you see here
- **Period**: **From** and **To**, plus the quick choices **This year** (from 1 January until today), **Last year** and **Last 12 months**.
- **Sections**: one tick per section. You only see the sections the company uses and you may see. At first all are ticked.
- **PDF** (arrow pointing down with “PDF”): creates the file and downloads it.

## What the report contains
- **Cover page** with the period, the time it was created and the contents with page numbers.
- **Members** (as of today): name, role and {bereich.ranks}. Those who may see personal details also get email, phone, address and date of birth; the table is then on a landscape page. This list doesn’t depend on the period.
- **{bereich.points}**: the total per member and all entries in the period with date, name and reason. What counts is what counts elsewhere, i.e. with the role’s points factor. Cancelled entries are missing.
- **{bereich.penalties}**: per member the total and how much of it is still open, below it **Total**, and all penalties in the period with amount, reason and status (**open** or **paid on …**). The date of the penalty counts, not that of the payment.
- **Passive members** (as of today): name and the person looking after them, with the permission for personal details also address, phone, email and date of birth.
- **{bereich.attendance}**: per member how often they were present, excused or absent, and the numbers per event.
- **{bereich.events}**: the public events in or running during the period, with date, time and location.
- **{bereich.calendar}**: the internal events if you maintain them in the app (not with Google Calendar or an iCal address). If accepting and declining is switched on, also the number of acceptances and declines per event and per member.
- **{bereich.ranks}**: every change in the period with date, name, type (promotion, demotion, initial rank), before, after and reason.
- **{bereich.titles}**: the titles awarded in the period. A title later taken away from a person is missing, because the app then no longer knows it.
- **{bereich.offices}**: per office the terms that fall within the years of the period, with their holders. Offices count by years, not by days.
- **{bereich.inventory}**: today’s stock and the history in the period (created, changed, moved, deleted) with date, person and reason.

Each section starts on a new page. The table header is on every page; at the bottom are company, period and page number. The font is embedded in the PDF, so letters such as “ł” or “č” are correct too.

## Step by step
1. Choose the period with the quick choices or with **From** and **To**.
2. Under **Sections**, remove the ticks you don’t need.
3. Tap **PDF**. On an iPhone the share menu opens; there you can save or pass on the file.

## Good to know
- The report can contain personal data. Only pass it on to people allowed to see it, and delete it when you no longer need it.
- Who created a report when is recorded under **Admin → Changes** (for everyone who may see changes), with period and sections but without content. If the change log is switched off, it isn’t recorded.
- {bereich.events} and {bereich.calendar} are in the report if you may maintain them and see the admin area anyway. Those who only maintain events don’t get their own admin area for this.
- The period can be at most ten years long.
- There is no report in the **View the app as …** preview.
- The report is created in the language of the app, in English also with the English names.
