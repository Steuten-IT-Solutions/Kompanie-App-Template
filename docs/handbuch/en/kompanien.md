This page shows the **{bereich.companies}** with their logos, in the order {kompanie.name} has chosen. The page is open to everyone, even without logging in.

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.companies}** at the bottom. If the item isn’t in the bar, you’ll find it under **Members’ area** or in another submenu of the navigation.

## What you see here
- One card per company with its **logo**, **name** and below that its short name, year of foundation and number of members (as far as they have been entered). Without a logo, the short name appears instead.
- Companies that have been disbanded are at the bottom and marked **former**.
- From two entries on, a **search field** and the **sorting** appear above the list, as under **{bereich.directory}**.
- Anyone who looks after the {bereich.companies} sees the **Manage {bereich.companies}** button at the top.

## Step by step
### Search for a company
1. Tap the **Search …** field at the top.
2. Type part of the name or short name. Upper and lower case don’t matter.
3. The list now shows only the matches. If nothing matches, it says “Nothing found.”

### Sort the list
1. Under **Sorting**, choose **As arranged** (the order {kompanie.name} has chosen), **Name**, **Founded** or **Members** (only if you can see the number of members).
2. Tap the button under **Order** to reverse the direction, e.g. from **A → Z** to **Z → A** or from **Oldest first** to **Newest first**.
3. With **Founded**, companies without a year of foundation come last. Disbanded companies are always at the bottom.
4. Your device remembers your choice.

### Find out more about a company
1. Tap the card. The company’s page opens.
2. You see the **logo**, the **description**, the **Website** and **Company app** buttons (they open in a new tab) and below them the company’s **Members**, as in the member list with photo, titles and {begriff.rank}.
3. Tap a person to open their page (with their {bereich.offices}). There you swipe to the next person in this company.
4. **‹ All {bereich.companies}** at the top (or “Back” in the browser) takes you back to the list, to the same place and with the same search.

## Good to know
- You only see a company’s members if your app uses the **{bereich.directory}** area and you have the right to see it. Without this area, the companies’ pages don’t show any members either.
- {bereich.offices} belong to people, not to a company. They are listed under **{bereich.offices}**, not on the company’s page.
- Under **{bereich.directory}** you can filter the list by company; a person’s page shows their companies; tapping one opens the company’s page.
- If there are no entries yet, the page says “No {bereich.companies} yet.”
