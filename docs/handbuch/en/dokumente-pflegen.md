On this page you provide {bereich.documents} for the {rolle.member} accounts: you upload files, change title, category and visibility, replace a file with a new version and create categories.

![The Manage {bereich.documents} page with the Upload document form](bild:dokumente-pflegen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** at the bottom, then **{bereich.documents}** and **Manage {bereich.documents}** at the top. Only those who may maintain {bereich.documents} see the button (e.g. with the role “Manage {bereich.documents}”).

## What you see here
- **‹ All {bereich.documents}**: back to the list as the {rolle.member} accounts see it.
- **Upload document**: form for a new document.
- **Documents**: all {bereich.documents}, sorted by category, with **Sorting**. Each shows category, version date, type and size of the file and who can see it, plus **Edit** (pencil), **New version** and **Delete** (bin).
- **Categories**: the **New category** field with the **Add category** button and below it the list of categories with the number of their {bereich.documents}, **Rename** and **Delete**.

## Step by step
### Upload a document
1. Tap the **File** field and choose the file. The app suggests the file name as **Title**.
2. Change the **Title** if needed.
3. Choose the **Category** or **No category**.
4. Under **Version date**, enter the date of the document. Today is preset.
5. Under **Visible to**, choose who sees it: **All members** or **Only those allowed to see restricted {bereich.documents}**.
6. Tap **Upload**.

![The filled-in Upload document form](bild:dokumente-pflegen-hochladen)

The new document’s category is then open, and you see it in the list.

### Change title, category, version date or visibility
1. Open the category and tap **Edit** next to the document.
2. Change the fields.
3. Tap **Save**. **Cancel** leaves everything as it was.

![A document being edited](bild:dokumente-pflegen-bearbeiten)

### Upload a new version
1. Tap **New version** next to the document.
2. Choose the new file. The app saves it immediately; title, category, version date and visibility stay.

The old file is deleted. If the version date no longer fits, change it afterwards with **Edit**.

### Delete a document
1. Tap **Delete** next to the document.
2. Confirm. The document and its file are then gone.

### Create, rename and order categories
1. At the bottom, enter a name under **New category**, e.g. “Minutes”, and tap **Add category**.
2. To rename, tap **Rename** next to the category, enter the new name and confirm.
3. To order, drag a category by the handle on the left (six dots) to the desired position. On a computer this also works with the keyboard: select the handle with the Tab key, then arrow up or down.

![The list of categories with handle, Rename and Delete](bild:dokumente-pflegen-kategorien)

The categories appear in this order for the {rolle.member} accounts too.

### Names in English too
If the admin has switched on English under **Settings → Languages**, an admin enters the English titles of the {bereich.documents} and names of the categories there, in the **Translations** section in the **{bereich.documents}** and **Document categories** groups. This form only shows a note with a link to it. Those using the app in English see these names; without an entry the German one appears. The file itself is the same for all languages. Here you always edit the German names, even if you use the app in English yourself.

## Good to know
- Allowed are PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument and JPG and PNG images, at most 20 MB. If the server only accepts smaller files, the limit is shown below the form.
- The title can be at most 191 characters long. Two categories can’t have the same name.
- **Only those allowed to see restricted {bereich.documents}** is suitable e.g. for treasurer’s reports. That means everyone who maintains {bereich.documents} and everyone with the permission **See restricted {bereich.documents}**. Other {rolle.member} accounts don’t see such {bereich.documents} at all; for the others the list shows **Restricted**.
- If you delete a category, its {bereich.documents} are kept and then appear under **More {bereich.documents}**.
- Anyone who had stored a document on their device gets the new version the next time they open it. The app also removes deleted {bereich.documents} from the {rolle.member} accounts’ devices as soon as they open the list of {bereich.documents} there.
- There is no notification when uploading.
