The admin area is your way into all the tasks you take on in the company: maintaining members, awarding {bereich.points}, entering {bereich.offices} and more. Each task has its own button here.

![Start page of the admin area with its buttons](bild:verwaltung)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** in the bar at the bottom. The section only exists if you have at least one task in the admin area.

## What you see here
You only see the buttons you have the permission for and whose section the company uses. Possible buttons are:
- **Members**: the member list with each member’s page. It opens for those who may see the members’ {bereich.points}, {bereich.penalties}, {bereich.ranks}, {bereich.titles} or {bereich.attendance}, who create members or assign roles.
- **Former members**: members who have left, died or otherwise departed (for those who may manage former members).
- **Passive members**: the internal list of passive members with the people who look after them (for those who may manage passive members).
- **Report**: the data of several sections for a period as a PDF file (for those who may see one of these sections).
- **{bereich.points} +**: award {bereich.points}.
- **{bereich.penalties} +**: enter {bereich.penalties}.
- **Promotions**: see pending promotions (only if the company uses {bereich.ranks} and {bereich.points}).
- **{bereich.attendance}**: attendance lists per event (those who may only view them see the existing lists).
- **{bereich.board}**, **{bereich.polls}** and **{bereich.inventory}**: lead to the own pages of these sections.
- **{bereich.lists}**: lists for printing.
- **{bereich.mail}**: write emails to roles, members and any addresses.
- **Setup**: what you create once and rarely change. Behind it are the buttons
  - **{bereich.catalog}**: maintain templates for awarding {bereich.points},
  - **{bereich.ranks}**: maintain {bereich.ranks} and their thresholds,
  - **{bereich.titles}**: create {bereich.titles} (those who may only view them see the list),
  - **{bereich.offices}**: who held which office when,
  - **{bereich.companies}**: your {bereich.companies} with logo, description and members,
  - **{bereich.sponsors}**: your {bereich.sponsors} with logo and link,
  - **{bereich.pages}**: your own pages such as history or directions,
  - **Website**: the website for visitors with its start page (only in the standalone version).
- **Settings**: only for {rolle.admin} accounts.

## Step by step
### Open a task
1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom.
2. Tap the task’s button.
3. For {bereich.catalog}, {bereich.ranks}, {bereich.titles}, {bereich.offices}, {bereich.companies}, {bereich.sponsors}, {bereich.pages} and the website, tap **Setup** first.
4. **‹ Admin** at the top left takes you back to this start page; from the sections under Setup, **‹ Setup** first takes you to its page.

## Good to know
- If you have only one of {bereich.catalog}, {bereich.ranks}, {bereich.titles}, {bereich.offices}, {bereich.companies}, {bereich.sponsors}, {bereich.pages} and the website, or no other task in the admin area, their buttons are directly on the start page instead of under **Setup**.
- If you have only a single task, there is no start page: **Admin** opens that task straight away.
- Subpages, e.g. a member’s page or a single office, have their own way back (e.g. **‹ All members**).
- Which buttons someone sees depends on their role. Roles are set by the {rolle.admin} accounts.
- The order of the buttons on the start page and under **Setup** is set by the {rolle.admin} accounts under **Settings** → **Navigation**, as a default for everyone. The list above shows the standard order.
- Where the buttons are for you and how wide they are, you decide yourself: [Arrange tiles](seite:kacheln-anordnen).
