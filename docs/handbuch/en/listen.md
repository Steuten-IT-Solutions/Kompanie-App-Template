Here you create lists for printing or as a file for Excel, e.g. signature lists, participant lists or checklists. You see all saved lists and open, copy or delete them.

![The {bereich.lists} overview](bild:listen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then the **{bereich.lists}** button.

## What you see here
- **The lists**: for each list the title, the date (if entered), the number of rows and who last changed it.
- **Open**: opens the list for editing, printing and downloading. Tapping the list itself does the same.
- **Copy**: creates a copy with all columns and rows. Its title ends with “(copy)”.
- **Delete** (bin): deletes the list after confirmation.
- **New list**: at the bottom, for creating a new list.

## Step by step
### Create a new list
1. Under **New list**, enter a **Title**.
2. Tap **Create**.
3. The new list opens. How to continue is described under [Edit a list](seite:liste-bearbeiten).

![A new list with title](bild:listen-anlegen)

### Reuse a list from last time
1. Tap **Copy** next to the old list.
2. Open the copy and change title and date.

## Good to know
- **View only:** with the permission **See and print {bereich.lists} and download them as CSV** but without the permission to create and edit, you open the lists, print them and download them as PDF or CSV. **Copy**, **Delete** and **New list** are then missing.
- A new list has the columns “Name” (surname), “First name” and a wide empty column “Signature”. It has no rows yet. The column headings and “(copy)” appear in the language of the person who creates the list.
- Everyone allowed to maintain {bereich.lists} sees and edits the same lists. Other {rolle.member} accounts don’t see the {bereich.lists}.
- The lists contain personal data. Destroy printouts after use and delete downloaded files.
