titel: "Review submitted photos and videos"
---
Here you see the photos and videos members have submitted for the {bereich.gallery}, and decide whether they go in.

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.gallery}** at the bottom and **Review submissions** (clipboard with tick) at the top; if you may only view, the button is called **View submissions**. **‹ All albums** takes you back.

## What you see here
- **Pending submissions**, oldest first: the photo or video, its name, the album and who submitted it. You can play a video here with ▶ before you decide.
- **Recently approved**: the most recently approved photos and videos, with the **members only** tick for photos only for {rolle.member} accounts.

## Step by step
### Approve a photo or video
Tap **Approve publicly** (everyone sees it) or **Approve for members only** (only {rolle.member} accounts see it). The photo appears in the album shortly afterwards.

### Decline a photo or video
Tap **Decline** and write briefly why. The server deletes the file immediately; the person sees the reason.

### Make an approved photo members-only or public
Under **Recently approved**, set or remove the **members only** tick. With the tick only {rolle.member} accounts see it, without it everyone does. Anyone who has already viewed or saved it keeps that copy. The same works in the {bereich.gallery}’s large view. Only those who manage the {bereich.gallery} may move and delete.

### Remove an approved photo again
Under **Recently approved**, tap **Remove again** (bin) and confirm. The photo disappears from the {bereich.gallery}.

## Good to know
- **View only:** with the permission **See submissions** but without **Review submissions**, the page is called **Submissions**. You see the pending and the recently approved photos and videos, but without **Approve publicly**, **Approve for members only**, **Decline**, the members-only switch and **Remove again**.
- For new submissions you get a notification, only one per person and batch (adjustable under **My area → Notifications**).
- The person who submitted learns whether their photo or video was approved or declined.
- Those who may both submit and review can approve their own photos too.
- The app never deletes unprocessed submissions by itself.
