Once you are logged in, you see your own details and everything meant only for {rolle.member} accounts, as well as the public content. You get your username and password from {kompanie.name}. If it has switched this on, you can also request access yourself (see [Request access](seite:zugang-beantragen)).

![Login form with username, password and “Stay logged in”](bild:anmelden)

## How to open this page
Tap **Login** at the top right. If the app runs through the website of {kompanie.name} (WordPress), the website’s login page opens instead; log in there.

## What you see here
- **Username**: your username. You can also enter your email address if it is stored with your account.
- **Password**: your password.
- **Stay logged in**: ticked by default. You then stay logged in on this device, even when you close the browser.
- **Log in**: logs you in.
- **Request access**: only if {kompanie.name} has switched this on. See [Request access](seite:zugang-beantragen).

## Step by step
### Log in
1. Tap **Login** at the top right.
2. Enter your **Username** (or your email address) and your **Password**.
3. Leave **Stay logged in** ticked if it is your own device. On someone else’s device, untick it.
4. Tap **Log in**.

The app then reopens, a greeting with your name appears at the top, and **My area** appears at the bottom.

### Open a shared link
If someone has sent you a link to a screen that is only for logged-in users, it says “This page is only visible after signing in. It will then open straight away.”
1. Tap **Log in**.
2. Log in as above.
3. The app then opens the screen from the link.

![Note “This page is only visible after signing in” with the Log in button](bild:anmelden-hinweis)

## Good to know
- With **Stay logged in** you stay logged in on this device for about a year (400 days); if you use the app in the meantime, this extends by itself. Without the tick, the login ends when you close the browser, after two days at the latest. This applies to logging in in the app; with the website’s login page, the website’s rules apply.
- After several wrong attempts the app blocks logging in for a while. It then says “Too many failed attempts. Please try again in 15 minutes.”
- Forgotten your password? An {rolle.admin} of {kompanie.name} (or anyone allowed to create members) can give you a new starting password.
- If you have requested access and it hasn’t been approved yet, the app tells you when you try to log in, e.g. “Your request for access is still waiting for approval. After that, you can log in with these details.”
- If your account is no longer active, the app says “This account is no longer active. Please contact the company.”
- You can log out and change your password under [My account](seite:anmelden-und-konto).
