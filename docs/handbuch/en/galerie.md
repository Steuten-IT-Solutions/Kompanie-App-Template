The **{bereich.gallery}** shows the photo albums of {kompanie.name}, sorted by year with the newest year at the top. From here you open an album and look at the photos.

![Overview of a year’s albums with title and number of photos](bild:galerie)

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.gallery}** at the bottom.

## What you see here
- **Year**: heading above that year’s albums.
- **Album**: a preview image, the title and the number of photos and videos, e.g. “3 photos · 1 video · 1 internal”. “internal” counts the photos and videos that only {rolle.member} accounts can see.

## Step by step
### Open an album
1. Tap the album.
2. The photos appear as small previews. What to do next is explained under [View an album](seite:galerie-album).

## Good to know
- Some photos are meant for {rolle.member} accounts only. You only see them when you are logged in as a {rolle.member}. Without logging in they are simply missing, as are albums that contain only such photos. How to log in: [Log in](seite:anmelden).
- The photos come from a folder that {kompanie.name} looks after; the app syncs it regularly. New albums therefore don’t appear straight away.
- If there are no photos yet, the page says “No photos yet.”
- If an album or a photo is missing, contact the people in charge at {kompanie.name}.
