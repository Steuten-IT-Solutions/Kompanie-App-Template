Here you see your {begriff.rank}, all {bereich.ranks} in order and when your {begriff.rank} changed. If you also use {bereich.points}, the page shows how many you still need for the next {begriff.rank}.

![My {begriff.rank} with badge, progress and the list of all {bereich.ranks}](bild:mein-dienstgrad)

## How to open this page
Tap **My area** at the bottom, then the menu at the top, and choose **{begriff.rank}**. Or tap your badge in the overview.

## What you see here
- **Badge**: large at the top, if the people responsible have added one.
- **My {begriff.rank}**: your {begriff.rank}, otherwise “No {begriff.rank} yet”.
- **Bar** (only with {bereich.points}): how far you are on the way to the next {begriff.rank}, below it “… more {bereich.points} to …”.
- **All {bereich.ranks}**: from lowest to highest, yours is marked (“Your {begriff.rank}”). With {bereich.points} the threshold is on the right, e.g. “from 25”.
- **History**: every change as “Initial rank”, “Promotion” or “Demotion”, with date, by whom and the reason, if one was given. With {bereich.points} it also shows how many you had at that time.

## Good to know
- Your {begriff.rank} is set by the people responsible. It never changes by itself.
- Once you have reached the threshold of the next {begriff.rank}, it says “… reached (since …) – the promotion is still pending.” Promotions are not automatic but made by the people responsible.
- If you are already at the top, it says “This is the highest {begriff.rank}.”
- You don’t see other members’ {bereich.ranks} here.
- Badges you have seen once are shown by the app even without a connection.
- When your {begriff.rank} changes, the app can send you a message, with a picture for a badge if your device shows pictures in notifications. You switch this on or off under **My area** → **Account** → **Set up notifications** with **{begriff.rank}: promotion or change**.
