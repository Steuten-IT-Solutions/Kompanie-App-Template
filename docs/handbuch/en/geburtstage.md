The **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** card shows which {rolle.member} accounts of {kompanie.name} have a birthday soon, the next one first. You enter your own birthday in your account and decide there who sees it.

![“Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}” card at the top of {bereich.events}](bild:geburtstage)

## How to open this page
Tap **{bereich.events}** at the bottom. The **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** card is at the top.

## What you see here
- **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** (card at the top of **{bereich.events}**): everyone who has a birthday today or in the next few days. How many days in advance a birthday appears is set by an {rolle.admin} (7 days at first). Whoever has a birthday today is highlighted with **Today**; for tomorrow it says “Tomorrow”.
- **Entry**: date, name and weekday, with the age released e.g. “turns 45”.

## Step by step
### Enter your birthday
1. Tap **My area** at the bottom, then **Account** in the menu at the top. The **Birthday** card is there.
2. Choose **Day** and **Month**. The **Year** is optional.
3. Leave **Show to other members** switched on if the others should see your birthday. Without the tick nobody sees it, and the others don’t get a notification either.
4. Tick **Show age** if the others should see e.g. “turns 45”. This only works with a year. Your year of birth itself never appears in the {bereich.birthdays}.
5. If {kompanie.name} uses the {bereich.directory} list, choose under **On my page under “{bereich.directory}”** whether your birthday is shown on your page there: **Don’t show**, **Show to members** or **Show publicly** (anyone with the app’s address sees it, even without logging in).
6. Tap **Save birthday**.

![“Birthday” card in My account with day, month, year and the switches](bild:geburtstage-eintragen)

You can change it the same way at any time. **Remove** deletes your birthday completely.

## Good to know
- If nobody has a birthday in the next few days, the **Upcoming {bereich.birthdays}** card is missing. If {kompanie.name} doesn’t use events, **{bereich.events}** only shows this card, then possibly with e.g. “Nobody has a birthday in the next 7 days.”
- At first **Show to other members** is on, **Show age** is off and **Show to members** is chosen for your page under {bereich.directory}.
- With **Show age** and a year, your page under {bereich.directory} shows the full date with your age, otherwise only day and month. This choice only applies to your page; the {bereich.birthdays} and the notification follow **Show to other members**.
- If your birthday is on 29 February, you appear in the card on 28 February in years without a leap day. You can only enter 29 February with a year that is a leap year.
- Birthdays are seen by those whose role has the permission **See other people’s {bereich.birthdays}**, at first the {rolle.member} accounts. Only {rolle.member} accounts appear there: guests and people who don’t belong to the company (e.g. candidates) don’t appear anywhere.
- Those allowed to create members can enter or correct the date for you. Only you set the switches.
- On the birthday the other {rolle.member} accounts get a notification such as “Today is Max Muster’s birthday (turning 45)” – the age only if it is released. It arrives from the time an {rolle.admin} sets (usually 7 am); they can also switch it off completely. It is received by those who have switched on the type **{bereich.birthdays}: on the day itself**.
