titel: View an album
---
In an album of the {bereich.gallery} you see all photos as small previews. If you tap a photo, it opens large; there you browse and zoom into details.

![Album with the previews; an internal photo has a padlock](bild:galerie-album)

## How to open this page

Tap **{bereich.gallery}** at the bottom and then an album.

## What you see here

- **‹ All albums**: back to the overview, to the place where you opened the album.
- **Title and year** of the album.
- **Previews**: all photos and videos of the album that you are allowed to see. Photos and videos for {rolle.member} accounts only have a padlock 🔒.
- **Videos** are marked with ▶ in the middle; their length is at the bottom left, e.g. “1:02”.
- **Submit photos and videos to this album** (arrow pointing up) below the title: only if you are allowed to submit photos and submitting is switched on. The album is then already selected in the form.
- **Button with an arrow pointing down** to the right of the title: downloads the whole album as a ZIP; only if {kompanie.name} allows downloading.

## Step by step

### View a photo large

1. Tap a preview. It opens large on a dark background, and after a moment in full resolution.
2. To go to the next or previous photo, swipe sideways or tap **›** or **‹**. On a computer the arrow keys work too. When you swipe, the photo follows your finger, and you already see the next one beside it. If you only drag a little, the photo stays. When a photo is zoomed in, swiping moves the photo instead of browsing.
3. At the bottom you see which photo it is, e.g. “3 / 3”, with “internal” for internal photos. Anyone who reviews submissions or manages the {bereich.gallery} finds **Members only** (padlock) or **Show publicly** (globe) below it for photos uploaded in the app (for all photos when Google Drive is the storage location): this makes the photo internal or public. Anyone who manages the {bereich.gallery} also has **Move** (folder with arrow) and **Delete** (bin) there.
4. Tap **✕** at the top right to close the photo. On a computer the Escape key works too, on a phone also the back button or gesture. The album then shows the place with the photo you last looked at.

![Photo in the large view with arrows for browsing and the ✕ button](bild:galerie-album-bild)

### Send a link to a photo

1. Open the photo or video large.
2. Tap the share icon (three connected dots) at the top right, to the left of the download button or next to **✕**. On a phone the share menu opens, e.g. for WhatsApp or email. On a computer the app copies the link and “Link copied” appears at the bottom; you then paste it into a message.
3. Whoever opens the link sees the album with exactly this photo in the large view. With **✕** or Back, they are in the album.

The recipient only sees a photo for {rolle.member} accounts if they are logged in and allowed to see it. Otherwise a note above the album says that the photo no longer exists or is only for logged-in members, with the **Log in** button; after logging in, the photo opens straight away. The same note appears if the photo has since been deleted or moved to another album.

### Play a video

1. Tap a preview with ▶. The video opens large.
2. Tap ▶ in the video to play it. The bar below lets you skip forwards and backwards, switch the sound on or off and go full screen.
3. To go to the next or previous photo, swipe sideways over the video or tap **›** or **‹**. In the video’s bars (at the top and bottom), swiping controls the video, e.g. for skipping. **✕** closes it.

### Download photos and videos

This only works if {kompanie.name} allows it; otherwise the buttons are missing.

- **One photo or video:** open it large and tap the button with the arrow pointing down at the top right next to **✕**. For a video, the progress is shown below, e.g. “Loading video … 40 %”. On a computer the file ends up in your downloads; on the iPhone the share menu opens, where you tap **Save Image**, **Save Video** or **Save to Files**.
- **The whole album:** tap the round button with the arrow pointing down below the album’s title. It is in the row of buttons right next to the submit button (arrow pointing up). On a computer, when you point at it with the mouse, it shows how many photos and videos will go into the ZIP. While it loads, a ring turns in the button, and below it you see how many files have been loaded, e.g. “Downloading 12 of 40 …”; **Cancel** stops it. At the end the app saves a ZIP file with all photos and videos, named after the album’s title and year.

### Zoom into a photo

1. Spread the photo apart with two fingers (up to four times) or double-tap it (twice).
2. When zoomed in, move the photo with one finger.
3. Double-tap to return to the whole view.

## Good to know

- The app prepares new photos the first time they are opened. Until then a grey placeholder sometimes appears briefly; the app then loads the photo by itself.
- While a photo is still loading or being prepared, a ring turns in it.
- Photos with a padlock 🔒 that you have looked at once are stored on your device: next time they appear immediately, even without internet. The app deletes them again when you log out.
- Videos come straight from Google Drive when played. They start loading as soon as you open them large. On a slow connection, starting still takes a moment.
- If it says “Your browser can’t play this video.”, the video is stored in a format your device doesn’t know. It often works on another device or browser.
- Downloaded photos are JPG files in full resolution; if that can’t be created right now, the app takes the 1600-pixel version. Videos come unchanged, as they are stored in the {bereich.gallery}. If a photo hasn’t been fully prepared yet, it is missing from the ZIP; the app says how many are missing. Videos make a ZIP large quickly: it takes a while and space on the device. ZIPs over 4 GB are possible too; the browser then needs as much free space on the device as the album is large. In a private window the space may be lacking; then download the videos one by one.
- The share icon at the top sends a link to the album; the one in the large view a link to a single photo. The recipient only sees photos for {rolle.member} accounts if they are logged in as a {rolle.member}.
