On a member’s page you open a window with their {bereich.points}. There you award {bereich.points} to this person only and cancel wrong entries.

![The {bereich.points} window on a member’s page](bild:mitglied-punkte)

## How to open this page
1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Members**.
2. Tap the member’s name.
3. Tap the **{bereich.points}** tile. The window opens.

**✕** or the back key closes it again.

## What you see here
- At the top the form as under **{bereich.points} +**, but without choosing members: **Template from the catalogue**, **{bereich.points}**, **Date**, **Reason** and **Award {bereich.points}**.
- **Entries**: all of the member’s {bereich.points} with reason, date, by whom, the value and the **Cancel entry** button. The newest are at the top.

## Step by step
### Award {bereich.points} to this person
1. Choose a template or enter the number of points and reason yourself.
2. Tap **Award {bereich.points}**. The entry then appears under **Entries**, and the form is empty again.

### Cancel an entry
1. Tap **Cancel entry** next to the entry.
2. Confirm “Really cancel the entry …?”.

## Good to know
- A cancelled entry no longer counts and disappears from the lists. Who created it and who cancelled it remains stored.
- If the member’s role has a {bereich.points} factor, **Apply the role’s {bereich.points} factor** appears; under **Entries** the value that counts is shown.
- For former members (the admin area’s **Former members** list, only for those who may create members) and for people who are no longer members, the form is missing. You can still view and cancel the entries there.
- **View only:** with the permission **See {bereich.points} of all members** but without **Award {bereich.points}**, the window only shows the entries, without the form and without **Cancel entry**.
