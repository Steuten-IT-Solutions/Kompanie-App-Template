Here you read a document directly in the app, without downloading it. This works for PDFs and images (JPG, PNG).

![An open PDF with title and version date](bild:dokument-ansehen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** at the bottom and **{bereich.documents}**, expand the category and tap **Open** next to the document.

## What you see here
- **‹ All {bereich.documents}**: takes you back to the list. The document’s category is expanded there.
- **Title** and **Version date**: name and date of the document.
- **Content**: a PDF is shown page by page, one below the other; swipe up to read on. An image is shown at full width.

## Good to know
- The app builds long PDFs bit by bit: pages further down appear as soon as you scroll there.
- The app stores an opened document on your device. After that you can open it without a network too.
- If it says **This document doesn’t exist (any more).**, it has been deleted or you aren’t allowed to see it.
- If it says **No connection, and not saved for offline use yet.**, you need an internet connection to open it the first time.
- To save the file, go back to the list and tap **Download**.
