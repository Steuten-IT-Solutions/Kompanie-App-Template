On a poll’s page you give your answer, change it or withdraw it. You also see here whether the poll is named or anonymous, and the results as soon as you may see them.

![A poll you haven’t answered yet](bild:umfrage-beantworten)

## How to open this page
In the banner at the top or on the {bereich.board}, tap **Take part** next to the poll (or **View answer** if you have already answered). You can also tap it in the list of {bereich.polls} or open the notification about the poll.

## What you see here
- **Question**: below it the type (**One answer**, **Multiple answers allowed** or **Free text**), the end and, if you have already answered, **answered**. Below that is a description, if there is one.
- **Named** or **Anonymous**: a box explaining who sees your answer and when the results become visible.
- **Your answer**: the options to tap or a text field, plus **Send answer** or **Change answer** and **Withdraw**.
- **Results**: the number of participants and for each option a bar with count and percentage; for free text, all answers as a list. For named {bereich.polls} the names are shown too. If you may not see the results yet, it says who sees them when.
- **‹ {bereich.board}** and **All {bereich.polls} ›**: lead to the board and to the list of all {bereich.polls}.

## Step by step
### Answer
1. Read in the box whether the poll is **Named** or **Anonymous**.
2. Tap your answer. With **Multiple answers allowed** you tap all that apply. With **Free text** you write your answer in the **Answer** field.
3. Tap **Send answer**.

![Two answers are selected, below them the Send answer button](bild:umfrage-beantworten-auswahl)

### Change or withdraw your answer
1. Open the poll. Your answer is already selected.
2. Choose a different answer and tap **Change answer**.
3. Or tap **Withdraw** and confirm. Your answer then no longer counts.

![An answered poll with Change answer, Withdraw and the results](bild:umfrage-beantworten-ergebnis)

## Good to know
- **Named**: whoever may see the results also sees who answered what.
- **Anonymous**: your answer is shown to nobody, not even to those who manage the {bereich.polls}. It only counts in the results, without a name. Only you see what you answered.
- When you see the results is set by whoever creates the poll: as soon as you have answered (after the end, all {rolle.member} accounts), only after the end, or not at all (only those who manage {bereich.polls}).
- The percentages refer to everyone who took part. With **Multiple answers allowed** they therefore often add up to more than 100 %.
- Once the poll has ended, at the given end or early, it says **The poll has ended. Answers can no longer be changed.** Answering, changing and withdrawing are then no longer possible.
- A free-text answer can be at most 1000 characters long.
- Once you have answered, the poll disappears from the banner; on the {bereich.board} it is then shown with **answered**.
