In this window you award {bereich.titles} to a member or take them away again.

![The {bereich.titles} window with ticked entries](bild:mitglied-titel)

## How to open this page
1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Members**.
2. Tap the member’s name.
3. Tap the **{bereich.titles}** tile. The window opens.

**✕** or the back key closes it again.

## What you see here
- All {bereich.titles} to tick. Those the member already has are ticked.
- A deactivated title is only listed if the member has it, with the addition “(deactivated – can no longer be awarded once deselected)”.
- **Save titles**: can only be tapped once you have changed something.
- If there are no {bereich.titles} yet, it says “No {bereich.titles} created yet (Admin → {bereich.titles}).”

## Step by step
### Award or take away titles
1. Tick the {bereich.titles} the member should have. Remove the tick to remove a title.
2. Tap **Save titles**.

## Good to know
- Those who have switched on notifications get a message when they are awarded a new title. Not when one is removed.
- Titles linked to an office follow the terms of office and can’t be changed by hand here. Their tick is grey, with **Awarded via the office “…”** below it. If you may manage {bereich.offices}, tapping the office’s name takes you straight there.
- Only members get new {bereich.titles}. Anyone who is no longer a member, e.g. after switching to the {rolle.guest} role, keeps their {bereich.titles}; you can then only remove them. This also applies to former members (the admin area’s **Former members** list, only for those who may create members).
- The **{bereich.titles}** tile shows the member’s titles; the member sees them under **My area** in their overview.
- **View only:** with the permission **See {bereich.titles}** but without **Maintain and award {bereich.titles}**, the window only shows the person’s {bereich.titles}, without ticks for changing them.
