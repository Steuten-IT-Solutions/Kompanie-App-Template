The {bereich.catalog} shows what you get how many {bereich.points} for at {kompanie.name}, e.g. “Work duty +4”. The page is for reference only.

![The {bereich.catalog} with occasions and points](bild:punktekatalog-ansehen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Internal** at the bottom and then the **{bereich.catalog}** button. **‹ Internal** takes you back.

## What you see here
- All entries of the catalogue in the order the people responsible have chosen, each with its name and the points on the right (e.g. “+4”, or “-2” for deductions).
- If there are no entries yet, it says “No entries yet.”

## Good to know
- The {bereich.points} are entered by the people responsible. What you have received is under **My area** → **{bereich.points}**.
- **{bereich.points} factor**: if your role has a factor, e.g. ×0.5, an entry counts correspondingly less (more with a factor above 1).
- If the people responsible change a value, this only applies to future entries. What has already been awarded stays as it is.
- There are also {bereich.points} for occasions that aren’t in the catalogue.
