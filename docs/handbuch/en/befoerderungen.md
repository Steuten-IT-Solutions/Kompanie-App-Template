The promotions overview shows who has enough {bereich.points} for the next {begriff.rank}, how many others still need, and who has fallen below the threshold of their {begriff.rank}. From here you promote with two taps.

![The promotions overview with the sections Below the threshold, Threshold reached and Not reached yet](bild:befoerderungen)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then the **Promotions** button. **‹ Admin** takes you back. The small number in the red circle at the corner of the button shows how many members have currently reached the threshold for their next promotion (section **Threshold reached**). Next to it, a number in a circle in your company’s colour shows how many will soon be there (section **Almost reached**). If there are none, the respective circle is missing.

## What you see here
- **Below the threshold** (at the very top, marked red): who has fewer {bereich.points} than their current {begriff.rank} requires. These are candidates for demotion. If nobody is below, the section is missing.
- **Threshold reached**: who has reached the threshold of the next {begriff.rank} with a threshold, with the date since when. Whoever has waited longest is at the top.
- **Almost reached**: who needs at most one more point to reach the threshold of the next {begriff.rank}. This is fixed.
- **Not reached yet**: everyone else who can still rise, with “… more to …”. Whoever needs least is at the top.
- For each name: current {begriff.rank} with badge (or “No {begriff.rank}”), the total of {bereich.points} and the next {begriff.rank}, also with badge.
- If nobody is in the list, “Nobody found.” appears.

## Step by step
### Promote a member
1. Under **Threshold reached**, tap the name.
2. The member’s **{begriff.rank}** window opens; under **New {begriff.rank}** the next {begriff.rank} is already chosen.
3. If needed, choose an earlier day under **Date** (today is preset), enter a **Reason** and tap **Change {begriff.rank}**.

### Find candidates for demotion
1. Look at the **Below the threshold** section at the very top.
2. Tap a name to change the {begriff.rank}.

## Good to know
- The page only exists if you use {bereich.ranks} and {bereich.points}.
- Only members are listed. Anyone who already has the highest {begriff.rank} is only listed if they are below their threshold.
- Promotion is never automatic. The list only shows who is ready.
- As the next {begriff.rank}, the overview takes the next higher one with a threshold; it skips {bereich.ranks} without a threshold in between.
- **View only:** with the permission **See promotions** but without **Maintain {bereich.ranks}, promote and demote**, you see the overview and, on the member’s page, their {begriff.rank} with history, but you can’t promote.
