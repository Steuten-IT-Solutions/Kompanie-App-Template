When someone reports a problem with **Report a bug**, the report lands here first. Often you can help yourselves, e.g. when someone lacks a permission or a setting is missing. Then you close the report with a reason. If it is a bug in the app, you forward the report to the app’s maintainers (Steuten IT Solutions). Only then does it go there.

## How to open this page

Tap **Admin** at the bottom and then **Bug reports**. If reports are open, the small number in the red circle on the corner of the button shows how many; if none are open, it isn’t there. When a new report comes in, you get a notification if you have turned notifications on.

## What you see here

At the top are the open reports, below them under **Done** the closed and forwarded ones of the last 30 days. One card per report with:

- the person’s **name** or **Not logged in**, the time and the role when reporting,
- the **description** and, if filled in, what the person **expected**,
- **More details**: page in the app, app version, device and browser, the app’s last error message and whether the name goes along when forwarding,
- **Show screenshot**, if one is attached,
- **Close …** and **Forward to the maintainers**.

## Step by step

### Close a report

1. Tap **Close …**.
2. Write a **Reason**, e.g. what you changed or how the person can carry on. The reason is required.
3. Tap **Close**. With **Cancel** the report stays open.

If the person was logged in, they see the reason under **?** → **Report a bug** in **Your reports** and get a notification.

### Forward a report

1. Tap **Forward to the maintainers** and confirm the question.
2. The app sends the report to the maintainers in the background. There it becomes an entry to work on.

The person’s name only goes along if they chose **Include my name when forwarding to the maintainers** when reporting. If they were logged in, they see that the report has been forwarded.

## Good to know
- **View only:** With the permission **See bug reports** but without **Handle bug reports** you read the reports and see the screenshot, but without **Close …** and **Forward to the maintainers**.

- {rolle.admin} accounts and everyone whose role has the permission **Handle bug reports** may handle bug reports.
- A screenshot is deleted from the list when the report is closed or forwarded.
- Completed reports are deleted after 30 days. Open ones stay until you deal with them. When 100 reports are open, the app accepts no new ones.
- If the **Forward to the maintainers** button is missing, no recipient is set up in this app. You can then only read and close reports.
- A reply from the maintainers does not come through the app.
