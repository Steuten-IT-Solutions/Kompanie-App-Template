Here you create the {bereich.titles} members can hold, e.g. honorary member or king of the marksmen, and set their order. Who has which title you change on the member’s page.

![The list of {bereich.titles} with handle for moving and the buttons](bild:titel-verwalten)

## How to open this page
Tap **Admin** at the bottom, then **Setup** and the **{bereich.titles}** button. **‹ Setup** takes you back.

## What you see here
- All {bereich.titles} in the order in which they are shown everywhere, each with “assigned …×” or “not assigned”. Deactivated ones carry the label “inactive”.
- On the left the **handle** (six dots) for moving.
- For each title **Rename**, **Deactivate** (or **Activate**) or **Delete** (bin).
- At the bottom the **New title** form with **Name** and **Create**.

## Step by step
### Create a title
1. Under **New title**, enter the **Name**.
2. Tap **Create**. The title then appears at the very bottom.

### Change the order
1. Hold a title by the handle on the left.
2. Drag it to the desired position and let go. The order is saved immediately.

On a computer it also works with the keyboard: select the handle with the Tab key, then arrow up or down.

### Rename a title
1. Tap **Rename** next to the title.
2. Change the **Name** and tap **Save** (or **Cancel**).

### Delete or retire a title
- If nobody has the title, tap **Delete** and confirm.
- If someone has the title, only **Deactivate** is possible: it is no longer awarded, and those who have it keep it. **Activate** undoes this.

## Good to know
- {bereich.titles} are independent of {bereich.ranks} and {bereich.points}. A member can have several.
- The name has at most 60 characters, and each exists only once.
- A title can be linked to an office (section {bereich.offices}). Then whoever currently holds the office gets it and loses it when the term ends. You set up the link on the office. In the list such titles show **Awarded via the office “…”**; if you may manage {bereich.offices}, tapping the office’s name takes you there. Renaming still works; if you delete the title, the link is dropped.
- **View only:** with the permission **See {bereich.titles}** but without **Maintain and award {bereich.titles}**, you see the list with the number of awards, but without **Rename**, **Deactivate**, **Delete**, handle and **New title**.
